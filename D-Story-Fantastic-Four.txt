D Story: Fantastic Four and Doom

Note: This is my own version of the story and its characters. Marvel and other works are inspirations only. References to canon, Raimi or other sources describe the inspiration and are not fixed facts of this story unless a file marks them as approved.

Status: planning ideas only. Nothing here is set. The only story canon is D-Story-So-Far.txt.

Approved Direction

* The Fantastic Four are based on the 2005 movie
* Victor Von Doom is the son of Latveria's royal family who lives abroad as a businessman
* After he gets his powers and Ultron's destruction hits Latveria, he goes back to rule it
* The Fantastic Four and Doom feud among themselves in the background
* D's Baxter Building deliveries are how the story checks in on them and Doom

Victor Von Doom

* Latverian-born billionaire who runs Von Doom Industries out of New York
* Son of Latveria's royal family. He lives abroad as a businessman and does not rule Latveria at the start
* Someone else rules Latveria until Ultron's destruction
* After he gets his powers and Ultron destroys Latveria, he goes back to rule it
* Proud, image-obsessed and used to getting his way, as in the 2005 movie
* Was a classmate and rival of Reed Richards
* Dates Sue Storm and wants to marry her
* Funds Reed's space station mission when Reed runs out of money and takes most of the credit and the profit
* Played detail (Popular Science, 1999): Von Doom Industries is buying American aerospace firms. In the newspaper and magazine world he is known as a businessman

The Four (2005 Movie Inspiration)

* Reed Richards: brilliant scientist who is nearly broke and has the cosmic storm theory. Becomes Mister Fantastic with an elastic body
* Sue Storm: Reed's ex-girlfriend who works for Victor as his director of genetic research. Becomes the Invisible Woman with invisibility and force fields
* Johnny Storm: Sue's younger brother, a hotshot pilot. Becomes the Human Torch with fire and flight
* Ben Grimm: Reed's best friend and an astronaut. Becomes the Thing with rock skin and great strength. Loses his fiancee Debbie, who leaves him after the change

Origin (2005 Movie Inspiration)

* Reed's theory says a cosmic storm drove human evolution
* Victor funds a trip to his orbital station to study the storm in exchange for most of the profits
* The storm arrives early. Reed, Sue, Johnny and Ben are exposed. Victor is hit too
* Back on Earth their DNA changes and the powers come out
* The four save people in a pileup on the Brooklyn Bridge and the press names them the Fantastic Four
* Victor's company collapses after the failed mission. His board pushes him out and his body slowly turns to metal and gains electrical powers
* Victor blames Reed, turns Ben against the others and is beaten in a fight in the city. In the movie his body is shipped to Latveria at the end

Changes for This Story

* Victor starts as a royal son abroad, not the ruler of Latveria
* The 2005 ending where his body is shipped to Latveria is not used as the way he takes power. He goes back to rule only after Ultron's destruction of Latveria
* Doom stays a recurring rival rather than a world-ending threat
* The Fantastic Four are public heroes from the start
* They live and work in the Baxter Building in Manhattan

Timing (to be decided)

* The orbital station and the cosmic storm come after the story's 1999 start. Victor is a businessman in the 1999 newspapers and magazines
* One option is to place the storm around 2005, like the movie, while D is in college and starting his courier business
* Baxter Building runs then keep D in touch with the Four and Doom from his courier years onward

Story Uses

* D delivers the strange parts the Fantastic Four order
* Doom's companies are customers too, since D is a neutral courier
* Arcs can open with a news clip of the Four's latest fight with Doom
* The feud stays in the background unless D steps into it

Latveria Replaces Sokovia

* Sokovia does not exist in this story. Latveria takes its place in every MCU event
* 2015: Hydra's Baron Strucker holds Loki's scepter at a base in Latveria and experiments on volunteers there
* Wanda and Pietro Maximoff are Latverian
* Ultron lifts a Latverian city into the sky and drops it. Pietro dies and the city is destroyed
* Victor Von Doom, by then powered, goes back afterward and rules Latveria
* 2016: the Lagos incident leads to the Latveria Accords
* Helmut Zemo is a Latverian colonel whose family died in the Ultron attack
* Open: how Victor's rule fits with the Accords and with Zemo
