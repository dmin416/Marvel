# Goose the Flerken: Year-by-Year Location (MCU)

Goose appears on screen only in Captain Marvel (1995). Years without on-screen appearances are marked as unconfirmed.

---

## Before 1989
**Location:** Earth, Project PEGASUS (California)
- Brought to Earth by Mar-Vell, who lived undercover as Dr. Wendy Lawson
- Lives as Lawson's pet while passing as an ordinary orange tabby
- Origin world and arrival date never stated on screen

## 1989
**Location:** Project PEGASUS
- Mar-Vell is killed during the light-speed engine test flight
- Carol Danvers is taken by the Kree
- Goose stays behind at PEGASUS

## 1990 to 1994
**Location:** Project PEGASUS
- Goose remains at the facility on her own
- No on-screen events

## 1995 (Captain Marvel)
Goose moves through several locations in a matter of days:
1. **Project PEGASUS:** Carol and Nick Fury find her while searching for Lawson's records; Fury takes a liking to her
2. **Quadjet:** Sneaks aboard the plane used to escape the base
3. **Louisiana:** Maria Rambeau's home
4. **Mar-Vell's laboratory (cloaked, Earth orbit):** Talos identifies her as a Flerken; she swallows the Tesseract and eats Kree soldiers
5. **Escape from the lab:** Scratches Fury's left eye, causing his permanent injury
6. **Earth, Fury's S.H.I.E.L.D. office:** Coughs up the Tesseract onto Fury's desk (mid-credits)

**End of year:** Carol leaves Earth with the Skrulls. Goose stays with Fury.

## 1996 onward
**Location:** Unconfirmed
- No further on-screen appearances
- Last confirmed with Fury on Earth in 1995
- Status during the Snap (2018) and the Blip (2023) is never addressed
