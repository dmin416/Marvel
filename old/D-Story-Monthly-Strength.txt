D Story: Monthly Strength

Note: This is my own version of the story and its characters. Marvel and other works are inspirations only. References to canon, Raimi or other sources describe the inspiration and are not fixed facts of this story unless a file marks them as approved.

Status: design model. This file is the source for D's body strength at any month of his life from the story start to 36.

How to Use

* Base lift is the weight D can lift any time without effort, in kilograms, from the floor with his own body
* Max effort is everything he has on purpose: base lift / 0.6
* Emergency is the mother-lifting-a-car-off-her-child lift: a short heave of a few centimeters from his strongest position with adrenaline on top. Emergency = max effort x 1.4 x adrenaline (1.30 at 14, easing to 1.15 by 22)
* Lifting one corner or end of a 1,300 to 1,600 kg car to free someone takes roughly 300 to 450 kg
* An emergency lift is a burst of a few seconds. It can tear muscle or tendon and leaves him wrecked afterward
* Find the month and read the line

Key Dates

* Birthday: December 30 (born 1984)
* The story starts on Sunday, September 12, 1999 at 14 years 8 months (D-Story-So-Far.txt). The first row covers September 1999
* December 30, 1999 (his 15th birthday): symbiote contact. From then on it maximizes his nutrition, recovery and gains to the highest level a human body can reach

Training

* Every phase: about 3 hours a day, 7 days a week (21 hours a week)
* Before the symbiote (September to December 1999): bodyweight work in the yard (pull-ups, dips, push-ups, sit-ups and laps), as in the played story
* 15 to 17: gymnastics strength and calisthenics: rings, bar and handstand work, planche and front lever progressions, weighted pull-ups and dips, pistol squats, Nordic curls, sprints and jumps
* 18 on: gymnastics strength and weighted calisthenics plus heavy sandbag and stone lifting for the floor lift, sprints and jumps
* 22 on: the same, plus courier work

Food

* High in protein with enough carbohydrates and oils
* Minerals run a bit low but are remedied
* From his 15th birthday the symbiote maximizes his nutrition and recovery on top of that

Formula

* Each month max effort closes part of the gap to his ceiling for that age: gain = rate x (ceiling - current)
* Rate is 0.08 a month before the symbiote (good food and 3 hours a day) and 0.18 a month after it (maximized nutrition, recovery and training)
* Ceiling = 360 kg max effort x maturity x aging. 360 kg at 88 kg body mass is about the best a natural human of that size can pull
* Maturity limits a growing body: 45% at 15, 58% at 16, 70% at 17, 80% at 18, 87% at 19, 92% at 20, 96% at 21, 98% at 22 and 100% from 23
* Aging takes 0.4% a year off after 30
* Body mass climbs from 40 kg at the start to 88 kg by 23
