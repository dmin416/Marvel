Note: This is my own version of the story and its characters. Marvel and other works are inspirations only. References to canon, Raimi or other sources describe the inspiration and are not fixed facts of this story unless a file marks them as approved.

Status: design brainstorm only. Nothing here is approved. Values are design estimates before any venom.

Training Lifestyles

* Peter: weak nerd. Does not train
* Gwen: police captain's daughter. Takes self defense but does not lift weights
* Harry: trains enough to attract women
* D: trains to survive the world from 14. D starts training at 14 and it is already happening in the played story (yard workout). D's lift values below come from D-Story-Monthly-Strength.txt. His sprint, reaction and jump values still model a start at 18 (see D-Story-Strength-Over-Time.txt and D-Story-Speed-and-Skill-Over-Time.txt)

Reference Points

* Untrained adult man lifts about 70 kg and an untrained adult woman about 39 kg
* Deadlift record is 505 kg (2025)
* Usain Bolt's top speed was about 12.4 m/s

Venom Results

* Gwen at a single dose (strength x50, speed x2, reaction time divided by 3, jump x10) on her adult baseline: lift 2,000 kg | sprint 12.0 m/s (43 km/h) | reaction 80 ms | jump 3.0 m. At the age 18 bite her 38 kg baseline gives about 1,900 kg
* D at 300% efficacy (strength x148, speed x4, reaction time divided by 7, jump x28): lift 51,800 kg | sprint 30.0 m/s (108 km/h) | reaction 31 ms | jump 14.0 m
* D lifts 25.9 times what Gwen lifts
