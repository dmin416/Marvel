Sexual Kinks of Superhumans

Scope: fictional superhuman rules for adult characters only (18 or older). They are not descriptions of ordinary human physiology.

Durability and physical contact

Superhumans are durable. Ordinary physical activity and intimate contact do not meaningfully damage their tissues when their durability applies. They can explore what they mutually want with someone they love without ordinary human fragility imposing the same limits.

Lifting a superhuman by any part of their body can feel like stretching, pressure or pleasure without causing damage.

Piercings do not follow ordinary human injury rules. A piercing can be pulled, twisted or used to lift the pierced superhuman's body without permanently damaging tissue or nerves.

Extreme pressure, powerful grips and compression can create sensations of pressure or stretching rather than injury. Superhuman flexibility allows unusual stretching, bending and positioning without damaging joints, muscles or connective tissue.

Pleasure and stimulation

Superhuman physiology can allow pleasure beyond normal physiological limits. Pleasure can scale upward indefinitely rather than reaching an overstimulation ceiling. Repeated pleasure does not inherently diminish through habituation.

Hypersensitivity after a climax remains. Instead of imposing a limit on continued stimulation, the increased sensitivity can make further stimulation even more pleasurable.

Continued stimulation and repeated climaxes are possible. Their occurrence, timing and intensity depend on the narrative rather than a fixed biological rule.

Narrative control

Endurance, stamina and refractory periods are narrative properties. A superhuman has exceptional endurance or needs recovery when the story specifies it, not as an automatic universal rule.

Fainting can occur after several exceptionally intense climaxes if the narrative calls for it. When it occurs, the loss of consciousness is harmless and does not cause lasting injury. Recovery can be rapid when specified.

Enhanced sensation is not a mandatory constant. Sensitivity and its intensity are determined by the narrative.

Altered time perception may change how long an experience subjectively feels when the narrative calls for it. Superhuman bodies may also adapt automatically to unusual physical conditions without injury. Neither ability needs special emphasis unless relevant to the scene.
