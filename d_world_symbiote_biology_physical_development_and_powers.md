## Integrated development rules

2. **Ordinary training matters** and began in September 1999.
3. **Nutrition and sleep are inputs, not free energy.**
4. **Distinguish performance modes:** optimized baseline; active symbiote assistance; copied powers (only after the post-bite unlock, from a real touch, plus practice); equipment-dependent feats.
5. **Specialization stays real:** sprinting, marathon efficiency, maximal strength, gymnastics and breath-holding need different technique.

7. **High-risk references are not training prescriptions.** Survival anecdotes are not portable powers.

## Development mechanics and biological limits (planning, not played)

The one conditional year-by-year path and its acquisition rules are maintained in **`d_powers_timeline.md`**, not a separate development file. Use four distinct layers: **current output** (available control and condition), **current capacity** (physiology/resources today), **native potential** (reach of integrated symbiote mechanisms), and **acquired potential** (new mechanisms from later sources). Control can be discovered rapidly; capacity also changes through physical adaptation, nutrition and remodeling. No training period creates a new power where the capability is absent.

**Shared control families:** (A) surface pigment/texture/disguise; (B) filament extrusion/cords/distributed covering/thin suit; (C) reinforcement and contraction, with an additional control needed for extruded-material pulling; (D) hardening into claws/shields/armor; (E) adhesion, **unverified** in D; (F) internal regulation and directed recovery; (G) sensing, with light, chemical and electrical perception **not yet established**; (H) genetic manipulation, which unlocks only after Gwen's spider bite in the planned late-2002 sequence. Mastering one family often speeds related variants, not unrelated phenomena.

**Material and energy:** nutrition, water and minerals remain real inputs. A thin, film-inspired full covering may redistribute D's integrated symbiote tissue without a separate armor-scale reserve; thick armor and large structures require material or an explicitly defined transformation mechanism. No fixed tissue growth rate, reserve-storage density, or strength tonnage has been established. Expression may be immediate when tissues/resources exist, while structural remodeling has its own time requirements.

**Unverified versus demonstrated:** ordinary optimization and recovery have been established through March 31, 2000; high-grade regeneration, host preservation under major trauma, exceptional toxin/disease resistance, wall adhesion, environmental shielding and specialized senses are catalog possibilities, **not tested story accomplishments**. Resonant-sound effects on D's wholly integrated body are a working hypothesis, not a demonstrated weakness; exact severity and mitigation are undecided.

# Part B: Personal image disruption (advanced concept)

**Type:** fictional anti-surveillance ability. **Status:** unmastered concept, not demonstrated.

The person looks normal to the naked eye but photographs and video show their **face, body and clothing** as a shifting, unrecognizable mass of blur, streaks and artifacts. The surrounding scene records normally.

## Mechanism

Thousands of microscopic **disruption points** across the body generate an adaptive, body-hugging electromagnetic/photonic field that interferes with the frequencies and image-forming processes of an observing device. Overlapping artifacts spoil the whole figure, including carried items.

**Visual analogy:** like the streaks metal causes in a CT scan, applied fictionally to cameras.

## Effects and limits

- **Human vision:** unaffected.
- **Surveillance:** analog CCTV, digital cameras and film record presence but not a reliable likeness. Requires fictional sensor-selective physics.
- **Footage:** corruption is in the original recording.
- **Not invisibility or camera shutdown:** silhouette, location, movement and actions may remain detectable.
- **Not complete anonymity:** witnesses and physical evidence still identify.

**1999 New York implication:** security tapes capture a moving but unidentifiable figure.

## Electrical transformation, speed and capacity (future concept; unplayed)

- **Lightning is not one speed:** an atmospheric stepped leader travels roughly **90 km/s**; its return-stroke disturbance can propagate around **90,000 km/s**. Neither makes a transformed person's mass travel at that speed. In vacuum, ordinary atmospheric lightning has no air channel; electromagnetic radiation travels at **299,792 km/s**, but transporting and re-forming D requires new fictional mechanisms.
- **Two proposed electrical transit speeds (after acquisition and mastery):** **Leader mode ~100 km/s**, initial travel through a newly forming conductive/plasma path; **return-stroke mode ~90,000 km/s (~30% light speed)**, rapid transit *along an established path*. The second is a fictional transport ability: real return strokes propagate electrical disturbances, not whole bodies. Neither mode is a demonstrated D feat. Vacuum travel separately needs propulsion, electromagnetic cohesion, shielding and heat rejection.
- **Energy model:** **2 TJ** safe full-body storage across **70 L** (≈**28.6 GJ/L**) is an *optional fictional containment assumption*, not a result of spider or symbiote biology. At **3 GW** discharge, 2 TJ lasts **667 s ≈ 11.1 min** only if D can safely sustain that output. Storage, generation, discharge rate and damage tolerance are independent.
- **Mass and impact:** 70 kg moving at **100 km/s** carries **350 GJ** of kinetic energy; a 2 kg solid fist at that speed carries **10 GJ**. Transforming back into solid matter and controlling momentum/recoil are separate powers.
- **Synergy, not automatic multiplication:** integrated symbiote biology may develop adaptive repair, insulation, conduction routes and field regulation; *future* spider-derived abilities may improve biomechanics and impact resilience; Electro-derived abilities could provide electrical conversion and absorption. None alone proves a large electrical reserve or relativistic speed. At **2 TJ**, even **0.001%** deposited as internal heat is **20 MJ**, enough to dangerously overheat ordinary tissue; containment and safe energy export matter more than regeneration.

**Speedster and reflex comparison (reference only):** Makkari is supersonic (exact maximum unstated); Pietro's ~Mach 1–2 is an estimate; Electro travels through conductors and through air but has no reliable numerical maximum. Fast electrical signaling is not the same as solid-body striking or fast thinking: a 2 m signal at light speed takes ~6.7 ns, while proposed **10 μs** conscious reactions also demand extraordinary sensing and processing. Electrical brain/nerves could hypothetically support faster processing and storage together, but physical limbs remain muscle-limited unless separately enhanced.

**Illustrative containment scaling:** an Electro-equivalent 70 L form storing **20 GJ routinely**, with **100 GJ overload**, and D possessing **100× the energy containment per transformed liter** yields D's proposed **2 TJ** routine reserve and **10 TJ** overload threshold. Those starting figures and the multiplier are *invented*, not movie measurements; 5 mL → ~143 MJ; 1.5 L → ~42.9 GJ; 7 L → 200 GJ; 70 L → 2 TJ. Overlapping volumes must not be double-counted. Increased toughness does not itself increase electromagnetic containment.

**Physics and Stark comparison:** energy-form travel carries little conventional impact momentum unless D restores and accelerates actual mass. **1 GJ** in light has only **3.34 kg·m/s** photon momentum; recreating a **2 kg** fist *from energy alone* requires ~**1.8×10¹⁷ J** mass-energy (absent a fictional restoration mechanism). Tony's first cave reactor is quoted at **3 GW for 15 min** (2.7 TJ *delivered if sustained at that output*, not 2.7 TJ stored); the second 2008 reactor improves power and duration by unspecified amounts. D's 2 TJ reserve is not a claim that he can generate 3 GW.

**Biological hypothetical milestones (P):** actuator input (human-interface limited) → **1 kb/s** encoded commands (~125 bytes/s) → **1 Mb/s** (~125 kB/s) → **100 Mb/s** (~12.5 MB/s) → **1 Gb/s** (~125 MB/s). These are *optional engineering targets*, **not measured symbiote speeds or guaranteed development dates**. D must also build an encoder, decoder and safe output path. A future copied electrical ability might help signaling but does not itself furnish the digital protocol.

**Meaningful bottleneck order:** intention/data already formulated → biological signal creation → adapter/protocol → cable or radio → remote machine/network → storage and computation → information returned and interpreted. The **slowest link and the slower of computation/understanding** govern useful work. E.g., a preexisting **10 MB** file takes **at least 80 seconds at 1 Mb/s**, **0.8 seconds at 100 Mb/s** or **0.08 seconds at 1 Gb/s**, ignoring overhead; developing and verifying the file can take much longer.

**Continuity:** no computer port, mind-upload, direct machine reading, wireless symbiote modem, or AI connection is presently canon. Earliest experiments, hardware design, choice to proceed and actual achievable bandwidth remain author decisions. The NovaCorp AI/computing master governs the optional external assistant independently.

## Symbiote hive-mind, copied minds and crystal-computer network — proposed future system (November 5, 2026)

**Classification: user-supplied speculative architecture / optional future deployment, not an April 2004 feat, public product capability or accomplished planetary installation.** D's one-trillion-lifetime archive is established in the powers master, but access to the complete three-dimensional archive remains restricted until the separately planned Tiamut milestone. Early detached symbiote portions remain extensions of D, not new independent people; Peter, Gwen and Lorna keep their own agency. ### Premise and division of labor

D could eventually subdivide living symbiote tissue into connected intelligent blobs inhabiting compatible organic hosts or suitable electronics. Each blob would run a *copy of some functional mental capacity*, retain D's personal identity and values via a continuous shared connection, and write selected experiences into a common memory. The central nervous system would not be used as a compute bottleneck; D could eat, sleep, converse, and live normally while the proposed network worked. **All of this is a proposed mature-state mechanism:** autonomous blob reasoning, consistent global identity, tissue-neuron equivalents, energy accounting, bandwidth, permissions and viable electronics interfaces remain unverified.

- **Symbiote = decision / mind layer:** distributed intent, judgment, conversation, situational and social reasoning; experience synchronization; no independently diverging identities in the intended design. A rat-brain-sized or bee-sized blob is a conceptual sizing unit, not an observed requirement.
- **Hypothetical crystal computers = calculation / muscle layer:** cameras, lidar, microphones and other sensor conversion; physical simulation, prediction, control loops under 10 ms, traffic/logistics coordination, compression and bulk computation. They do not acquire personhood or autonomous judgment merely by calculating.
- **Connection = shared network:** authenticated blob-to-blob queries, selected compressed experience transfer, optional oversight by D. Continuous perfect planetary connectivity, latency-free links, access to all cameras, host consent, security and practical chip fabrication are *not* established.
- **Privacy and human agency:** installation in other people's phones, cars or bodies, universal observation and mind-copying are proposals, **not blanket authorization or completed surveillance**. Governance, individual consent, local controls, data minimization and opt-out are unsettled.

### Consumer-device architecture — processor stays conventional (preferred design, future only)

For a *future approved* symbiote-enabled NovaPhone, the preferred integration is **hybrid**, not a total replacement of every electronic component. A conventional CPU, GPU, caches and some fast RAM continue to run ordinary applications, graphics, radio-related workloads and deterministic real-time tasks. A safely contained symbiote node supplies optional long-term/personal memory, specialized reasoning and assistance, and guarded security operations. If symbiote memory cannot meet silicon RAM's latency and bandwidth requirements, it must not replace that RAM; if storage retention, offline use or recovery cannot be proven, encrypted conventional flash must remain. Conventional sensors, displays, radios, batteries and **independent thermal/charging shutdown** remain required. Larger hypothetical crystal processors may still accelerate high-volume simulations and sensor workloads in other systems.

A node must operate offline and **not** automatically share customers' content with D or the hive; customer data access and synchronization require informed consent and isolation. Prevent propagation into people or other devices and prevent uncontrolled node replication; independent safety controls work during node failure. An apparent processor void under X-ray is a proposed fictional concealment possibility, **not** proof of uncopyable technology, lawful patent protection or a certified safety feature. A real silicon processor remains visible in this preferred design. Whether the node is biologically stable, energy-efficient, electrically coupled at sufficient speed or manufacturable at useful scale remains **unproven**. The complete design tradeoffs and NovaPhone chronology are in `marvel_technology_reference.md`.
