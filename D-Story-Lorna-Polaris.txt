D Story: Lorna Dane (Polaris)

Note: This is my own version of the story and its characters. Marvel and other works are inspirations only. References to canon, Raimi or other sources describe the inspiration and are not fixed facts of this story unless a file marks them as approved.

Status: reference and planning ideas. The comics and adaptation sections describe the source material. The story section holds what the planning files already say plus ideas. The only story canon is D-Story-So-Far.txt. Lorna has not appeared in the played story yet.

Accuracy note: this file is written from memory of the comics and shows. Issue numbers and the order of some retcons should be checked before they are relied on. Items marked (verify) are the least certain.

Quick Facts (Comics, Earth-616)

* Real name: Lorna Sally Dane
* Codename: Polaris
* First appearance: X-Men #49 (October 1968), created by Arnold Drake and Jim Steranko
* Species: mutant
* Hair: naturally green, a visible sign of her mutation. Early on she dyed it brown to hide it
* Father: Magneto (Max Eisenhardt) by later retcon. She was raised believing her father was Kenneth Dane
* Mother: Suzanna Dane
* Education: a master's degree in geophysics
* Long-term partner: Alex Summers (Havok)
* Teams: X-Men, X-Factor (several versions), Starjammers, Brotherhood-adjacent roles in the Genosha and Krakoa eras, X-Men Red
* Home bases over time: Westchester, Arizona, Washington (X-Factor), Genosha, space, Krakoa, Mars (Arakko)

Abilities

Magnetism (core power)

* Senses magnetic fields everywhere, including the Earth's own field. She can navigate by it like a compass
* Moves metal. Ferrous metal is easiest. At higher power she affects any metal and other materials through their electromagnetic properties
* Flight by riding magnetic fields, fast enough for long-distance travel
* Force fields and shields made of magnetic energy that stop bullets, blasts and impacts
* Magnetic blasts that push, crush or knock targets away
* Tears apart, reshapes and rebuilds metal structures: cars, girders, bridges, ships
* Disrupts or destroys electronics and machines by scrambling their fields, much like an EMP
* Manipulates the electromagnetic spectrum to some degree, including light effects. Auroras sometimes appear around her when she uses a lot of power
* Fine control: she can move tiny objects such as a hairpin or a lock's tumblers
* Large-scale feats: shifting tectonic or geomagnetic forces, moving huge masses of metal and affecting a whole region's field
* Iron in blood: some stories show her or Magneto affecting the iron in a living body (verify for Lorna specifically)
* Power level: often written as close to Magneto's. Writers raise and lower it. Whether she counts as an Omega-level mutant has been debated by fans and is not consistently stated (verify)

Powers she has had at other times

* Superhuman strength, growth and a body that fed on negative emotions: after Zaladane stole her magnetic powers in the Savage Land, Lorna gained great strength and size and grew stronger from hate, fear and anger around her. Her magnetism later returned
* Possession by Malice: the psychic entity Malice controlled her for a long stretch and made her lead the Marauders. Malice gave her no new powers but used hers
* Pestilence: Apocalypse made her his Horseman Pestilence. As Pestilence she could spread disease and plague alongside magnetism. This came after she lost her powers on M-Day. Apocalypse's technology restored them (verify the order)
* Amplified power after Genosha: the death of millions on Genosha left a huge disturbance in the magnetic field there. In some stories that boosted her power and also damaged her mind

Skills and training

* Geophysics: graduate-level expertise. She understands the Earth's fields and structure, which helps her control
* X-Men combat training and decades of field experience
* Leadership: she led X-Factor in the All-New X-Factor series (2014)
* Flying and fighting in three dimensions

Weaknesses

* Emotional state affects her control. Stress, rage and grief can make her power surge or fail
* Mental health struggles. Later writers depict her with mental illness. The TV series The Gifted makes her bipolar explicitly
* Magnetic interference and power-dampening technology
* Psychic attack and possession. Malice, Mesmero and others have controlled her
* The pull of Magneto's legacy, which shapes her choices and how others see her

Personality

* Kind and idealistic at heart. She wants to help people and believes in coexistence
* Fierce temper. When pushed she hits hard, sometimes too hard
* Emotionally intense. Her highs and lows run strong, which later writers tie to mental illness
* Insecure about her identity for much of her history: is she her own person, Magneto's daughter, Havok's partner or a tool someone else is using
* Wants independence. A long-running theme is Lorna fighting to be defined by herself and not by her father, her boyfriend or whoever has taken over her mind
* Loyal. She stands by friends and teammates even when they fail her
* Brave to the point of recklessness
* A scientist's mind: practical and analytical when she is steady
* Carries trauma: her parents' deaths, years of possession, Genosha, M-Day and being used by villains
* Sense of humor and warmth that show more in the X-Factor books
* Over time she grows from an uncertain young woman into a confident leader who accepts both her power and her heritage without being ruled by them

History (Comics, Earth-616)

Childhood

* Born to Suzanna Dane. The man she believed was her father was Kenneth Dane
* Her green hair marks her as a mutant from birth
* As a small child she was on a plane with her parents during a confrontation tied to Magneto. Her powers emerged in fear and anger and the plane crashed, killing her parents (later retcon; versions differ on how much Magneto was involved) (verify)
* Magneto had her memories altered and left her to be raised by her mother's relatives
* She grew up not knowing she was a mutant and dyed her hair brown

1968: The X-Men

* The villain Mesmero finds her and manipulates her into believing she is Magneto's daughter, part of a plan to bring out her powers and use her
* The X-Men intervene. Bobby Drake (Iceman) falls for her
* She learns the truth about her mutation and joins the X-Men's world
* She falls in love with Alex Summers (Havok), which strains things with Bobby

1970s to 1980s: Havok, Arizona and the Marauders

* Lorna and Alex leave active hero work for a quieter life. She studies geophysics and earns her master's degree
* They return to help the X-Men at times
* The entity Malice possesses her and she leads the Marauders against the X-Men, including during the Mutant Massacre era
* The X-Men eventually free her

Late 1980s: Zaladane

* In the Savage Land, the priestess Zaladane claims to be Lorna's sister and steals her magnetic powers
* Lorna is left with superhuman strength, greater size and a body that feeds on negative emotion
* Her magnetism returns after Zaladane dies at Magneto's hands (verify)

1990s: X-Factor

* Lorna and Havok join the government-sponsored X-Factor, with Multiple Man, Strong Guy, Wolfsbane and Quicksilver (Peter David's run)
* She is a core member for years, working through her identity and her relationship with Alex

Early 2000s: Genosha and Havok's wedding

* 2001: Cassandra Nova's Sentinels destroy Genosha, Magneto's mutant nation, killing about sixteen million mutants. Lorna is on Genosha at the time (verify). The trauma and the magnetic aftermath damage her mind
* Around this time it is revealed or confirmed that Magneto is her biological father (verify order)
* Havok, after a coma, plans to marry Annie Ghazikhanian. Lorna is shattered at the wedding and has a violent breakdown

Mid 2000s: M-Day, Pestilence and space

* 2005, House of M and M-Day: the Scarlet Witch, Lorna's half-sister at the time, removes most mutants' powers. Lorna loses hers
* Apocalypse restores her power and makes her his Horseman Pestilence. The X-Men free her
* She goes into space with Havok, Marvel Girl (Rachel Grey) and others. She is captured by the Shi'ar under the emperor Vulcan, then joins the Starjammers and fights through the Shi'ar wars
* She returns to Earth later

2010s: X-Factor and leadership

* Joins Havok's government-sponsored X-Factor team (vol. 3, Peter David)
* Leads All-New X-Factor (2014), a corporate team funded by Serval Industries, with Quicksilver, Gambit and others
* Wanda and Pietro are later revealed not to be Magneto's biological children, which makes Lorna his only known living biological child at that point. Magneto also had a first daughter, Anya, who died young
* Depictions of her mental health become more explicit in this period

2019 to 2024: the Krakoa era

* Krakoa becomes a mutant nation. Lorna lives there
* X-Factor (2020) investigates mutant deaths so the dead can be resurrected on Krakoa. Lorna is a member
* X-Men Red (2022) moves her to Mars (Arakko) in the Brotherhood formed around Magneto and Storm
* Magneto dies during this era and Lorna grieves him and carries his legacy (verify later developments)

Relationships

* Magneto: her biological father. Distant and complicated. Over time she reaches a wary understanding of him
* Suzanna and Kenneth Dane: her mother and the man she believed was her father. Both died when she was small
* Wanda Maximoff and Pietro Maximoff: once her half-siblings, then not by blood after the 2015 retcon. Pietro and Lorna stay close as teammates
* Anya Eisenhardt: Magneto's first daughter, who died before Lorna was born
* Zaladane: claimed to be her sister. Whether this is true is murky
* Alex Summers (Havok): her great love. Together for decades with breaks, a broken wedding and reunions
* Bobby Drake (Iceman): her first admirer in the X-Men
* Jamie Madrox, Guido Carosella (Strong Guy) and Rahne Sinclair (Wolfsbane): X-Factor teammates
* Malice, Mesmero, Apocalypse, Zaladane and Cassandra Nova: the villains who used or broke her

Adaptations

* The Gifted (2017 to 2019, Fox TV, played by Emma Dumont): a leader of the Mutant Underground and Magneto's daughter. Explicitly bipolar. In a relationship with Marcos Diaz (Eclipse) and has a daughter, Dawn. In the second season she joins the Inner Circle with Reeva Payge
* X-Men '97 (2024): appears on Genosha in connection with Magneto (verify details)
* Earlier animated and game appearances are minor

Approved for This Story

* Lorna is Magneto's daughter
* Like the twins, she has never known her father
* She lives whatever life she has without him: a ward of the state, an orphan or similar (details open)
* She is not bipolar
* Which other traits she shares with her other versions is open
* Mutants mostly stay out of the MCU events. They are busy with their own affairs for some reason. Idea: an alternate-reality twist where they disappeared. The reason is open

Story Ideas (Proposed)

* Hidden at the start: in 1999 she dyes her hair and does not know or will not admit she is a mutant. Her powers are latent or barely showing
* The diner job gives her a reason to be independent and a regular place D can find her
* A scientist's mind: geophysics in the comics can become a strong interest in earth science at school, which puts her near Peter and Gwen
* Family: grows up without her parents as a ward of the state, an orphan or similar. That gives her and D a shared kind of loss without making them the same
* Her powers emerging under stress, possibly from the Juggernaut idea, gives a dramatic reveal
* Her struggle to be her own person and not defined by a man fits a story where she is a lead in her own right
