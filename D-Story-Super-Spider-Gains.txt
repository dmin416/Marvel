D Story: Super Spider Gains

Note: This is my own version of the story and its characters. Marvel and other works are inspirations only. References to canon, Raimi or other sources describe the inspiration and are not fixed facts of this story unless a file marks them as approved.

Status: brainstorm only. Nothing here is approved. All numbers are story design values with a single bite as the 100% baseline. The multiplier model in D-Story-Spider-Abilities.txt supersedes the efficacy numbers below.

Source

* The super spider is from Oscorp
* It could have been made by Peter's father or Cindy
* Gwen is the one bitten, right before college on the senior trip to Oscorp
* D collects the spider and works on it

Bite Baseline

* A bite on the hand sends venom through the bloodstream and lymph
* Fever, collapse and a rough night of rewriting follow
* The subject wakes changed with rough control

Protocol

* Fully supported with fluids, glucose, electrolytes, cooling, sedation and monitoring
* Micro-doses spread over a week
* Doses small enough to trigger no fever
* Each dose builds on the last
* Straight IV is the reckless shortcut that backfires

Gains vs a Single Bite

* Strength: about 150 to 200%
* Speed and reflexes: about 150 to 200%
* Durability: about 160 to 220%
* Healing: about 180 to 250%
* Spider-sense range and precision: about 150 to 200%
* Wall-crawling and grip: about 130 to 170%
* Stamina and recovery: about 200 to 300%
* Control on waking: near perfect

Why the Gains Are Larger

* Almost none of the vector is wasted
* No fever means the immune system never fights the rewrite
* Seven layers stack without progress resetting
* Full support means the rebuild never stalls

Extending the Protocol

* A second week at the same pace could add another 20 to 30% in each trait before returns flatten
* Slower spacing lets the body finish each layer fully
* A very high calorie diet and sleep schedule push results toward the top of each range

Ceiling

* Past about 300% of baseline the body starts to reject the changes
* Mutation and the Lizard-style failure begin there
* The optimal stopping point is just below that line

Limits

* Venom supply is the bottleneck: one spider cannot supply a week of doses
* D needs several spiders, a breeding method or a synthesis method
* The week out of school needs a cover story with May and Ben
