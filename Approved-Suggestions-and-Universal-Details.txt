Approved Suggestions and Universal Details

Note: This is my own version of the story and its characters. Marvel and other works are inspirations only. References to canon, Raimi or other sources describe the inspiration and are not fixed facts of this story unless a file marks them as approved.

Status: planning ideas only. Nothing here is set. The only story canon is D-Story-So-Far.txt, where D is fourteen and has just finished lunch at a New York children's home.

Approved Suggestions

D's Start

* Line to dinner table: D is in the F.E.A.S.T. meal line as a kid on his own. May notices, gives him an extra plate and invites him to Sunday dinner. Ben and Peter end up treating him like family.
* Played story note: D actually agreed to go to F.E.A.S.T. with the children's home group to help set up tables, with lunch provided. The meeting with May can grow out of that outing.
* Same age: D, Peter, Harry, Gwen and Lorna are the same age and all go to school together.
* Harry at school: Harry is the rich kid at their public high school. He shields Peter from bullies while Peter tutors him.
* Lorna's diner job (maybe): D learns Lorna works at the local diner to pay for her things.

Delivery Business

* Neutral courier: D delivers to anyone, heroes and villains alike. It gives him a reason to meet every faction without picking fights.
* Cover for intel: Every delivery puts him inside labs, warehouses and penthouses.
* Not world renowned: the business is a running joke among the people who know D is an eccentric superhero who delivers whatever, wherever. Word of it passes person to person.
* Secret or public business: He could run it either way just for fun. He has no one they can threaten him with.
* Damage Control contract (maybe Oscorp instead): D's company wins the Chitauri salvage hauling contract after the Battle of New York. That's the contract Adrian Toomes loses, which makes D the reason Vulture exists.
* Baxter Building runs: The Fantastic Four order strange parts. D's runs there are how the story checks in on them and Doom.
* Stark in Afghanistan: D delivers something to Stark in Afghanistan for Pepper.
* Idea: the delivery is a burger. Afterward Tony keeps D's number and calls him for deliveries. Maybe, maybe not.

MJ

* MJ's father is Nick Fury.
* She is Michelle Jones with a personality that mixes Michelle Jones and Mary Jane Watson.
* Harry loves her, the way Raimi's Harry loved Mary Jane.

Pairings

* Peter and Gwen
* Harry and MJ
* D and Lorna
* Flash and Liz Allan
* Betty Brant and Eddie Brock. Peter is never at the Bugle to get in Eddie's way, so Eddie becomes a famous journalist and Betty gets with him after that
* Jessica Jones and Luke Cage
* Danny Rand and Misty Knight. Colleen Wing stays as Misty's partner in Daughters of the Dragon and as Danny's training partner

Ned Leeds

* Ned becomes Hobgoblin. Without Peter as a friend there is nobody to pull him off a self-destruction path, so he goes down it and ends up with the Goblin gear somehow. How he gets it is open
* In the comics Ned was a Bugle reporter who was brainwashed into acting as Hobgoblin while Roderick Kingsley pulled the strings

Aunt May

* Aunt May works with F.E.A.S.T., Martin Li's shelter in Chinatown

Universal Details (Inspiration Reference)

Raimi's Spider

* Genetically engineered super-spider from a university lab
* Not keyed to anyone's bloodline, so anyone bitten gets powers

Gwen (Earth-65 Inspiration; the story follows the plans in the Outline instead)

* Drummer in a band called the Mary Janes
* Her father, Captain George Stacy, is a police captain
* After losing her powers she had never fought without them and could barely throw a punch

Symbiotes

* Weak to sound and fire
* Don't trigger spider-sense
* Can spawn offspring like Carnage

Peter (Raimi Inspiration)

* Fought Bone Saw in a wrestling cage
* Uncle Ben's line: "With great power comes great responsibility"
* Sold Spider-Man photos to J. Jonah Jameson at the Daily Bugle
* Dr. Curt Connors was his college professor

Norman (Raimi Inspiration)

* Oscorp head under military pressure for results
* Tested his performance enhancer on himself, which split his personality
* Goblin glider, pumpkin bombs and armor
* Killed the Oscorp board at the World Unity Festival after they pushed him out

Harry

* Raimi: how he met Peter is never shown; they are already best friends in high school when the first film opens
* Raimi: a rich kid at a public school who protects Peter from bullies while Peter helps him with math and science
* Raimi: embarrassed to be seen arriving by limo in front of the public school kids
* Raimi: shares an apartment with Peter after high school
* Comics: meets Peter at Empire State University after a rocky start, then becomes his best friend and roommate
* The Amazing Spider-Man 2 gave Harry a hereditary disease from Norman (retroviral hyperplasia)

Flash Thompson

* Approved: Flash is with Liz Allan
* Comics: Liz Allan was at Midtown High with Flash and dated him there
* Raimi: high school bully who picks a fight with Peter right after the spider bite
* Comics: Spider-Man's biggest fan even while bullying Peter
* Comics: joins the Army, loses his legs in combat and later bonds with the Venom symbiote as Agent Venom through Project Rebirth 2.0

Hobgoblin

* Approved: Ned Leeds is Hobgoblin in this story
* Inspiration: Roderick Kingsley finds one of Norman's hidden lairs and uses his gear
* Comics: Kingsley found Norman's lair and gear first, then set up Ned Leeds to take the fall as Hobgoblin

Mister Negative

* Martin Li runs the F.E.A.S.T. shelter in Chinatown as his front
* Martin Li is a genuine philanthropist and Mister Negative is a crime lord
* Comics: his Darkforce powers came from the same Maggia drug that created Cloak and Dagger
* Darkforce blasts, superhuman strength and a touch that corrupts people into loyal servants
* Leads the Inner Demons gang
* Insomniac's Spider-Man game: Norman and Otto's experiments gave Li his powers, which accidentally killed his parents. Li blames Norman
* Insomniac's Spider-Man game: tries to release Devil's Breath, an Oscorp bioweapon

Cloak and Dagger

* Two runaways given an experimental Maggia drug
* Cloak: Darkforce teleportation with a hunger only Dagger's light can calm
* Dagger: light daggers that can cure addiction

Polaris

* Lorna Dane
* Same age as D, Peter, Harry and Gwen and goes to school with them in this story
* Works at the local diner to pay for her things (maybe)
* Magnetic powers and green hair
* Magneto's daughter in the comics
* Approved for this story: she is Magneto's daughter and, like the twins, has never known her father. She is not bipolar. Her living situation (ward of the state, orphan or similar) is open

Eddie Brock

* Raimi's Eddie was fired from the Daily Bugle for faking a photo
* In this story Eddie never meets any symbiote

Other Villains

* Doctor Octopus (Raimi): fusion reactor experiment, inhibitor chip protecting his mind, wife Rosalie
* Electro (The Amazing Spider-Man 2): Max Dillon, a blue-glowing former Oscorp electrical engineer
* Sandman (Raimi): Flint Marko, who steals to pay for his sick daughter Penny
* Rhino: Aleksei Sytsevich
* Vulture (MCU): Adrian Toomes, who turns to crime with Chitauri salvage after losing his cleanup contract
* Scorpion: Mac Gargan
* Chameleon: master of disguise and Kraven's half-brother in the comics
* Tombstone: Lonnie Lincoln, a Harlem crime boss
* Kraven: big-game hunter
* Mysterio: illusionist with effects expertise; the MCU version is a former Stark employee
* Juggernaut: Cain Marko with unstoppable momentum. Chthon is the source in this story (the comics use the Crimson Gem of Cyttorak)

Hell's Kitchen

* Kingpin (Wilson Fisk) runs organized crime there
* Daredevil is Matt Murdock, a lawyer
* Punisher is Frank Castle

Fantastic Four and Doom (2005 Movie)

* Powers come from a cosmic storm on Victor Von Doom's space station
* In this story Victor is a son of Latveria's royal family who lives abroad as a businessman. Ultron's attack kills Latveria's rulers while Victor is fighting the Fantastic Four. The UN's Latveria Accords anger him and after Civil War he goes back to rule Latveria (see D-Story-Fantastic-Four.txt)

MCU Tie-Ins

* 2008, Tony abducted in Afghanistan: D delivers something to Stark in Afghanistan for Pepper
* 2010, Hulk fights Abomination in Harlem: the fight lands close to Tombstone's turf
* 2012, Battle of New York: Chitauri salvage fuels Vulture, Shocker and street-level weapons; Damage Control (or Oscorp) takes over the cleanup
* 2014, Hydra exposed inside S.H.I.E.L.D.: S.H.I.E.L.D.'s collapse can leave weapons on the street
* 2016, Latveria Accords: registration puts pressure on D, Gwen, Peter and Harry
