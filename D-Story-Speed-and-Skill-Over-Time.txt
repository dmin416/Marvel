D Story: Speed and Skill Over Time

Note: This is my own version of the story and its characters. Marvel and other works are inspirations only. References to canon, Raimi or other sources describe the inspiration and are not fixed facts of this story unless a file marks them as approved.

Status: design brainstorm only. Nothing here is approved. Companion to D-Story-Strength-Over-Time.txt.

Premise

* D is an undernourished 14 year old who starts training at 18
* Same two tracks as the strength file: untrained and trained from 18
* D trains to survive the world. Peter stays a weak nerd and Gwen takes self defense without weights and Harry trains just enough to attract women
* Trained values at age 22 match D's baseline in D-Story-Spider-Abilities.txt: sprint 7.5 m/s and vertical jump 0.5 m and reaction 220 ms
* Processing speed and working memory are indexes that peak at 100
* Task skill is an index of how skilled D is at practiced tasks such as driving and tools and delivery work. It rises with practice

Sourced Anchors

* Maximal sprint speed develops around and after peak height velocity. Step frequency drives speed before it and step length matters more after it (studies of boys aged 11 to 15 and a 2017 study of 375 boys)
* Countermovement jump height peaks at about 18 in males and peak jump power at about 21 (Finnish study of 30,217 people)
* Jump height rises about 5.6 percent per year in males from 11 to 15
* Boys' median vertical jump rises from about 24 to 38 cm across ages 9 to 17.9 (Colombian study)
* Processing speed peaks earliest at about 18 to 19 and declines gradually (Hartshorne and Germine 2015)
* Working memory peaks in the late 20s to early 30s (same study)
* Simple reaction time slowed about 0.55 ms per year across ages 18 to 65 in one study. Another found simple reaction time changes little until about 50 while choice reaction time slows throughout adulthood
* Practice drives task skill. No source gives a curve for it so the skill index is a design value
* Broad athletic peak is typically 20 to 30 years and varies by sport

Key Points

* Sprint speed
  * Untrained peaks at 6.8 m/s from about 20 to 29 and falls to 6.5 m/s by 35
  * Trained peaks at 7.8 m/s from 25 to 29 and falls to 7.3 m/s by 35
  * Biggest gains at 14 to 18 as the body grows and then at 18 to 22 through training
* Vertical jump
  * Untrained peaks at 0.37 m around 18 to 21
  * Trained peaks at 0.52 m from 24 to 27
* Reaction time
  * Untrained reaches about 250 ms by 20 and then slows slowly
  * Trained reaches 215 ms by 25 through practice and holds until about 29
* Processing speed peaks at 19 and declines about 0.6 points per year
* Working memory peaks at 30
* Task skill rises fastest from 15 to 22 and plateaus at 94 from 31 to 34

Use With the Venom Model

* The venom multiplies the baseline at the age of the dose
* Reaction time is divided by the reaction multiplier
* Task skill is not multiplied. Faster bodies need more practice to control
* The venom speeds the body but not the learning of control. Weeks of controlled dosing give D time to adjust
