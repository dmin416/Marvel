How Boys Mature From 14 to the Mid-20s

14 to 15

* Puberty timing varies by years. Early bloomers are taller and stronger and get status from it. Late bloomers like a nerdy Peter are smaller and get picked on
* Status comes from athletics, toughness, humor and being seen with the right people
* Friendships form side by side through shared activity: games, sports, projects and hanging out
* Closeness is shown through teasing, banter and loyalty more than talk. Feelings are mostly hidden
* Bullying is still common, though it peaks in middle school. Bullies usually pick targets who will not fight back and who have no protector

16 to 17

* Most finish growing. Late bloomers catch up physically but often lag in confidence
* Risk-taking peaks, especially in front of peers. Impulse control lags behind because the brain's planning centers mature into the mid-20s
* Dating becomes real and status starts to include girls
* Hierarchies loosen a little as students sort into their own groups: athletes, smart kids, band kids, skaters and others

18 to 20

* Leaving home or high school resets status. Being popular in high school counts for much less
* Many bullies mellow once the audience is gone. Others peak in high school and drift
* Late bloomers often gain confidence once brains count for more than size
* First real failures, losses and responsibilities shape who they become

21 to 25

* Impulse control and long-range planning settle
* Identity firms up around work, relationships and values
* Old friendships either deepen or fade. The ones that survive high school are usually for life

Source Ages

Raimi trilogy (Tobey Maguire, who was about 26 in the first film)

* Spider-Man (2002): Peter is a high school senior, about 17 to 18, and graduates partway through. Harry and Flash are classmates of the same age
* Spider-Man 2 (2004): Peter is in college at Empire State University, roughly 20. The films never give an exact age
* Spider-Man 3 (2007): Peter is still in college, roughly 21 to 22

The Amazing Spider-Man (Andrew Garfield, who was about 28 in the first film)

* The Amazing Spider-Man (2012): Peter is a high school student, usually given as about 17. Flash is a classmate
* The Amazing Spider-Man 2 (2014): Peter graduates from high school at the start, about 18. Harry is a childhood friend of about the same age who has been away at boarding school

MCU (Tom Holland) for comparison

* Far From Home: 16
* No Way Home: 17 to 18

The Source Versions

Peter

* Raimi: shy, nerdy and broke. Bullied in high school. Grows into a guilt-driven adult who keeps sacrificing his own life for responsibility. His growth is learning to let people in
* Webb: a loner with a skateboard and a camera, smarter and cockier than Raimi's Peter. Driven by his missing parents. His growth runs through loss: Ben, Captain Stacy and Gwen
* Comics: a picked-on science nerd who becomes confident and funny once he has the mask

Harry

* Raimi: the rich kid at a public school who protects Peter while Peter tutors him. Desperate for Norman's approval and never gets it. Resents Spider-Man after Norman dies and turns that into revenge
* Webb: sent away to boarding school and neglected by Norman. Returns charming, bitter and sick with retroviral hyperplasia. Fear of death turns him into the Goblin
* Comics: meets Peter at Empire State University after a rocky start. Struggles with drugs and with Norman's legacy

Flash

* Raimi: a jock bully who picks a fight with Peter right after the bite
* Webb: a bully who softens after Ben's death and comforts Peter
* Comics: Spider-Man's biggest fan even while bullying Peter Parker. His father Harrison Thompson is an abusive alcoholic. He joins the Army, loses his legs in combat and later bonds with the Venom symbiote as Agent Venom. Grows into one of the most changed characters in the comics

Story Projections (Ideas)

Peter Parker

* 1999 to 2001 (14 to 16): a late bloomer, small and weak. Brilliant in science, shy and awkward with girls. Bullied by Flash. Tutors Harry and is protected by him. Close to May and Ben
* 2001 to 2003 (16 to 18): finds his footing through brains and his friendships. Still weak since he does not train. Raimi-style crush or quiet interest in a girl in the group
* 2003 to 2007 (18 to 22, college): works for Curt Connors. The Lizard path brings strength, aggression and cage fights, then Ben's talk. Becomes the Lizard, nearly dies after Gwen's fight and is saved by D
* After: becomes a hero. Guilt and responsibility shape him like the Raimi Peter, softened by having people who know the truth
* Adult: understanding and steady. Gets together with Gwen in 2016

Harry Osborn

* 1999 to 2001 (14 to 16): the rich kid at a public school. Embarrassed by the limo. Uses money and confidence to fit in. Protects Peter, partly out of real loyalty and partly because having someone to protect makes him feel needed. Craves Norman's approval
* 2001 to 2003 (16 to 18): trains just enough to impress girls. Popular, a little reckless. Parties. His grades depend on Peter's tutoring
* 2003 to 2007 (18 to 22): college with Peter. Norman's pressure grows. Signs of the genetic curse may start (backstory TBD)
* 2008: Norman's madness. Learns D kept Norman alive and learns about the insanity
* 2009 to 2011: takes the Goblin mantle, cures his curse with Peter, Gwen and D and becomes a hero. Running Oscorp gives the group money and labs (unapproved idea)
* Adult: grows from approval-seeker into someone carrying his own legacy. Hobgoblin is the villain who mirrors him (unapproved idea)

Flash Thompson

* 1999 to 2001 (14 to 16): early bloomer and jock. Bullies Peter for status. Open: whether his father is abusive at home as in the comics
* 2001 to 2003 (16 to 18): still on top socially. Cracks start to show
* After high school: loses the audience that made him a bully. Comes around eventually and joins the team in some way, possibly as Harry's sidekick (timing TBD in the 2012 to 2014 era)
* Open: whether he joins the Army as in the comics
* Ned exists only if he is needed
