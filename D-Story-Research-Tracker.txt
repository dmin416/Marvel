Part 2: Science Fixed This Session

* Electric eel pulse: the 2 ms figure is likely the gap between the low voltage pulse and the first strong pulse and not the pulse width. Low voltage discharge lasts 1.24 to 1.78 ms. Hunting pulses fire at about 500 Hz. High voltage output scales with body length (151 V at 200 mm up to 572 V at 609 mm)
* Axolotl limb staging: wound healing by day 3 and dedifferentiation by day 8 and early bud by day 15 and palette by day 28 and digital outgrowth by day 37 (age of animals not stated). Adult siblings regenerated slower and with deformities in one experiment
* Cell division and energy numbers for regrowth added in D-Story-Regeneration-Speeds.txt
* Portia acuity confirmed against Harland and Jackson (2000)
* Jump distance corrected to about 6 body lengths
* Trap-jaw strike corrected to 0.084 ms mean

Part 3: Comic and Film Notes

* Spider-Man strength: Class 10 handbook rating (about 10 tons). Later handbook text about 20 tons. Single stories show 50 to 200 plus tons (fan summaries). No source confirms a garbage truck lift
* Comic feats: Amazing Spider-Man #33 (machinery) and #424 (subway car) and Spider-Man #98 (Daily Bugle building)
* Spider-Man 2 (2004) train stop: no official tonnage. A modern NYC subway car weighs about 38.6 tonnes empty
* Symbiotes: strength figures unverified (Venom about 11 tons and Anti-Venom 13 to 70 tons and Carnage over 80 tons)
* Symbiote codex: each symbiote leaves a cellular imprint wrapped around the host's DNA (Venom #8)
* Control examples: Ben Reilly's will kept a symbiote from taking control. Flash Thompson struggled with Agent Venom. Max Modell overcame the symbiote with help
* Regeneration: Wolverine regrows eyes in seconds and organs from a skeleton in minutes (varies by writer). Lizard regrows an arm in the comics and film. See D-Story-Regeneration-Speeds.txt
