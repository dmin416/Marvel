The Multiplier Model

* Super spider venom is a multiplier applied to a person's own baseline
* A single full dose (Gwen) gives multiplier M1 for each trait
* Efficacy is the effect size of the venom. A single dose is 100%
* The effect is M1 minus 1. At efficacy E the multiplier is 1 + E x (M1 - 1)
* D at 300% efficacy: M = 1 + 3 x (M1 - 1)
* A higher baseline means a higher absolute result. Venom does not level people out
* Gain in absolute terms equals baseline x (M - 1). The stronger person gains the most

Trait Multipliers

* Strength and lift: single dose x50 and D at 300% x148 (chosen middle option between grounded x10 and comic Class 10 parity at x200)
* Jump height: single dose x10 and D at 300% x28
* Running speed: single dose x2 and D at 300% x4
  * Speed scales far less than strength because air drag grows with the square of speed
* Reaction speed (time divided by M): single dose x3 and D at 300% x7
* Durability and impact tolerance: single dose x3 and D at 300% x7
* Healing rate: single dose x5 and D at 300% x13
* Stamina and recovery: single dose x5 and D at 300% x13

Baselines Used

* Verified
  * Untrained adult man deadlift is about 70 kg and an untrained adult woman about 39 kg (strength standards summaries)
  * Deadlift record: 501 kg by Hafthor Bjornsson in 2020 (with a suit) and 505 kg in 2025. Eddie Hall's 500 kg is held in a separate category
  * Raw bench press record: 355 kg by Julius Maddox in 2021
  * Usain Bolt's top speed was about 12.4 m/s (about 44.7 km/h). A UC Davis analysis gives 12.28 m/s
  * Bolt's reaction time to the gun was 0.146 s to 0.17 s depending on the source
* Design estimates (not sourced)
  * Lift of 50 kg for a scrawny nerd and for Gwen. Gwen is a fit drummer so she sits above the 39 kg untrained figure
  * D lifts 140 kg as a larger fit man
  * Elite tier (Daredevil or Punisher) lifts 250 kg
  * Sprint speeds, reaction times and vertical jumps for the non-record people
  * Reaction times for ordinary people are estimates and need a source before use

Results

Baselines
* Scrawny nerd (Peter type): lift 50 kg | sprint 5.5 m/s (20 km/h) | reaction 260 ms | vertical jump 0.3 m
* Gwen (small woman): lift 50 kg | sprint 6.0 m/s (22 km/h) | reaction 250 ms | vertical jump 0.3 m
* D (larger fit man): lift 140 kg | sprint 7.5 m/s (27 km/h) | reaction 220 ms | vertical jump 0.5 m
* Elite (Daredevil/Punisher tier): lift 250 kg | sprint 9.0 m/s (32 km/h) | reaction 180 ms | vertical jump 0.75 m
* Peak human (record level): lift 505 kg | sprint 12.4 m/s (45 km/h) | reaction 150 ms | vertical jump 1.0 m

Single full dose
* Scrawny nerd (Peter type): lift 2,500 kg | sprint 11.0 m/s (40 km/h) | reaction 87 ms | jump 3.0 m
* Gwen (small woman): lift 2,500 kg | sprint 12.0 m/s (43 km/h) | reaction 83 ms | jump 3.0 m
* D (larger fit man): lift 7,000 kg | sprint 15.0 m/s (54 km/h) | reaction 73 ms | jump 5.0 m
* Elite (Daredevil/Punisher tier): lift 12,500 kg | sprint 18.0 m/s (65 km/h) | reaction 60 ms | jump 7.5 m
* Peak human (record level): lift 25,250 kg | sprint 24.8 m/s (89 km/h) | reaction 50 ms | jump 10.0 m

D protocol 300% efficacy
* Scrawny nerd (Peter type): lift 7,400 kg | sprint 22.0 m/s (79 km/h) | reaction 37 ms | jump 8.4 m
* Gwen (small woman): lift 7,400 kg | sprint 24.0 m/s (86 km/h) | reaction 36 ms | jump 8.4 m
* D (larger fit man): lift 20,720 kg | sprint 30.0 m/s (108 km/h) | reaction 31 ms | jump 14.0 m
* Elite (Daredevil/Punisher tier): lift 37,000 kg | sprint 36.0 m/s (130 km/h) | reaction 26 ms | jump 21.0 m
* Peak human (record level): lift 74,740 kg | sprint 49.6 m/s (179 km/h) | reaction 21 ms | jump 28.0 m

Comparisons

* D at 300% against Gwen at a single dose
  * Multiplier ratio: strength 3.0x and jump 2.8x and speed 2x and reaction 2.3x
  * Result ratio: lift 8.3x and jump 4.7x and sprint 2.5x and reaction 2.7x quicker
  * About two thirds of the lift gap is D's higher baseline. The rest is his higher efficacy
* Peak human against a scrawny nerd
  * Baseline: lift 10.1x and sprint 2.3x and reaction 1.7x quicker and jump 3.3x
  * After any equal dose the ratios stay the same for lift because the venom is a pure multiplier
  * Single-dose peak human lifts 25,250 kg against 2,500 kg for the nerd
  * Peak human gains 24,745 kg from one dose and the nerd gains 2,450 kg
* Peak human on a single dose against D at 300%
  * Lift: peak human is 1.2x stronger (25,250 kg against 20,720 kg)
  * Sprint: D is faster (30.0 m/s against 24.8 m/s) because speed scales so little
* Peak human at 300% efficacy lifts about 75 tonnes and sprints near 50 m/s. That is the extreme ceiling for this model

Notes for the Story

* Gwen's single dose puts her at about 2.5 tonnes: far above every human record and enough to lift a car while staying a small woman
* An empty garbage truck (about 14 to 16 tonnes) is out of reach for Gwen but within D at 300% (about 20.7 tonnes). A loaded truck (about 24 to 28 tonnes) is beyond both
* D's protocol is the best case for a body of his size. A stronger person on the same protocol would exceed him
* The ceiling from the earlier spider files still applies. About 300% is the safe limit before mutation
* Cost still applies: calories, sleep and recovery time scale with the multiplier

Open Questions

* Whether efficacy can stack past 300% for someone with a higher baseline
* Whether the multiplier should apply to cross-section strength or to muscle quality only
* Whether durability keeps pace with strength so users do not injure themselves

Canon Feat Check

* Comics lifting rating
  * Classic Marvel handbook rates Spider-Man as Class 10 or about 10 tons
  * Later handbook text cited at about 20 tons under optimal conditions (secondary source)
  * Single stories show 50 to 200 plus tons when he is pushed to the limit. These are not day-to-day (fan summaries and unverified)
  * Amazing Spider-Man #33 (1966): lifts giant machinery off himself while trapped
  * Amazing Spider-Man #424 (1997): lifts a subway car
  * Spider-Man #98 (1998): lifts part of the Daily Bugle building
* Garbage truck
  * No source found for a specific Spider-Man garbage truck lift. The feat could not be confirmed
  * Truck weights if used as a reference: about 14 to 16 tonnes empty (31,000 to 36,000 lb) and about 24 to 28 tonnes loaded (52,500 to 62,000 lb), per municipal specs. A Barrie, Ontario recycling truck lists 15,460 kg empty and 26,870 kg loaded
* Train
  * Spider-Man 2 (2004): he stops a runaway elevated train by anchoring with webs and straining against its momentum
  * No official tonnage for the film train exists in the sources found
  * Modern NYC subway car: about 38.6 tonnes empty and 55.3 tonnes full (R160B, secondhand from a forum quoting Wikipedia). An older 60 foot car weighed 84,300 lb (about 38 tonnes). A 1911 ten-car train was about 350 tons
  * Stopping a train depends on the force over time and not only on lift strength. The webs share the load

Story Model vs Canon

* Chosen: strength x50 for a single dose. Gwen lifts about 2.5 tonnes and D at 300% lifts about 20.7 tonnes
* A 10 ton lift from a 50 kg baseline needs a multiplier of x200
* A loaded garbage truck of about 27 tonnes from a 50 kg baseline needs about x540
* Options considered
  * Keep the grounded model: strength x10 gives a Gwen who is strong but cannot lift a car
  * Raise the strength multiplier to about x200 for Class 10 parity: Gwen about 10 tonnes and D at 300% (1 + 3 x 199 = x598) about 84 tonnes from a 140 kg baseline
  * Pick a middle value such as x50: Gwen about 2.5 tonnes and D at 300% (x148) about 20.7 tonnes
