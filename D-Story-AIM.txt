D Story: A.I.M. and Extremis

Note: This is my own version of the story and its characters. Marvel and other works are inspirations only. References to canon, Raimi or other sources describe the inspiration and are not fixed facts of this story unless a file marks them as approved.

Status: planning ideas only. Nothing here is set. The only story canon is D-Story-So-Far.txt.

Accuracy note: written from memory of the source material and not fact checked. Dates, names, issue numbers and plot details should be verified before they are relied on.

Approved Direction

* A.I.M. is important to the story because it drives the Iron Man 3 storyline
* Idea for the resolution: something like the animated Iron Man: Extremis. Tony gets the living tar beaten out of him and then takes a version of Extremis that saves him and changes him. This is only an idea

A.I.M. in This Story (Proposed)

* Advanced Idea Mechanics is Aldrich Killian's private think tank for dangerous research
* It sells to governments, militaries and anyone else who pays
* It starts small and grows through the 2000s on defense money and black-market buyers
* It fits the leak-downward pattern in D-Story-World-Economy.txt: research made for governments that ends up with criminals

Timeline (Proposed, Following the MCU Book)

* December 31, 1999: New Year's Eve in Bern, Switzerland. Killian pitches A.I.M. to Tony Stark and Maya Hansen. Tony brushes him off and leaves him waiting alone on a rooftop. The humiliation becomes Killian's grudge against Tony. This is one day after D's 15th birthday and the planned symbiote contact
* 2000s: Maya joins A.I.M. Extremis is developed and tested on wounded veterans of the wars of the decade. Unstable subjects explode
* 2000s to 2013: Killian covers the explosions by inventing a terrorist called the Mandarin. The Mandarin is played by an actor, Trevor Slattery. The fake attacks let A.I.M. sell to both sides of a war it created
* December 2013 to early 2014: the Iron Man 3 storyline. Killian moves on Tony, Pepper and the President
* After: Wenwu, the real leader of the Ten Rings, is angry that Killian used the name "the Mandarin." The Ten Rings take Trevor prisoner

Extremis

* Maya Hansen's regenerative treatment
* It rewrites the brain's repair center so the body can regrow limbs and heal almost anything
* Subjects gain great strength, fast healing and burning heat
* Subjects whose bodies reject it overheat and explode
* Regeneration science links it to Curt Connors' lizard research and the Oscorp programs

Iron Man 3 Inspiration (MCU Movie)

* Killian recruits Maya and uses Extremis soldiers as his army
* The Mandarin broadcasts claim responsibility for the explosions
* Tony's Malibu home is destroyed and the world thinks he is dead
* Tony works alone in Tennessee with a damaged suit and learns the Mandarin is a fake
* Killian makes a deal with the Vice President, who wants Extremis for his daughter's leg
* Killian kidnaps Pepper and injects her with Extremis
* Killian tries to kill the President on live television and put a puppet in charge
* On an oil rig Tony calls in all his suits. Pepper, now carrying Extremis, kills Killian
* Maya dies along the way
* Tony has the shrapnel removed from his chest and destroys his suits

Extremis Comic and Animated Inspiration (Iron Man: Extremis)

* Iron Man: Extremis is a 2005 to 2006 comic by Warren Ellis and Adi Granov, adapted as a motion comic in 2010
* Maya Hansen develops Extremis at the company Futurepharm with Aldrich Killian
* Killian leaks Extremis to Mallen, an anti-government extremist. Killian then kills himself
* Mallen becomes superhumanly strong and tough
* Mallen beats Tony in the armor nearly to death
* To survive, Tony takes a version of Extremis that Maya reprograms for him
* The modified Extremis heals him and rebuilds him. Part of the armor now lives inside his body and he controls his suits and computers directly with his mind
* With it Tony beats Mallen
* Tony learns Maya helped Killian leak Extremis and has her arrested

Resolution Idea for This Story

* Blend the two: the MCU storyline of A.I.M., the fake Mandarin and Killian's grudge, with the comic's ending
* An Extremis soldier, Killian himself or another enhanced fighter, beats Tony nearly to death
* Tony takes a version of Extremis to survive
* It heals him and lets him link with his suits by thought
* Open: who gives Tony the version (Maya, someone else or D)
* Pepper is injected with Extremis and kills Killian, probably as in the movie
* Pepper keeps Extremis unless it is taken away. Open: whether it is removed and what her mindset is about keeping it
* Open: whether Tony still destroys his suits afterward

Comics A.I.M. (Inspiration)

* Founded as Hydra's science division and later broke away
* Its scientists wear yellow hazmat suits nicknamed beekeepers
* Led by the Scientist Supreme
* Its creations include the Cosmic Cube and MODOK, a scientist mutated into a giant head built for killing
* In later comics it runs its own island nation

D's Possible Links (Ideas)
