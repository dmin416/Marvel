Story Outline

Note: This is my own version of the story and its characters. Marvel and other works are inspirations only. References to canon, Raimi or other sources describe the inspiration and are not fixed facts of this story unless a file marks them as approved.

Story Order

1. School and college are the start of the story
2. Then the other stuff and the MCU stuff

D

* Christian who lived a good life
* God allows him to experience the stories he loves: Marvel, DC, zombie worlds, anime, Avatar, Fallout, Cyberpunk and wherever else he wants
* Has experience from going through countless multiverses, multiple times
* Attains powers and items along the way
* Can choose to have his powers or start fresh
* Likes helping people and helps them if he can
* The only people with good souls are the people who live with him
* In college and has powers when Tony is abducted
* Idea: runs a sort of delivery business
* Orphan
* Meets Peter and Aunt May in the F.E.A.S.T. meal line as a kid on his own
* May notices him, gives him an extra plate and invites him to Sunday dinner
* Ben and Peter end up treating him like family
* Peter, Harry, D, Gwen and Lorna are the same age
* They all go to school together

Delivery Business

* Neutral courier is fun
* Cover for intel is a given
* He could have a secret or public business just for fun
* He has no one they can threaten him with

Polaris

* Really important
* Main female lead next to Gwen
* Lorna is the same age as D, Peter, Harry and Gwen
* Goes to school with them

Flash

* Originally a bully
* Comes around eventually and joins the team in some way

World

* Gwen Stacy and Peter Parker exist
* Miles Morales doesn't exist
* Green Goblin and Otto Octavius exist
* Electro exists
* Sandman exists
* Rhino exists
* Chthon's Juggernaut exists
* Eddie Brock exists as a lousy reporter and eventually a SWAT team member
* D will take Eddie's symbiote and absorb it after experimenting on it
* Kingpin, Kraven and Mysterio can show up when it makes sense
* Kingpin, Punisher and Daredevil probably stick to Hell's Kitchen
* Scorpion, Hobgoblin, Chameleon and Tombstone exist
* Big note: Mister Negative
* Cloak and Dagger are in the story
* Aunt May works with F.E.A.S.T.
* Vulture appears after The Avengers
* Most of Spider-Man's villains exist in one way or another
* Raimi's version of Spider-Man
* No Morlun or totems
* Super spiders exist
* Cindy Moon is downplayed for now (idea: scientist turned villain like the Green Goblin)

Gwen

* A different version of Gwen who doesn't change dimensions
* Has some of her memories and character growth
* Loses her powers after Cindy gives her a McGuffin neutralizer that makes her unable to get spider powers again
* Has to rely on symbiote D
* As a symbiote, D appreciates adult Gwen's body
* It's rude to Peter. Peter is fine since he loved Mary Jane after she ditched him for every famous nobody a thousand times
* Peter likely won't know
* Unknown if Gwen and Peter like each other

Green Goblin and the Osborns

* Norman is like the Raimi version
* Uncle Ben gives Peter the talk after Lizard Peter beats guys in the wrestling cage
* Norman will fight whoever (Peter, Gwen, D or whoever) and nearly kill himself but survive
* Maybe still have the killing of the shareholders and taking over the company
* Harry Osborn will learn that D kept Norman alive and learn about his father's insanity
* Harry takes on the mantle
* With the trio's help, Harry perfects the serum and cures his own genetic curse (backstory to be decided)
* The four of them do their own hero stuff
* Idea: D and Peter lead a subdivision of Oscorp after the takeover, Oscorp's deep science division the way Hydra was Hitler's deep science division in the MCU
* Peter Parker is on track to become the Lizard
* Peter will be saved and eventually become a hero after his near death
* Assuming she killed him, Gwen goes on her character arc
* Gwen will also be saved by D
* D will eventually take over a symbiote maybe and bond with Gwen in a managed way
* Gwen and Peter will eventually get together and become a crime-fighting duo
* Superpowers are acknowledged as existing but somewhat rare
* Many villains and an overall helpless police force, comic style
* No massive world-ending issues
* The Fantastic Four and Doom are side notes fighting amongst themselves, like the 2005 movie
