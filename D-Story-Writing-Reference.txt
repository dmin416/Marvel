D Story: Writing Reference

Note: This is my own version of the story and its characters. Marvel and other works are inspirations only. References to canon, Raimi or other sources describe the inspiration and are not fixed facts of this story unless a file marks them as approved.

Premise

* D is a Christian who lived a good life. God allows him to experience the stories he loves: Marvel, DC, zombie worlds, anime, Avatar, Fallout, Cyberpunk and anywhere else he wants.
* He carries experience from countless multiverses, many traveled more than once.
* He gains powers and items along the way and can choose to keep his powers or start fresh in each world.
* In this world he starts as a normal kid with most of his memories sealed off.
* He likes helping people and helps them when he can.
* The only people with good souls are the people who live with him.

This World

* The world is the story's own, built from many inspirations, with a Raimi-style Spider-Man core.
* Superpowers exist but are somewhat rare.
* The police force is mostly helpless, comic style.
* There are no massive world-ending threats.
* The Fantastic Four and Doom feud in the background, like the 2005 movie.
* Kingpin, Punisher and Daredevil mostly stay in Hell's Kitchen.
* Super spiders exist and come from Oscorp. There are no Morlun or totems, and no Miles Morales.
* The symbiote comes from space.

Story Order

1. School and college arc
2. Then the wider world events

Core Cast

D

* Normal kid at the start with most of his memories sealed off
* Orphan who meets Peter and Aunt May in the F.E.A.S.T. meal line as a kid on his own
* May gives him an extra plate and invites him to Sunday dinner; Ben and Peter treat him like family
* Same age as Peter, Harry, Gwen and Lorna, and goes to school with them
* Finishes college right before 2008 and has his powers when Tony is abducted in spring 2008
* Runs a delivery business: a neutral courier for heroes and villains alike and a cover for intel, run secretly or publicly just for fun. He has no one villains can threaten him with.
* Later takes Eddie's symbiote, a space-born symbiote, experiments on it and absorbs it

Peter Parker

* Raimi-style Peter, but on the Lizard path instead of getting spider powers
* The Lizard arc is finished before 2008
* Lizard Peter beats guys in the wrestling cage; Uncle Ben gives him the talk
* Becomes the Lizard, nearly dies and is saved by D
* Becomes a hero afterward
* Gets together with Gwen as a crime-fighting duo
* Learns about Gwen's symbiote bond with D and understands it: the symbiote is basically a clone of D and completely connected to D
* Tries to help when Gwen loses her powers, but Cindy's serum blocks it

Gwen Stacy

* A version who never changes dimensions but has some of her memories and character growth
* Bitten by a super spider from Oscorp in school and gains her powers
* Fights Lizard Peter and believes she killed him; the guilt starts her character arc
* Later her multiversal memories confuse her further, adding to the arc
* D also saves her (situation TBD)
* Cindy Moon's neutralizer permanently takes her spider powers
* As an adult, relies on symbiote D in a managed bond
* D's symbiote gives her the powers she desires instead of technology
* D appreciates her thankfulness and her body but quickly gets over it and steps back, since Peter truly loves her
* Gets together with Peter. Not really a thruple: D is a third wheel only when he chooses and only in the beginning

Harry Osborn

* Rich kid at their public high school who shields Peter from bullies while Peter tutors him
* Learns D kept Norman alive and learns about his father's insanity
* Takes on the Goblin mantle
* Peter, Gwen and D help him perfect the serum and cure his genetic curse
* Joins the other three as a hero team

Lorna Dane (Polaris)

* Main female lead next to Gwen, and really important to the story
* Same age as the group and at their school
* Maybe: D learns she works at the local diner to pay for her things

Flash Thompson

* Starts as a bully
* Eventually comes around and joins the team, possibly as Harry's sidekick
* No Ned unless he's needed

Norman Osborn

* Raimi-style: the enhancer test, then possibly killing the shareholders and taking over Oscorp
* His madness happens after Peter works for Curt and the Lizard arc
* Fights whoever and nearly kills himself; D keeps him alive

Supporting

* Aunt May: works at F.E.A.S.T., Martin Li's shelter in Chinatown
* Uncle Ben: treats D like family and gives Peter the talk
* Eddie Brock: lousy reporter who later becomes a SWAT team member
* Cindy Moon: downplayed for now; possible scientist turned villain later

Villains

* Big note: Mister Negative
* Present: Green Goblin, Otto Octavius, Electro, Sandman, Rhino, Chthon's Juggernaut, Scorpion, Hobgoblin, Chameleon and Tombstone
* When they fit: Kingpin, Kraven and Mysterio
* After the Battle of New York: Vulture
* Allies: Cloak and Dagger
* Most of Spider-Man's villains exist in one form or another.
* Otto Octavius: his existence begins the same way as in the original trilogy (fusion reactor experiment, inhibitor chip, wife Rosalie). Once D interacts with him anything is on the table. Whether his wife dies is unknown.

Writing Approach

* The story plays like a game. Files record starting points and plans, and outcomes are not fixed because it is hard to say how things will turn out.
* Daily life is the backbone; hero work is the interruption.
* Anchor locations: Parker Sunday dinners, school, Lorna's diner, the F.E.A.S.T. kitchen, Gwen's band practice and Peter's lab corner.
* D as the lens: courier routes and dinners give reasons to drop into everyone's routines.
* Cost scenes: heroics cost missed gigs, sleep, grades, dates and May's trust.
* Interludes: short slice-of-life chapters after big fights.
* Montages: cover routine stretches quickly and slow down for moments that change something.
* Dramatic irony: D and the reader know secrets Peter and Gwen keep from each other.
* Small rituals: May's extra plate, Gwen's drumsticks and the regular diner booth make the world feel lived in.
* Ratio: about two daily life scenes per hero scene in the school and college arc, shifting toward action later.
