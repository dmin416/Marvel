D Story: Unapproved Ideas

Note: This is my own version of the story and its characters. Marvel and other works are inspirations only. References to canon, Raimi or other sources describe the inspiration and are not fixed facts of this story unless a file marks them as approved.

Status: planning ideas only. Nothing here is set. The only story canon is D-Story-So-Far.txt, where D is fourteen and has just finished lunch at a New York children's home.

Suggestions from earlier planning that are not approved yet.

Oscorp Deep Science Division

* Gives D's symbiote experiments a legitimate home and cover story
* First job is cleaning up Norman's leftover formula, lairs and projects; Kingsley reaching a lair first is another possible Hobgoblin setup
* Mister Negative targets the division, with Devil's Breath as the prize
* D's courier company moves the division's sensitive material
* Electro and Octavius can work there as staff before things go wrong
* If Oscorp wins the Chitauri cleanup contract, the salvage goes straight to the division

Osborns

* Norman ends up in a coma or locked away after D saves him
* Harry running Oscorp gives the group money and labs
* Hobgoblin is the villain who mirrors Harry

Public Heroes (plan for later)

* For now D, Harry, Peter, Gwen and Flash stay masked heroes
* Later the group could go public, as a team backed by D's courier company, Harry's money or the Oscorp deep science division
* Stark went public with "I am Iron Man" (2008) and the Fantastic Four were public by default
* Hybrid option: Harry and Flash as the public faces with D's company as the brand while Peter and Gwen stay masked
* The 2016 Latveria Accords could force the question
* Risk: Aunt May, Uncle Ben, Captain Stacy and the Parkers become targets

Hobgoblin Origin (to figure out later)

* Idea: Harry commits Norman to a facility when his craziness gets worse
* Idea: a person at the facility injects Norman's blood into himself and becomes Hobgoblin
* Open: who the person is and whether this is the origin

Mister Negative

* Li and Negative are two sides that don't fully know each other
* In the game version Norman's experiments gave Li the powers that accidentally killed his parents. That gives Li a grudge against Norman and later Harry
* His corrupting touch could turn D's staff or Gwen
* The Inner Demons gang supplies street-level enemies
* Darkforce reacting badly to symbiotes makes him a direct threat to Gwen's suit
* Works as the slow-burn main villain across several arcs

Cloak and Dagger

* Dagger's light can undo Mister Negative's corruption
* Cloak's teleportation makes them ideal delivery staff for D

Polaris

* Hides her powers and green hair
* Juggernaut could be what makes her powers come out
* Approved since: Magneto exists and Lorna is his daughter who has never known him (see D-Story-Lorna-Polaris.txt)
* Could join D's household or business

Police

* Captain Stacy is the bridge between police and heroes
* Public opinion swings between hero worship and fear

Fantastic Four

* Arcs can open with a news clip of their latest fight with Doom

Arc Structure

1. Business starts and D meets everyone
2. Lizard Peter and Gwen's guilt (Gwen is bitten right before college on the senior trip to Oscorp)
3. Goblin, the shareholders die and Norman survives
4. Cindy's neutralizer and D's symbiote (D's symbiote contact comes around 2000)
5. Harry takes the mantle and the four form a team
6. Mister Negative steps out as the main villain

Villain-of-the-week stories run alongside all six arcs.

Parker Family and Friendships

May and Ben Adopt D

* May and Ben are somewhat younger than in the Raimi films (around 48 and 50) and modestly more financially comfortable
* A stable home in Queens with room for Peter and D, possibly a three-bedroom house
* May works at F.E.A.S.T. and meets fourteen-year-old D through the charity's meals or outreach
* May and Ben come to care for D, possibly fostering him first, then formally adopting him
* D and Peter would become brothers through adoption; timing and details stay open

After Uncle Ben's Death

* Ben may die later in the story; whether, how and when are undecided
* Afterward, D pursues his own life independently rather than staying constantly with Peter and May
* May and Peter may live together or apart at different times, keeping some of Peter's modest Raimi apartment life
* Ursula Ditkovich, the landlord Mr. Ditkovich's daughter, quietly has a crush on Peter

Peter, Harry and D

* Peter and Harry become especially close in school; Harry shields Peter from bullies (Flash included) and Peter tutors Harry in math and science
* D gets to know both, while the distinct Peter and Harry bond is preserved
* Peter and Harry might room together, as in the Raimi films; whether D lives separately is undecided

Tony Stark and D

* D's Afghanistan delivery for Pepper is a burger
* Tony keeps D's number afterward and asks him for deliveries. Maybe, maybe not
* Tone: Chinese web novel crack humor. Nobody treats the absurd as absurd, D least of all
* The business is not world renowned. It is a joke among people who know D is an eccentric superhero who delivers whatever. Open: how Pepper hears of it

Cave Burger (Idea)

* Pepper places an order for one cheeseburger to Tony Stark, address unknown. She does not expect it to work
* D walks up the mountain to the Ten Rings cave with a paper bag while Tony is building the Mark I. The guards do not stop him
* Raza watches on the camera feed and cannot work out who let a delivery guy in
* Tony is mid-weld with the hammer raised. The burger is still hot
* The order said "and a friend," so there are two. Yinsen asks about fries and there are fries
* Tony signs for it with a soot-black hand. D leaves a punch card: buy nine, get the tenth free
* The guards stop D on the way out to place their own orders. Raza signs for a package of his own
* Yinsen asks who that was. Tony says he thinks it was Pepper's idea of a rescue
* Payoffs: Rhodey searched for months and a courier found Tony in a day but D will not give him the coordinates because of client confidentiality. Tony gets off the plane asking for an American cheeseburger with the punch card in his pocket. Weeks later at 3 a.m. Tony calls: "You delivered to a cave. Can you deliver to Malibu?"
* Open: whether D does anything more for Tony in the cave

MJ and Nick Fury

* Approved: MJ's father is Nick Fury
* Approved: she is Michelle Jones with a personality that mixes Michelle Jones and Mary Jane Watson
* Approved: Harry loves her, the way Raimi's Harry loved Mary Jane
* Source traits, Michelle Jones (Holland films): dry, deadpan and sarcastic. Observant and quietly smart. Reads and sketches people. An activist streak. Acts like she does not care and cares a lot. Guarded about her feelings
* Source traits, Mary Jane Watson (Raimi films and comics): warm, outgoing and charismatic. An aspiring actress in the Raimi films. Lively and fun on the surface while hiding pain underneath. "Face it, Tiger, you just hit the jackpot" in the comics
* Proposed blend: the deadpan wit and sharp eye of Michelle with Mary Jane's warmth and charisma. Guarded with most people and lively with the few she trusts
* Note: the Raimi Mary Jane's abusive father does not apply, since her father is Fury. Her pain could come from his absence instead
* Open: whether she returns Harry's feelings and whether this becomes a triangle with Peter as in the Raimi films
* Open: whether she is the group's age (born about 1985) and at Midtown High
* Open: who her mother is and whether Fury is in her life or mostly absent because of S.H.I.E.L.D.
* Open: whether she knows what her father does
* Comics note: Nick Fury Jr. (Marcus Johnson) is Fury's son in the comics. MJ as his daughter is new to this story
