D Story: Unapproved Ideas

Note: This is my own version of the story and its characters. Marvel and other works are inspirations only. References to canon, Raimi or other sources describe the inspiration and are not fixed facts of this story unless a file marks them as approved.

Suggestions from earlier planning that are not approved yet.

Oscorp Deep Science Division

* Gives D's symbiote experiments a legitimate home and cover story
* First job is cleaning up Norman's leftover formula, lairs and projects
* Mister Negative targets the division, with Devil's Breath as the prize
* D's courier company moves the division's sensitive material
* Electro and Octavius can work there as staff before things go wrong
* If Oscorp wins the Chitauri cleanup contract, the salvage goes straight to the division

Osborns

* Norman ends up in a coma or locked away after D saves him
* Harry running Oscorp gives the group money and labs
* Hobgoblin is the villain who mirrors Harry

Public Heroes (plan for later)

* For now D, Harry, Peter, Gwen and Flash stay masked heroes
* Later the group could go public, as a team backed by D's courier company, Harry's money or the Oscorp deep science division
* Stark went public with "I am Iron Man" (2008) and the Fantastic Four were public by default
* Hybrid option: Harry and Flash as the public faces with D's company as the brand while Peter and Gwen stay masked
* The 2016 Sokovia Accords could force the question
* Risk: Aunt May, Uncle Ben, Captain Stacy and the Parkers become targets

Hobgoblin Origin (to figure out later)

* Idea: Harry commits Norman to a facility when his craziness gets worse
* Idea: a person at the facility injects Norman's blood into himself and becomes Hobgoblin
* Open: who the person is and whether this is the origin

Mister Negative

* Li and Negative are two sides that don't fully know each other
* Norman killed Li's parents in the game version, which gives Li a grudge against Norman and later Harry
* His corrupting touch could turn D's staff or Gwen
* The Inner Demons gang supplies street-level enemies
* Darkforce reacting badly to symbiotes makes him a direct threat to Gwen's suit
* Works as the slow-burn main villain across several arcs

Cloak and Dagger

* Dagger's light can undo Mister Negative's corruption
* Cloak's teleportation makes them ideal delivery staff for D

Polaris

* Hides her powers and green hair
* Juggernaut could be what makes her powers come out
* Magneto's existence and her parentage are an open thread
* Could join D's household or business

Police

* Captain Stacy is the bridge between police and heroes
* Public opinion swings between hero worship and fear

Fantastic Four

* Arcs can open with a news clip of their latest fight with Doom

Arc Structure

1. Business starts and D meets everyone
2. Lizard Peter and Gwen's guilt (Gwen is bitten right before college on the senior trip to Oscorp)
3. Goblin, the shareholders die and Norman survives
4. Cindy's neutralizer and D's symbiote (D's first symbiote interaction comes earlier, before college at ages 14 to 18)
5. Harry takes the mantle and the four form a team
6. Mister Negative steps out as the main villain

Villain-of-the-week stories run alongside all six arcs.
