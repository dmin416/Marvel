D Story: Gwen Stacy Life Projection

Note: This is my own version of the story and its characters. Marvel and other works are inspirations only. References to canon, Raimi or other sources describe the inspiration and are not fixed facts of this story unless a file marks them as approved.

Status: planning ideas only. Nothing here has happened. Gwen has not appeared in the played story yet. The only story canon is D-Story-So-Far.txt.

Already Set in Other Files

* Same age as D, Peter, Harry and Lorna (born about 1985) and at the same school
* Her father is Captain George Stacy of the NYPD. He is described as police chief in planning talk. Open: whether he is a captain who later rises to chief
* Takes self defense classes but does not lift weights
* A drummer. Gets more rebellious over the years, with piercings
* Visual progression from 6 to 21 follows the character sheet in Approved-Suggestions-and-Universal-Details.txt (take it with a grain of salt)
* A version who never changes dimensions but has some of the other Gwens' memories and character growth
* Bitten by an Oscorp super spider on the senior trip to Oscorp, spring or summer 2003. D collects the spider
* Fights Lizard Peter in college and believes she killed him. D saves her (situation TBD)
* Cindy Moon's neutralizer permanently takes her spider powers. As an adult she relies on a managed bond with symbiote D
* Gets together with Peter and becomes a crime-fighting duo with him

The Three Source Gwens

Spider-Gwen (Earth-65 comics and the Spider-Verse films)

* Drummer in a band called the Mary Janes
* Her father, Captain George Stacy, hunts Spider-Woman before learning she is his daughter and backing her
* Peter Parker of her world becomes the Lizard and dies after their fight. Her guilt over his death drives her
* Cindy Moon made the spider that bit her and later took her powers away
* Ballet: this is mostly design, not backstory. Her costume has ballet-slipper feet and the Spider-Verse films animate her fighting like a dancer with spins, flips and poise. The comics never make her a ballet student the way they make her a drummer. Drums are the core of her identity; ballet is how she moves
* Punk look, hood, attitude and humor

Gwen in The Amazing Spider-Man (Emma Stone)

* A science student at Midtown Science High and the top student in her class
* Head intern at Oscorp under Dr. Curt Connors
* Makes the antidote that cures the Lizard
* Valedictorian. Wins a scholarship to Oxford
* Smart, confident and steady. Dies at the clock tower in the second film

Classic comics and Raimi Gwen

* Comics: a science student at Empire State University who dates Peter and dies at the bridge after the Green Goblin throws her
* Raimi: a model and Peter's lab partner in Spider-Man 3

This Story's Blend (Proposed)

* Brains from the Amazing Spider-Man Gwen: strong in science, which is why she is on the Oscorp trip
* Drums, punk style and attitude from Spider-Gwen
* Ballet-style movement as her fighting style once she has powers, from the Spider-Verse films. Idea: she took dance as a small child and quit, so the poise comes back with the powers
* Her rebellion runs against her father's rules and worry, not against school. She keeps her grades up

Life Projection

Childhood (up to 1999)

* Grows up in Queens or Manhattan as a cop's daughter
* 6 to 12: bright and playful, then finding her own style (character sheet)
* Idea: a few years of dance classes as a child
* Her father puts her in self defense early because of his job
* Open: her mother. Helen in the comics and the Webb films. Alive, dead or gone is undecided
* Open: siblings. The Webb films give her younger brothers

High School, 1999 to 2003 (ages 14 to 18)

* 1999 to 2000, freshman (14 to 15): long wavy hair, a black graphic hoodie and cargo pants. Quiet rebellion starts. Top grades in science
* 2000 to 2001 (15 to 16): picks up the drums. First band with friends in a garage or basement. First ear piercings at 16 against her father's wishes
* 2001 to 2002 (16 to 17): edgier look with graphic crop tops, ripped cargos and hair clips. Gigs at small clubs and parties. Fights with her father over curfew and the band. Self defense classes continue
* 2002 to 2003, senior (17 to 18): applies for science programs. Idea: wins an Oscorp internship spot or a science award
* Spring or summer 2003: the senior trip to Oscorp. The super spider bites her. D collects the spider
* After the bite: fever and a rough night, then she wakes changed with rough control

College, 2003 to 2007 (ages 18 to 22)

* 18: the undercut with the top swept over, pink tips, a spider-graphic crop tank and eyebrow and ear piercings (character sheet)
* Learns her powers in secret. Idea: drumming rhythm and dance poise become her fighting style
* Studies science. Open: Empire State University with Peter and Harry, or a scholarship elsewhere
* The band keeps playing. Idea: it takes the name the Mary Janes
* Peter works for Curt Connors and starts down the Lizard path
* Gwen fights Lizard Peter and believes she killed him. Her guilt begins
* D saves Peter and also saves Gwen (situation TBD)
* Her multiversal memories may start surfacing here or later (timing TBD)
* 21: platinum blonde undercut, black tank, dark bomber and layered necklaces. Confident and self-assured

2008 to 2011 (ages 23 to 26)

* Norman's madness. The Goblin fights whoever, Gwen included
* Helps Harry perfect the serum and cure his curse
* The four begin hero work together as masked heroes
* Open: her day job. Lab scientist, grad student, music or something else

2012 to 2014 (ages 27 to 29)

* Battle of New York aftermath and the street-level era
* Mister Negative's corrupting touch is a threat to her

2014 to 2016 (ages 29 to 31)

* Cindy Moon's neutralizer permanently takes her spider powers
* Peter tries to help but Cindy's serum blocks it
* Without powers she has to relearn fighting. In Earth-65 canon she could barely throw a punch without them; here her self defense training gives her a little to stand on
* Relies on a managed bond with symbiote D, which gives her the powers she wants
* Darkforce reacting badly to symbiotes makes Mister Negative a direct threat to her suit
* Her multiversal memories confuse her further

2016 onward (ages 31 and up)

* The Latveria Accords put pressure on her
* Gets together with Peter and becomes a crime-fighting duo with him
* 2018: the Snap. Whether she vanishes is undecided
* 2023: the return

Open Questions

* Where she goes to college and what she studies
* The band's name and members
* Her hero name
* When her multiversal memories start
* How much ballet is part of her past
