## MAJOR CANON RULE: possession is not mastery (December 30–31, 1999)

When the symbiote tries to take D over and cracks his memory seal, its mind is destroyed and its old operating know-how is **wiped, like a BIOS flash**. D keeps the fully integrated symbiote biology and its *potential* but extraordinary techniques must be **learned from scratch** through exploration and practice. D is not instantly an expert.

- **Complete ownership, no resistance:** no competing will. Perfect control means no takeover, not instant mastery.
- **Starting point:** the first night maintains, repairs and optimizes a human body (health and digestion), with **no** superhuman baseline strength, speed or durability.
- **Potential is not skill:** the catalog in the symbiote file is a menu to explore, not a loadout.
- **Analogy:** extraordinary hardware, freshly flashed firmware; the software and muscle memory are rebuilt.

## Shadow and the archive

- **Shadow** is the provisional name for **D's own deeper mind**, acting through his integrated body: his shadow and himself. The symbiote was a blip in the plan; its mind is gone.
- **Archive:** one trillion lifetimes. The December 30 crack exposed a two-dimensional surface layer of **one million** lifetimes. The full three-dimensional cube (a million across, a million deep) unlocks only when D feeds and shapes Tiamut's mind and power.
- **Transmigration pattern:** in each world D recalls fragments on contact with people and places, orients himself, then, if he chooses, lets the memories fade to live as a normal person.
- **Finance versus physical skill:** Shadow applies the archive's knowledge to finance and company stewardship (see the finance master). This gives D **no** instant physical-technique mastery and no speaking companion. Dream appearances of the gray-and-white reflection happen only when the user chooses.

**External software boundary:** A proposed NovaCorp digital research assistant would be written software, not a copy of Shadow or an extra symbiote ability. Future-efficient algorithms might make it possible on early-2000s servers, but no AI system is installed as of March 31, 2000. See the NovaCorp AI and computing development reference (preferred research JARVIS-class window: 2003–2004).

## Learning speed: intuitive and self-paced

Gaining control feels like **finding an unfamiliar muscle or limb**. Within hours or days of trying, D may sense a simple function; within several tries, a basic local action becomes comfortable; coordinated tendrils, clothing mimicry, mobility or defense take days or weeks of practice. Simple tricks are not held back for drama and high-end feats are not skipped to.

## Integrated human–symbiote biology (from December 30–31, 1999)

- **One organism, not host and passenger.** D is completely human in identity, everyday body and reproductive compatibility and extraordinary at the microscopic level. Exact cell structure is not defined (triple-helix DNA is an analogy only).
- **Passive optimization from December 31:** health, cellular maintenance, recovery, metabolism, digestion and everyday attention, learning and recall improve gradually without commands. On January 3, D confirms recall has been improving over several days.
- **First overnight pass:** exceptionally healthy, physically ordinary fifteen-year-old; markedly better digestion and absorption; a famously foul next bowel movement.
- **Food is a real limit:** the symbiote uses nutrients efficiently but cannot create them. D buys extra food. Income: $57/week (Jan–Feb: route + tutoring); **$32/week from the March handoff** (tutoring only).

## Touch-copying: unlocks after D learns DNA manipulation

- **Not active before the late-2002 unlock.** The integrated biology holds the potential but D cannot copy anyone yet. **No contact before the unlock copies anything**, including the Dec 31, 1999 embrace and every 2000 kiss and hand-hold with Lorna.
- **Unlock:** **shortly after Gwen's spider bite** (proposed Oct 17, 2002), D works out how to manipulate DNA (the collected Columbia spider is a natural first subject). From then on he **can copy the genome and abilities of anyone he touches**.
- **Two modes:** copy **by choice** on a given contact, or set a **standing "copy everyone I touch" mode as a precaution**. Which mode he runs at any time is his decision.
- **A copy is potential, not mastery.** Copied abilities still need practice to use.
- **Samples are separate:** deliberately collected material (the spider, Lizard, Goblin, Sandman sources) is research stock, distinct from a touch-copy.
- **Only real contact counts:** being near someone is not a copy. For the five 2005 cosmic-exposed individuals, the author has **established that D does make the necessary post-unlock physical contact and has copied all five by 2008**; exact contact scenes remain to be developed. Other contacts are not presumed.

## March 31, 2000 athletic baseline and training rules (master copy)

**Training is continuous.** From September 1999 D trained calisthenics, running, mobility, balance, low-obstacle climbing, parkour foundations and progressively refined striking, guard and footwork. December 30 did not erase that conditioning. After integration, Shadow's optimization of coordination, tissue maintenance, digestion and nutrient allocation makes practice unusually productive. The March route handoff frees time for training. No gym membership, instructor, match or fight is established.

**Maximum useful nutrient use:** exceptionally high extraction and allocation of nutrients, faster repair and adaptation. He still needs enough calories, water, protein, vitamins and minerals; nothing is created from nothing.

**Training during sleep:** while D sleeps, his biology and Shadow consolidate movement learning, rehearse neuromuscular patterns, tune muscle recruitment, refine proprioception and calibrate low-level control. Genuine skill development on top of waking training, not a substitute for real practice or sparring. Sleep stays restorative; no involuntary movements.

**Performance by March 31:** a notably well-conditioned, athletic fifteen-year-old. Much greater relative strength (strong pull-ups and dips), stamina, acceleration, balance, reaction consistency, recovery and whole-body coordination than in September. Parkour: controlled landings, low vaults, short linked routes, basic climbing, efficient direction changes. Fighting: good stance, guard, footwork, simple combinations and defensive movement, not experienced-fighter judgment. Exceptional body awareness for his age. Brief conscious boosts increasingly reproducible but modest and short.

## 1. Biological premise and governing decisions

On December 30, 1999, the mind and operating knowledge of the *Spider-Man 3*-type symbiote were destroyed. D retained its fully integrated biology and potential, but no native operating technique. Shadow is D's deeper mind, not another person or a surviving symbiote intelligence. It optimizes biological maintenance and consolidation of movements D actually practices. The first night did **not** grant superhuman baseline strength, speed, or durability [E].

| Layer | Meaning | What changes it |
|---|---|---|
| Current output | What D accomplishes now | Control, coordination, reinforcement, current condition |
| Current biological capacity | What his tissues can currently sustain | Adaptation, growth, restructuring, resource availability |
| Native potential | Capabilities and ultimate reach of integrated symbiote mechanisms | D-world premise; not every comics-era symbiote feat |
| Acquired potential | New mechanisms and possible ceilings | Successful future acquisitions |

**Core rule [R]:** Discovering control may grant access to current capacity quickly. Training and physical adaptation can increase current capacity *within* native potential. Acquisitions can add new capabilities or extend potential. Skill alone creates neither material nor a fundamentally absent mechanism.

| Governing decision | Working default |
|---|---|
| Native potential | Superhuman strength, speed, agility and reaction; organic structures; reshaping/disguise; reinforcement/protection; accelerated healing/host preservation as potential; specialized variants not automatic [R] |
| Shadow discovery mode | **A:** D discovers pathways; Shadow refines them. **B [O]:** Shadow searches for adjacent pathways during sleep, bringing active-control dates earlier without automatically changing physiology |
| Expression speed | Immediate activation where existing structures/resources permit; construction, tissue remodeling or additional material take mechanism-dependent time |
| Material | Thin film-style covering can redistribute existing integrated material without special armor-sized reserves; thick armor/constructs need resources, no fixed daily production figure |
| Adhesion | Native adhesion **undecided**; smooth-wall crawling otherwise requires Gwen's later spider powers |
| Regeneration | Everyday recovery [E]; severe regeneration/host preservation unverified potential; missing-part regrowth undecided |

**Potential versus mastery:** Native physical output need not climb along a calendar alone. Shadow's consolidation accelerates practiced actions but cannot replace firsthand experience. Control-pathway discovery: commonly days–weeks; related repetitions: hours–days; coordinated functions: days–weeks; advanced automatic use and situation judgment: weeks–months or longer if practiced rarely. These are rough scenarios, not fixed multipliers.
