D Story: The Hulk, Gamma and Banner's Blood

Note: This is my own version of the story and its characters. Marvel and other works are inspirations only. References to canon, Raimi or other sources describe the inspiration and are not fixed facts of this story unless a file marks them as approved.

Status: reference and design notes. Nothing here is approved for the story. The only story canon is D-Story-So-Far.txt.

Accuracy note: the film details come from Wikipedia and fan wikis rather than the film itself. Sources disagree on exactly how Blonsky received Banner's blood. Strength figures for the Hulk are fan estimates, since no film states one.

Why the Hulk Matters to This Story

* The Hulk is pure biology. No stone, no armor, no magic and no alien metal. A man, a serum and radiation
* That makes him the proof that biology in this world reaches far higher than the super soldier serum does. The serum is five strong men. The Hulk is in the hundreds of tonnes
* So the ceiling in D-Story-Peak-Human.txt is the ceiling for a trained natural body, not the ceiling for biology
* For scale against this story's own numbers: D at 300% spider venom lifts about 51,800 kg. A calm Hulk is usually put above that and an angry one has no stated limit. So D with the spider venom is roughly half a calm Hulk and nowhere near an angry one
* The Hulk is also the warning. Everyone who has chased this has produced a monster

What the Hulk Is

* Bruce Banner, a scientist, was working on a program Ross told him was radiation resistance. It was really an attempt to replicate the super soldier serum
* Banner tested it on himself with a gamma dose. The result was the Hulk
* So the Hulk is the super soldier serum's failure mode: the same goal reached with gamma radiation instead of Erskine's formula and the Vita-Ray chamber
* Where the serum gave Steve Rogers perfection in proportion, gamma gave Banner mass, strength and rage with no control
* Strength scales with rage. There is no fixed number. The angrier he is the stronger he gets, which is why no figure holds
* He cannot be killed by ordinary means. Banner says he tried to shoot himself and the other guy spat the bullet out
* He regenerates from almost anything and ages very slowly
* The cost is the whole point. Banner loses himself. The power is real and the man is gone while it lasts

Banner's Blood

The canon facts

* His blood is the carrier. The gamma is in it and it is transmissible
* A single drop of it fell into a soda bottle at a Brazilian factory. The man who drank that bottle in Milwaukee got gamma sickness, and that is how Ross tracked Banner down. One drop, across the world, through a drink
* Banner mailed blood samples to Samuel Sterns from Brazil under a false name, looking for a cure
* Sterns synthesized those samples into a large supply. He intended to apply its limitless potential to medicine
* Emil Blonsky took a small dose of an altered super soldier serum first. It gave him strength, speed, agility and fast healing. His bones broke against the Hulk and healed
* Sterns warned that the serum and gamma together would make an abomination. Blonsky wanted it anyway
* Blonsky got Banner's gamma blood and became the Abomination. Sources disagree on whether Sterns injected him willingly or under threat
* Sterns himself took gamma blood into an open head wound and started to change

What that establishes

* Gamma is inheritable through blood and needs no equipment to transmit
* It can be synthesized and stockpiled. Sterns did it in a basement lab with 2008-era technology
* Combined with the super soldier serum it produces something worse, not something better
* Every single person who has taken it has lost control of themselves. Banner, Blonsky and Sterns, three for three

What D Can Do With a Sample

Getting one, spring 2010

* D does not wait for a cleanup contract. By 2010 he is twenty-five, has his powers and runs his own company. He takes it
* Option one, steal it. Sterns' lab in New York holds a large synthesized supply and the lab is wrecked by the end of the fight. D walks in during the chaos and leaves with it. Nobody is counting vials while an Abomination is loose in Harlem
* Option two, join the fight. Ross brought the Army into a neighborhood and people are dying in the street. D helps because that is who he is. A sample is what he walks away with. Blood is already everywhere
* Either way he is there in person rather than filing paperwork afterward
* What is physically available: Hulk blood, Abomination blood, Sterns' synthesized supply, serum residue and shredded military hardware
* Helping in the fight is the richer option. It puts him in front of Banner, Ross and Blonsky, it is in character and it means the sample is something he earned rather than took
* Risk of joining in: Ross sees him. The Army files a report on an unidentified enhanced man in Harlem. That is the first time D appears on anyone's radar

What the symbiote can do with it

The symbiote is the ultimate in cellular manipulation and it analyzes biological samples (see D-Story-Symbiotes.txt). Proposed capabilities, in order of difficulty:

* Read it. Understand exactly what gamma did to Banner's cells, which is more than Sterns or Ross ever managed
* Separate it. The whole gamma package is worth having once it can be taken apart. Regeneration, durability and the refusal to die come first. The mass is potential rather than waste and so is the rage. The only part with no value is the loss of self. Pulling the useful from the uncontrolled is the prize and nobody else in this world can do it
* Take the regeneration. The symbiote copies Banner's healing and durability into D without the transformation. A large upgrade with no monster attached

The mass as potential

* Hulk's strength comes largely from mass. Banner is an ordinary-sized man who becomes something several times his own size, so the extra matter has to come from somewhere
* That is not waste, it is a capability: adding mass on demand. The symbiote already adds mass to D, since it is a separate body wrapped around him
* Proposed: the symbiote can do what gamma does, controlled. It builds mass when D wants it and sheds it when he does not. No green, no rage, no loss of himself
* The cost is energy and therefore food, which is already the symbiote's standing cost (see D-Story-Symbiotes.txt)
* Limits worth keeping: adding mass takes time rather than happening in a second, there is a ceiling on how much, and more mass brings more inertia and less agility. He gets to choose between fast and heavy rather than having both

Emotion as the throttle

* Gamma's most interesting mechanic is that Hulk's strength scales with rage. The emotion is the dial
* For Banner that is a curse, because the dial is not in his hands. The angrier he gets the stronger and the less himself he is
* Proposed for D: the symbiote reads his adrenaline already, which is canon symbiote behavior. With the gamma knowledge it can use emotion as a deliberate throttle rather than a leak
* So D can push his output by choosing to let go and pull it back by choosing to be calm. The dial is in his hands because his mind is the thing that beat a symbiote
* This is the clean inversion of Banner. Same mechanism, opposite ownership. Banner's emotion controls his power and D's power answers to his emotion
* Story value: it gives him a reason to feel things hard in a fight and a reason to keep hold of himself, which is a better fight scene than a fixed number
* Limits worth keeping: it is still a throttle on what he has and not a new ceiling, letting go has a cost in control and judgment, and he can misjudge how far he opened the dial
* Build a cure. Banner spends years looking for one. D could have it, which is enormous leverage over Banner, Ross and S.H.I.E.L.D.
* Build a weapon. The same knowledge makes gamma weapons, which is exactly what Ross and Blonsky wanted. D would not, which is a choice worth playing
* Immunize. Make himself, or Peter and Gwen and Harry, immune to gamma exposure

What it probably cannot do

* Reach Hulk's top end. An enraged Hulk has no stated limit and D does. The mass and the emotional throttle raise his ceiling, they do not remove it
* Fix Banner by itself. Banner's problem is as much psychological as cellular while the symbiote works on tissue
* Stay secret if used openly. Anyone who sees gamma-grade regeneration will ask where it came from

The dangers

* Gamma is radioactive. A sample is dangerous to transport, to store and to be near
* One drop poisoned a stranger in Milwaukee. Carrying a vial is carrying a public health incident
* Ross would kill to get it back and would kill whoever had it
* Sterns is a competitor who already has a large synthesized supply and the ambition to use it
* A failed experiment on himself turns D into the thing every other subject became. The symbiote reduces that risk and does not remove it
* If Oscorp or A.I.M. ever got the sample, they would make an Abomination and sell it (see D-Story-AIM.txt)

Where This Sits Among the Enhancement Programs

Ranking what each one gets you, with everything from this story's own files:

* Erskine's super soldier serum: perfection in proportion. Five strong men, ten times robust, four times metabolism and efficiency. Worked once, never reproduced
* Gamma, the Hulk: hundreds of tonnes and near immortality, at the cost of the man. Reproducible through blood and worse every time
* Oscorp's enhancer: Norman's split personality. The same bargain as gamma with a smaller payoff
* The super spider: about 2,000 kg for a single dose, up to about 51,800 kg for D's controlled 300%. No loss of self, which makes it the best of the four
* A.I.M.'s Extremis: regeneration and heat, with unstable subjects exploding
* Dr. Noah Burstein's work at Seagate: unbreakable skin and strength, by accident
* D's symbiote: the only one that can read all of the above and take the parts it wants

The pattern worth writing: every program in this world trades the subject's self for power. The symbiote is the only thing that does not, because D destroyed its mind before it could take his.
