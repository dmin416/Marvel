D, Peter and Harry: Life Timeline

Note: This is my own version of the story and its characters. Marvel and other works are inspirations only. References to canon, Raimi or other sources describe the inspiration and are not fixed facts of this story unless a file marks them as approved.

Status: planning ideas only. Nothing here has happened. The played story stops after lunch at the children's home with D aged fourteen (see D-Story-So-Far.txt).

Dating Assumptions

* Proposed start: 2003, with D, Peter, Harry, Gwen and Lorna all about fourteen
* That puts their birth years around 1988 to 1989 and high school graduation around 2007
* Timeline anchor: they finish college right before 2008, and D has his powers when Tony Stark is abducted in spring 2008
* College length and exact dates still need to be reconciled with a 2007 graduation
* All school years, birthdays and dates remain adjustable

Childhood and High School

* 2003 (age 14, Played): D wakes in a New York children's home and agrees to a Saturday F.E.A.S.T. outing with the home's group to help set up tables
* 2003 onward (14+, Idea): D starts as a normal kid with most of his memories sealed off and trains to survive the world
* 2003 onward (14+, Idea): D may meet Aunt May at F.E.A.S.T.; May gives him an extra plate and invites him to Sunday dinner. Ben and Peter come to treat him as family.
* 2003 to 2005 (14 to 16, Idea): May and Ben, somewhat younger and more financially stable than the Raimi versions, may foster and later adopt D, making D and Peter adoptive brothers
* 2003 to 2007 (14 to 18, Idea): D, Peter, Harry, Gwen and Lorna attend the same school. Flash bullies Peter. Harry shields Peter; Peter tutors Harry in math and science. D gets to know both.
* 14 to 18 (Idea): D interacts with a symbiote before college
* Before 2008 (Idea): Peter works for Curt Connors and the Lizard arc plays out. Uncle Ben gives Peter the talk after Lizard Peter beats guys in the wrestling cage. Gwen fights him and believes she killed him; D saves Peter and also saves Gwen (situation TBD).
* Right before college (about 18, Idea): Gwen is bitten by an Oscorp super spider on the senior trip to Oscorp and gains powers. D collects the spider.
* Lorna is part of the group; her diner job is undecided.

College and Family Changes

* College (ends right before 2008, Idea): D, Peter and Harry attend college. D starts his courier business and has his powers.
* Undecided (Idea): Ben's death, its cause and its timing are undecided.
* After Ben's death (undecided, Idea): D pursues his own life independently. May and Peter may live together or apart.
* College or later (young adult, Idea): Peter might room with Harry, then move into Mr. Ditkovich's building. Ursula Ditkovich, the landlord's daughter, has a crush on Peter.

2008 and the Hero Era

* Spring 2008 (about 19, Idea): Tony Stark is abducted in Afghanistan. D delivers something to him for Pepper Potts.
* 2008 (Idea): Norman's madness follows Peter's work for Curt and the Lizard arc. Norman fights whoever and nearly kills himself; D keeps him alive.
* 2009 to 2011 (20 to 22, Idea): Harry learns what happened to Norman, takes the Goblin mantle and perfects the serum with Peter, Gwen and D. The four begin hero work together.
* 2010 (about 21, Background anchor): Hulk fights Abomination in Harlem, near Tombstone's turf
* May 2012 (about 23, Idea): Battle of New York. D's company wins the Chitauri salvage contract, displacing Toomes and leading to Vulture.
* 2012 to 2014 (about 23 to 25, Idea): Street-level villains and Mister Negative grow more important; Cloak and Dagger are involved
* 2014 to 2016 (about 25 to 27, Idea): Eddie gets the space symbiote; D takes it. It tries to infiltrate D's mind and inadvertently unlocks his memories, which kills its mind and leaves it for D to absorb. Cindy Moon permanently neutralizes Gwen's powers; Gwen later relies on a managed bond with symbiote D.
* 2016 (about 27, Idea): Sokovia Accords put pressure on the group. Gwen and Peter get together and become a crime-fighting duo.
* 2018 (about 29, Background anchor): The Snap; who vanishes is undecided
* 2023 (about 34, Background anchor): The vanished return; ages vary depending on who vanished
