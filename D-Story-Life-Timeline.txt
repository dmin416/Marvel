D, Peter and Harry: Life Timeline

Note: This is my own version of the story and its characters. Marvel and other works are inspirations only. References to canon, Raimi or other sources describe the inspiration and are not fixed facts of this story unless a file marks them as approved.

Status: planning ideas only. Nothing here has happened. The played story stops after lunch at the children's home with D aged fourteen (see D-Story-So-Far.txt).

Dating Assumptions

* Proposed start: 1999, with D, Peter, Harry, Gwen and Lorna all about fourteen (birth years around 1985)
* The start moved from 2003 to 1999 so a four-year college can end right before 2008, as the outline sets
* High school runs about 1999 to 2003 and college about 2003 to 2007
* D has his powers when Tony Stark is abducted in spring 2008, at about 23
* All school years, birthdays and dates remain adjustable

High School (about 1999 to 2003)

* 1999 (age 14, Played): D wakes in a New York children's home and agrees to a Saturday F.E.A.S.T. outing with the home's group to help set up tables
* 1999 onward (14+, Idea): D starts as a normal kid with most of his memories sealed off and trains to survive the world from 14 (already happening in the played story)
* 1999 onward (14+, Idea): D may meet Aunt May at F.E.A.S.T.; May gives him an extra plate and invites him to Sunday dinner. Ben and Peter come to treat him as family.
* 1999 to 2001 (14 to 16, Idea): May and Ben, somewhat younger and more financially stable than the Raimi versions, may foster and later adopt D, making D and Peter adoptive brothers
* 1999 to 2003 (14 to 18, Idea): D, Peter, Harry, Gwen and Lorna attend the same school. Flash bullies Peter. Harry shields Peter; Peter tutors Harry in math and science. D gets to know both.
* December 30, 1999 (15th birthday, Idea): D's one and only symbiote contact comes on his 15th birthday, December 30, 1999. There is only one symbiote. From then on it maximizes his nutrition, recovery and gains. Its attempt to infiltrate his mind unlocks his memories
* Spring or summer 2003 (about 18, Idea): Gwen is bitten by an Oscorp super spider on the senior trip to Oscorp and gains powers. D collects the spider.
* Lorna is part of the group; her diner job is undecided.

College (about 2003 to 2007)

* 2003 to 2007 (18 to 22, Idea): D, Peter and Harry attend college and finish right before 2008. D starts his courier business and has his powers.
* During college (Idea): Peter works for Curt Connors and the Lizard arc plays out. Uncle Ben gives Peter the talk after Lizard Peter beats guys in the wrestling cage. Gwen fights him and believes she killed him; D saves Peter and also saves Gwen (situation TBD).
* Undecided (Idea): Ben's death, its cause and its timing
* After Ben's death (undecided, Idea): D pursues his own life independently. May and Peter may live together or apart.
* College or later (young adult, Idea): Peter might room with Harry, then move into Mr. Ditkovich's building. Ursula Ditkovich, the landlord's daughter, has a crush on Peter.

2008 and the Hero Era

* Spring 2008 (about 23, Idea): Tony Stark is abducted in Afghanistan. D delivers something to him for Pepper Potts.
* 2008 (about 23, Idea): Norman's madness follows Peter's work for Curt and the Lizard arc. Norman fights whoever and nearly kills himself; D keeps him alive.
* 2009 to 2011 (about 24 to 26, Idea): Harry learns what happened to Norman, takes the Goblin mantle and perfects the serum with Peter, Gwen and D. The four begin hero work together.
* 2010 (about 25, Background anchor): Hulk fights Abomination in Harlem, near Tombstone's turf
* May 2012 (about 27, Idea): Battle of New York. D's company wins the Chitauri salvage contract, displacing Toomes and leading to Vulture.
* 2012 to 2014 (about 27 to 29, Idea): Street-level villains and Mister Negative grow more important; Cloak and Dagger are involved
* 2014 to 2016 (about 29 to 31, Idea): Eddie gets the space symbiote; D takes it, experiments on it and absorbs it after its attempt to infiltrate his mind kills its own mind. Cindy Moon permanently neutralizes Gwen's powers; Gwen later relies on a managed bond with symbiote D.
* 2016 (about 31, Idea): Sokovia Accords put pressure on the group. Gwen and Peter get together and become a crime-fighting duo.
* 2018 (about 33, Background anchor): The Snap; who vanishes is undecided
* 2023 (about 38, Background anchor): The vanished return; ages vary depending on who vanished
