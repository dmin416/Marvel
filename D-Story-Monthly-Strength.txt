D Story: Monthly Strength

Note: This is my own version of the story and its characters. Marvel and other works are inspirations only. References to canon, Raimi or other sources describe the inspiration and are not fixed facts of this story unless a file marks them as approved.

Status: design model. This file is the source for D's base body strength at any month from 14 to 35. It replaces the trained track in D-Story-Strength-Over-Time.txt.

How to Use

* Base lift is D's one rep floor lift (deadlift) in kilograms with his own body and no powers. It is a full maximum effort lift done on purpose
* Easy lift is 60% of the base lift. D can lift and carry it without strain and repeat it many times
* Emergency lift is the adrenaline lift: a mother lifting a car off her child. It is the base lift x 1.30 at 14, falling to x 1.15 by 22 and staying there. Training teaches the body to use more of its muscle on purpose, so a trained D has less hidden strength left for an emergency
* An emergency lift is a one-time burst of a few seconds. It can tear muscle or tendon and leaves him wrecked afterward
* Powers are a formula on top of the base: venom lift = base lift x multiplier (x50 for a single dose, x148 at 300% efficacy, see D-Story-Spider-Abilities.txt)
* Find the month, read the lift you need, apply the multiplier. Powers multiply easy and emergency lifts the same way

Assumptions

* Month 1 is September 1999 at age 14 years 0 months. If the real start month differs, shift the dates and keep the ages
* D starts undernourished at 38 kg body mass and 25 kg lift
* D trains from 14. The played story already shows him working out in the yard (pull-ups, dips, push-ups, sit-ups and laps)
* Food is poor for the first 3 months, improving to month 9 and steady after that
* Exercise is the weekly schedule listed on each line

Formula

* Each month: gain = 0.075 x training load x food factor x (ceiling for his age - current lift)
* Training load = weekly training hours / 5, capped at 1.25
* Food factor = 0.55 poor, 0.8 improving, 1.0 steady
* Ceiling for his age = 180 kg x maturity x aging. Maturity rises from 30% at 14 to 100% at 24. Aging takes 0.6% per year off after 30
* 180 kg is his adult trained ceiling after the small permanent cost of his undernourished start
* If training drops, lift falls 8% of the gap per month toward the lower ceiling

Exercise Phases

* 14y 0m to 14y 5m: 4 x 45 min per week of bodyweight work in the yard
* 14y 6m to 15y 11m: 5 x 50 min of bodyweight circuits, running and loaded carries
* 16 to 17: 3 barbell sessions in the school weight room and 2 conditioning sessions, 55 min each
* 18 to 21 (college): 4 strength and 2 conditioning sessions, 65 min each
* 22 to 29: 4 strength and 2 conditioning sessions, 60 min each, plus courier work
* 30 to 35: 3 strength and 2 conditioning sessions, 60 min each, plus courier work
