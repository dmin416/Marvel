## 7. End harmful PFAS/PTFE chains

**From authorization**, D uses Shadow's knowledge to stop avoidable poisoning, retool or close dangerous manufacturing, pursue responsible actors **at every level**, provide restitution and restore ecosystems. The mandate also covers proven grave wrongdoing in oil, gas, coal, mining, toxic waste, drinking water, pesticides, dumping and environmental corruption. Employment or shareholding alone is not a crime.

**Chemistry:** PTFE is a fluoropolymer within broad PFAS definitions; **PFOA and PFOS are different substances**. Target hazardous substances, emissions and concealment on evidence; do not eliminate lifesaving essential uses before tested alternatives exist.

| Workstream | What changes |
|---|---|
| **Identify wrongdoing** | Ranked dossiers: who knew what and when, who authorized what, releases, harm, where proof resides; exculpatory facts included. |
| **Reconstruct evidence** | Faithful memory reconstructions labeled by origin; originals obtained where helpful. |
| **Stop exposure** | Filtered water, safe supply, screening, food warnings, containment in the first days and weeks. |
| **End releases** | Emergency injunctions, discharge limits, capture, lawful receivership where justified. |
| **Personal accountability** | Directors, executives, managers, contractors, haulers, complicit financiers and officials who criminally participated. |
| **Legal and economic pressure** | Prospective ecocide and concealment rules; support for prosecutors and victims' counsel; restitution through judgments. |
| **Replace PFAS markets** | Validated alternatives for coatings, packaging, textiles, foam, plumbing, components; retrain uninvolved workers. |
| **Remediation** | Map sources; carbon, ion exchange or RO; isolate hotspots; restore groundwater and habitat. |

**Illustrative alternatives:** stainless steel, cast iron, ceramics, PEEK, UHMWPE. Do not swap one hazard for another. References: [1999 Tennant lawsuit](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/tort_trial_insurance_practice/resources/brief/2023-summer/survey-trends-pfas-regulation-litigation/); [EPA PFAS risk management](https://www.epa.gov/assessing-and-managing-chemicals-under-tsca/risk-management-and-polyfluoroalkyl-substances-pfas).

### D's legal endgame

- **From authorization:** target-specific knowledge and prosecutorial roadmaps ready; NovaCorp funds public protection, claims, inspectors, forensics and victim support.
- **Every level:** no executive immunity, no collective guilt; civil restitution, disqualification, forfeiture and prosecution in parallel. Damages go to victims and restoration.
- **Reform speed:** forward-looking mass-poisoning/ecocide and cross-border crime laws, disclosure rules, accelerated fair procedures and rules for verified superhuman reconstructions.
- **Sentencing:** an environmental-terrorism category for defined conduct, applied prospectively; strongest sentences the proven offense permits. Public agricultural and habitat-restoration prison programs only with correctional, constitutional and labor protections, never for NovaCorp profit.
- **Proof:** truthful reconstructions plus records, measurements and witnesses. A trial establishes guilt.

**Binding strategic direction, not a claim that all contracts or technologies have already launched:** NovaCorp seeks secure, sufficient, traceable and *fairly priced* raw materials for its phones, electronics, batteries, vehicles, robotics, energy systems, construction and other businesses. **Australia is the preferred early cornerstone supplier** wherever its available mineral grades, processing capacity, prices, law and logistics make it suitable. NovaCorp also buys elsewhere when Australia cannot supply a necessary ingredient or a second origin is prudent; Australia does **not** possess a complete substitute for all advanced semiconductor, specialty chemical and manufacturing inputs. Prioritize recycling and efficient product design before opening unnecessary new extraction. Shadow can manage ordinary authorized corporate procurement as part of existing operations; D's larger environmental, mining-ownership, space and new-energy initiatives still require their appropriate in-story authorization and executable agreements.

### 1. Earth-side sourcing and fair terms

| Supply requirement | Practical Australian sourcing focus | Use and constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Iron ore and processed steel | Iron ore producers, steelmaking and downstream partners as capacity permits | Industrial machinery, vehicles, buildings; buying ore does not provide ready-to-use alloy or sheet steel |
| Bauxite, alumina and aluminum | Australian bauxite/alumina and smelter supply | Vehicle structures, power and electronics; high electricity requirement of refining |
| Copper and conductive metals | Copper mines, concentrators and refiners; supplementary gold, silver and zinc where appropriate | Wiring, motors, contacts, conductors and corrosion protection; match industrial purity and form |
| Nickel, manganese, lithium, cobalt and graphite | Purchase actual available feedstocks with suitable chemistry and refining partners; expand supply as legitimate Australian capacity develops | Batteries, alloys and energy storage; **do not project 2020s lithium/rare-earth production volumes backward onto 2004** |
| Mineral sands, rare earths and other specialty materials | Ilmenite/rutile/zircon; rare-earth supply only once mines, separation and processing really exist | Titanium inputs, ceramics, optics, magnets and specialty manufacturing; downstream separation and export controls are distinct bottlenecks |

**Fair price** means a transparently negotiated *delivered cost*, not artificially suppressed wages or a fixed bargain price. Structure optional multiyear purchase/offtake agreements around independently observable commodity benchmarks, tested grade/purity, processing yield, credible operating and capital costs, price review mechanisms and reasonable margins. Include predictable minimum-income protections where needed without promising to buy unusable ore or masking subsidies; pay legitimate land-access fees, royalties and taxes; respect Indigenous land rights, consultation, cultural heritage, informed engagement, lawful permits, worker representation and verifiable wages, health and safety. Budget closure bonds, rehabilitation, water and tailings management, pollution liability and independent audits. Encourage competition and multiple qualified suppliers, not an exclusive monopoly over Australian resources. The same standards apply when NovaCorp controls a mine or refiner.

**Actual physical chain:** product bill of materials and forecast → mine/concentrator/refinery contracts and assay → rail/road to an Australian port → scheduled ocean freight and insurance → destination port, customs and duties → domestic transport → refinery/semiconductor-grade processing or component plant → quality inspection, inventory buffers, manufacturing and recycling. Some refining may be better located in Australia; other specialty processing requires qualified facilities elsewhere. Maintain lot-level traceability, alternate freight routes and supplier redundancy. Track separately ore tonnage, recovered metal, purified product, yield losses, unit cost and delivery times. A supply contract does not automatically imply owning the mine, shipping company or product factory.

### 3. Asteroid sourcing — later, contingent infrastructure

**Selected long-term direction:** NovaCorp advances from orbital reconnaissance/remote sensing to robotic sampling, prospecting, resource characterization, a lawful extraction/processing capability and eventually useful deliveries **only when the system can actually operate and pass cost/safety tests**. Asteroids vary greatly: never assume every rock is rich in platinum, iron, water or any chosen element. In-space demand comes first: water and other volatiles for orbital systems, plus metals for fabrication, may avoid the high cost of returning bulk rock to Earth. On Earth, substitute asteroid-derived material for Australian supply only if refined usable output survives the full cost and environmental comparison. Do not assign existing Earth-market bulk-metal prices to an unprocessed asteroid in orbit.

The supply chain is **identify orbit/composition → mission access and permissions → assess reserves and usable recovery fraction → extract/sort/process in space → certify purity and contain debris → (a) use in orbit or (b) safely return a controlled shipment → customs, refinery, transport and factory**. Include spacecraft manufacture, launch, propulsion, travel time, crew/robot maintenance, energy, losses, insurance, landing safety, environmental impact, orbital-debris prevention, property/resource-law review and independent oversight. No automatic towing whole asteroids into Earth orbit, uncontrolled reentry or free delivery; no asteroid has been mined in the played April 2004 story. This is a *later* complement to Earth sourcing, not an excuse to strand Australian suppliers and workers without a transition.
