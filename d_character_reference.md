## D's six classic designs and paired-vehicle garage — binding 2008 continuity

The two versions share an outwardly authentic classic shape but serve deliberately different purposes:

1. **Historically faithful, gently improved car:** Preserve the original mechanical character, sound, styling, cabin experience and period identity. Perform sympathetic, preferably reversible upgrades to reliability, braking, tires, cooling, lighting, electrical safety, seat restraints and serviceability. This is **not** a radical restomod or modern drivetrain replacement.
2. **NovaCorp technological twin, classic appearance:** Re-create the same recognizable, period-accurate exterior over **the most capable complete vehicle D and NovaCorp can actually engineer at that point in the story**: purpose-designed structure, propulsion, energy storage, chassis, brakes, occupant protection, electronics and discreet driver assistance. The underlying car can be radically modern; the visible vintage shape remains its hallmark. Its equipment evolves with NovaCorp's genuine breakthroughs rather than automatically inheriting all theoretical future technologies.
