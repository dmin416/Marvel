# MCU Villain Timeline

Year-by-year record of when villains appear and how they are defeated. Dates follow Marvel's 2023 official timeline book (The Marvel Cinematic Universe: An Official Timeline). The book covers Phases One to Four; later films use the placements on Wikipedia's MCU timeline. Films only. Eternals and The Marvels are excluded.

**Key**
- **Appears:** First active as a villain
- **Defeated:** Killed, imprisoned, cured or otherwise taken out of play
- **At large:** Survives and remains a threat

---

## Before 2008

### 1943 to 1945 (Captain America: The First Avenger)
- **Appears:** Red Skull, Arnim Zola, Hydra
- **Defeated:**
  - Red Skull: transported to Vormir by the Tesseract; bound there as the Soul Stone's guardian
  - Arnim Zola: captured by the Allies
- **At large:** Hydra survives; Zola is recruited into the early S.H.I.E.L.D. and rebuilds Hydra inside it

### 1989 to 1995 (Captain Marvel)
- **Appears:** Yon-Rogg, the Supreme Intelligence, Ronan the Accuser
- **Defeated:**
  - Yon-Rogg: beaten by Carol and sent back to Hala with a warning
- **At large:** Supreme Intelligence, Ronan, the Kree Empire

### Background events
- **1988:** Ego's tumor kills Meredith Quill; Yondu's Ravagers take Peter Quill
- **1991:** The Winter Soldier kills Howard and Maria Stark
- **1992:** Ulysses Klaue steals vibranium from Wakanda
- **1999:** Aldrich Killian is snubbed by Tony in Bern

## Spring 2008 (Iron Man)
- **Appears:** Obadiah Stane, Raza and his Ten Rings cell
- **Defeated:**
  - Raza: killed by Stane's men after Stane takes the Mark I parts
  - Obadiah Stane: killed as Iron Monger when the arc reactor overloads
- **At large:** Wenwu and the main Ten Rings organization

## Spring 2010 (Iron Man 2, The Incredible Hulk, Thor)
- **Appears:** Ivan Vanko, Justin Hammer, Emil Blonsky, Samuel Sterns, Loki, Laufey, the Destroyer
- **Defeated:**
  - Ivan Vanko: dies in his own self-destruct
  - Justin Hammer: arrested
  - Emil Blonsky: defeated as the Abomination and imprisoned
  - Laufey: killed by Loki
  - The Destroyer: destroyed by Thor
  - Loki: falls into the void after the Bifrost breaks
- **At large:** Samuel Sterns (Banner's blood begins mutating him)

## Spring 2012 (The Avengers)
- **Appears:** The Chitauri, The Other, Thanos (revealed)
- **Defeated:**
  - Chitauri army: destroyed when the mothership is nuked
  - Loki: captured and imprisoned on Asgard
- **At large:** Thanos, The Other

## Fall 2013 (Thor: The Dark World)
- **Appears:** Malekith, Kurse (Algrim), the Dark Elves
- **Defeated:**
  - Kurse: killed by Loki's grenade
  - Malekith: crushed by his own ship
- **At large:** Loki fakes his death and secretly rules Asgard disguised as Odin; the Aether (Reality Stone) goes to the Collector

## December 2013 to Early 2014 (Iron Man 3)
- **Appears:** Aldrich Killian, Maya Hansen, Eric Savin, the Extremis soldiers
- **Defeated:**
  - Maya Hansen: killed by Killian
  - Eric Savin: killed by Tony
  - Aldrich Killian: killed by Pepper
  - Trevor Slattery: arrested

## 2014 (Captain America: The Winter Soldier, Guardians of the Galaxy, Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2)
- **Appears:** Alexander Pierce, Jasper Sitwell, Brock Rumlow, Batroc, Korath, Nebula, the Collector, Ego, Ayesha and the Sovereign, Taserface
- **Defeated:**
  - Arnim Zola: his Camp Lehigh computer is destroyed
  - Jasper Sitwell: killed by the Winter Soldier
  - Alexander Pierce: killed by Fury
  - Hydra: exposed and S.H.I.E.L.D. collapses
  - The Other: killed by Ronan
  - Korath: killed by Drax
  - Ronan: destroyed by the Power Stone
  - Taserface: killed when his ship is destroyed
  - Ego: killed when Quill destroys his core
  - Yondu: dies saving Quill
- **At large:**
  - Brock Rumlow: survives with severe burns
  - Winter Soldier: escapes and begins recovering his memories
  - Nebula: escapes and later turns ally
  - Ayesha: begins creating Adam Warlock
  - Baron Strucker: holds Loki's scepter in Sokovia

## 2015 (Avengers: Age of Ultron, Ant-Man)
- **Appears:** Ultron, Baron Strucker, Darren Cross (Yellowjacket)
- **Defeated:**
  - Baron Strucker: captured, then killed by Ultron
  - Ultron: destroyed by Vision
  - Darren Cross: shrunk into the Quantum Realm
- **At large:**
  - Ulysses Klaue: loses his arm to Ultron and escapes
  - Mitchell Carson: escapes with Yellowjacket tech

## 2016 (Civil War, Black Widow, Black Panther, Spider-Man: Homecoming)
- **Appears:** Helmut Zemo, Crossbones (Rumlow), Dreykov, Taskmaster, Killmonger, Ulysses Klaue, Adrian Toomes (Vulture), Herman Schultz (Shocker), Mac Gargan
- **Defeated:**
  - Crossbones: killed by his own bomb in Lagos
  - Helmut Zemo: captured and imprisoned
  - Dreykov: killed in the Red Room's destruction
  - Taskmaster: freed from Red Room mind control
  - Ulysses Klaue: killed by Killmonger
  - Killmonger: chooses death after losing to T'Challa
  - Vulture: arrested
  - Shocker: arrested
- **At large:**
  - Mac Gargan: imprisoned, later the Scorpion
  - General Ross: Secretary of State and an antagonist to the rogue Avengers

## 2016 to 2017 (Doctor Strange)
- **Appears:** Kaecilius, Dormammu
- **Defeated:**
  - Kaecilius: dragged into the Dark Dimension
  - Dormammu: bargained into leaving Earth alone
- **At large:**
  - Dormammu: still rules the Dark Dimension
  - Mordo: turns against sorcerers

## 2017 (Thor: Ragnarok)
- **Appears:** Hela, Skurge, Fenris, Surtur, the Grandmaster
- **Defeated:**
  - Skurge: killed defending the refugees
  - Fenris: dropped into the sea
  - Hela: destroyed with Asgard by Surtur
  - The Grandmaster: overthrown on Sakaar
- **At large:** Surtur, the Grandmaster

## 2018 (Ant-Man and the Wasp, Avengers: Infinity War)
- **Appears:** Ghost (Ava Starr), Sonny Burch, the Black Order
- **Defeated:**
  - Sonny Burch: arrested
  - Ghost: partially cured and reformed
  - Ebony Maw: blown out of his ship by Tony
  - Proxima Midnight: killed by Wanda in Wakanda
  - Cull Obsidian: killed by Bruce in the Hulkbuster
  - Corvus Glaive: killed by Vision
  - Loki: killed by Thanos (2012 variant survives elsewhere)
- **At large:** Thanos wins and erases half of all life

## 2018 to 2023 (Avengers: Endgame)
- **Defeated:**
  - 2018 Thanos: beheaded by Thor
  - 2014 Nebula: killed by present-day Nebula
  - 2014 Thanos, the 2014 Black Order and their army: erased by Tony's snap
- **At large:**
  - 2012 Loki: escapes with the Tesseract and is captured by the TVA
  - 2014 Gamora: missing in the present timeline
  - Red Skull: status after Natasha's sacrifice not shown

## 2024 (Shang-Chi, Spider-Man: Far From Home, Spider-Man: No Way Home, Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness)
- **Appears:** Wenwu, Death Dealer, Razor Fist, the Dweller-in-Darkness, Mysterio, the multiversal villains (Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus, Electro, Sandman, Lizard), Scarlet Witch, Gargantos
- **Defeated:**
  - Death Dealer: killed by Wenwu
  - Wenwu: killed by the Dweller-in-Darkness
  - Dweller-in-Darkness: killed by Shang-Chi and the Great Protector
  - Mysterio: killed during his drone attack
  - Multiversal villains: cured and sent home
  - Gargantos: killed by Wanda and Strange
  - Earth-838 Illuminati: killed by Wanda
  - Scarlet Witch: apparently dies destroying the Darkhold
- **At large:**
  - Ten Rings: taken over by Xialing; Razor Fist joins her
  - Abomination: free, fighting in an underground club with Wong
  - Peter's identity: exposed by Mysterio, then erased from the world's memory

## 2024, Earth-10005 and the Void (Deadpool & Wolverine)
- **Appears:** Mr. Paradox, Cassandra Nova, the Void mutants
- **Defeated:**
  - Cassandra Nova: killed by the Time Ripper
  - Mr. Paradox: arrested by the TVA

## 2025 (Thor: Love and Thunder, Black Panther: Wakanda Forever)
- **Appears:** Gorr the God Butcher, Zeus, Namor, Valentina Allegra de Fontaine
- **Defeated:**
  - Gorr: dies after choosing to resurrect his daughter
  - Namor: reaches a truce with Wakanda
- **At large:**
  - Zeus: survives and sends Hercules after Thor
  - Namor: rules Talokan
  - Valentina: an active CIA operative

## 2026 (Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania, Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3)
- **Appears:** Kang the Conqueror, MODOK, The High Evolutionary, Adam Warlock
- **Defeated:**
  - MODOK: revealed as Darren Cross and killed
  - Kang: defeated in the Quantum Realm
  - High Evolutionary: defeated and left alive
  - Adam Warlock: turns ally
- **At large:**
  - Ayesha and the Sovereign
  - Council of Kangs: storyline later dropped from the saga

## 2027 (Captain America: Brave New World, Thunderbolts*)
- **Appears:** Samuel Sterns (The Leader), Red Hulk, Sidewinder and the Serpent Society, Bob (Sentry / The Void)
- **Defeated:**
  - The Leader: imprisoned in the Raft
  - Red Hulk: Ross turns himself in to the Raft
  - Sidewinder: captured
  - Taskmaster: killed in Valentina's vault trap
  - The Void: contained when Bob is pulled back by the team
- **At large:** Valentina rebrands the Thunderbolts as the "New Avengers"

## Earth-828, 1964 (The Fantastic Four: First Steps)
- **Appears:** Galactus, the Silver Surfer, Doctor Doom
- **Defeated:**
  - Galactus: defeated and driven off
  - Silver Surfer: turns against Galactus
- **At large:** Doctor Doom (mid-credits)

## 2028 (Spider-Man: Brand New Day)
- **Appears:** Scorpion (Mac Gargan), Tombstone, Boomerang, Tarantula, the Hand, Department of Damage Control director Bill Metzger, Jean Grey
- **Defeated:**
  - Street-level villains: beaten by Spider-Man over four years
  - Jean Grey: stopped after her powers explode across Manhattan; she keeps Peter alive after he is shot
- **At large:** Department of Damage Control's experiments are exposed in the climax
