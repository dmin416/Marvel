# MCU Alternative Technology Timeline: June 1943 to Spring 2008

Runs from the 1943 Stark Expo to Tony Stark's capture in Afghanistan. Marvel's 2023 official timeline book places that capture in spring 2008. Marvel's older 2018 timeline used 2010.

---

## 1943 to 1945: World War II

- **June 1943:** Howard's Stark Expo hovercar prototype hovers briefly, then fails mid-demo (*Captain America: The First Avenger*)
- **1943, Project Rebirth:** Erskine's Super Soldier Serum plus the Vita-Ray chamber, run off Brooklyn's power grid with Howard on the reactor (*Captain America: The First Avenger*)
- **1943, Stark R&D:** carbon-polymer gear, a shield wired with electrical relays and the vibranium prototype. The vibranium shield is stronger than steel at a third of the weight and absorbs all vibration (*Captain America: The First Avenger*)
- **1943:** a Stark homing transponder Steve carries into the Azzano rescue (*Captain America: The First Avenger*)
- **1943 to 1945, Hydra's Tesseract arsenal** (*Captain America: The First Avenger*):
  - Zola's machine for harvesting the Tesseract's energy
  - blue-bolt rifles, pistols and cannons that vaporize targets
  - Hydra tanks and Lindworm-5 motorcycles
  - Schmidt's Focke-Wulf Triebflügel aircraft
  - a minisub
  - Schmidt's Cadillac with a red boost button
- **1945, Valkyrie:** Schmidt's flying-wing super-bomber, loaded with weapons of mass destruction bound for the U.S. East Coast. Steve crashes it in the Arctic (*Captain America: The First Avenger*)
- **1945:** Zola's experiments on captured soldiers let Bucky survive his fall from the train (*Captain America: The Winter Soldier*)
- **Late war, Midnight Oil:** Stark's stamina gas for soldiers that instead drove them to psychotic violence. It caused the massacre at the Battle of Finow (*Agent Carter*)
- **1945, SSR raid in Austria:** Hydra weaponry seized along with the Obelisk and a blue Kree corpse. The corpse later supplies the GH-325 drug behind Project T.A.H.I.T.I. (*Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.*)
- **1945 or later:** Howard recovers the Tesseract from the ocean floor with a bathysphere (*Captain America: The First Avenger*)
- **Postwar:** Operation Paperclip brings Zola into S.H.I.E.L.D., where he rebuilds Hydra from the inside (*Captain America: The Winter Soldier*)
- **Postwar, Winter Soldier program:** Bucky gets a metal arm with superhuman strength, repeated memory wipes, trigger words and cryosleep between missions (*Captain America: The Winter Soldier*, *Captain America: Civil War*)

## 1946 to 1947: Agent Carter Era

- **1946, Zodiac:** Peggy recovers the Zodiac serum. Howard names her co-head of the newly created S.H.I.E.L.D. (*Agent Carter* One-Shot)
- **1946, Nitramene:** Stark's molecular formula, weaponized into bombs with enormous blast power (*Agent Carter*)
- **1946, Blitzkrieg Button:** billed as a citywide blackout device, it actually holds a vial of Steve Rogers' blood (*Agent Carter*)
- **1946:** Leviathan's typewriter receives encoded orders. Stark built an experimental explosive vest that cannot be switched off (*Agent Carter*)
- **1946, Red Room:** the Soviet program training girls as sleeper agents, including Dottie Underwood (*Agent Carter*)
- **Arc reactor:** a young Anton Vanko works with Howard in 1946. The pair co-invent the arc reactor (year not given). Howard later has Anton deported for trying to sell it (*Agent Carter*, *Iron Man 2*)
- **1947, Zero Matter (Darkforce):** Isodyne Energy's research yields a substance that freezes, absorbs and shatters people. An atomic test tears open a dimensional rift (*Agent Carter*)
- **1947, Zero Matter devices:** Wilkes' containment chamber, a Zero Matter extraction device, Frost's rift machine and Stark's gamma cannon that seals the rift (*Agent Carter*)
- **1947:** Howard's hovercar finally works and helps close the Zero Matter portal (*Agent Carter*, "Hollywood Ending")

## 1950s to 1970s

- **1951:** Isaiah Bradley, a Black Korean War super soldier, wrecks half the Winter Soldier's metal arm in Goyang. The U.S. government and Hydra then imprison him and experiment on him for 30 years (*The Falcon and the Winter Soldier*)
- **1954:** the first official Stark Expo shows off the tech Howard used to defeat global tyranny (*Iron Man 2* marketing lore)
- **1964:** a Stark Expo themed "Better Living Through Technology" (*Iron Man 2* lore)
- **1970, Camp Lehigh:** S.H.I.E.L.D. stores the Tesseract at this New Jersey base. Hank Pym keeps his Pym Particles there (*Avengers: Endgame*)
- **1972:** a terminally ill Zola uploads his mind into 200,000 feet of data banks beneath Camp Lehigh (*Captain America: The Winter Soldier*)
- **1974:** Howard's last Stark Expo. Its City of the Future model encodes a new element he couldn't produce with the tech of his time (*Iron Man 2*)
- **Through 1987:** Pym's Ant-Man suit shrinks the wearer while boosting strength. His control tech commands ants. Janet fights beside him as the Wasp (*Ant-Man*)
- **Undated:** Project Goliath, Pym's growth research with his assistant Bill Foster (*Ant-Man and the Wasp*)

## 1980s

- **1980:** Ego plants a xenoforming seedling on Earth during his Missouri romance with Meredith Quill (*Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2*)
- **1987:** Janet shrinks to subatomic size to disable a Soviet nuclear missile and is lost in the Quantum Realm (*Ant-Man*)
- **1988:** a Ravager ship abducts young Peter Quill from Missouri (*Guardians of the Galaxy*)
- **1989:** Pym catches S.H.I.E.L.D. trying to copy his shrinking tech. He quits in front of Howard and Peggy and hides the formula (*Ant-Man*)
- **1989, Project PEGASUS:** Dr. Wendy Lawson (Mar-Vell) tests a light-speed engine powered by the Tesseract. Carol Danvers absorbs its energy when she destroys it in the crash (*Captain Marvel*)
- **Undated:** Elihas Starr, a former S.H.I.E.L.D. scientist, runs his own Quantum Realm experiment. The accident kills him and leaves his daughter Ava phasing from "molecular disequilibrium." S.H.I.E.L.D. then trains her as an operative (*Ant-Man and the Wasp*)

## 1990s

- **1991:** the Winter Soldier ambushes the Starks' car to seize a case of super-soldier serum. Hydra's Siberian base keeps more Winter Soldiers in cryostasis (*Captain America: Civil War*)
- **1992:** Klaue steals vibranium from Wakanda with N'Jobu's help (*Black Panther*)
- **Wakanda (centuries old):** vibranium tech hidden behind the country's isolationism, including the Panther suit, Kimoyo beads, the heart-shaped herb and maglev ore trains (*Black Panther*)
- **1995, the Kree-Skrull war reaches Earth** (*Captain Marvel*):
  - Skrull shapeshifting and memory probes
  - a Kree implant that suppresses Carol's powers
  - a Kree bombing force under Ronan
  - Mar-Vell's cloaked orbital lab hiding the Tesseract
- **1995:** Fury's pager becomes an emergency beacon to Carol. Goose the Flerken swallows the Tesseract, then coughs it up onto Fury's desk (*Captain Marvel*)
- **1995:** Soviet super soldier Alexei Shostakov (Red Guardian) and the Widow Melina Vostokoff steal S.H.I.E.L.D. intel while posing as an Ohio family. The Red Room takes Natasha and Yelena (*Black Widow*)
- **1999:** Maya Hansen's Extremis, an experimental regenerative treatment. Aldrich Killian tries to recruit her and Tony for A.I.M. at a New Year's Eve party in Bern (*Iron Man 3*)

## 2000s

- **About 2005:** Ross tries to revive the WWII super-soldier program. Banner's gamma experiment at Culver University turns him into the Hulk (*The Incredible Hulk*, set five years later in 2010)
- **By 2008:** a full-size arc reactor powers the Stark Industries plant. Tony's home runs on his AI J.A.R.V.I.S. along with his workshop robot arms (*Iron Man*)
- **Spring 2008:** Tony demos the Jericho missile in Afghanistan. Attackers hit his convoy with Stark missiles. In captivity an electromagnet keeps shrapnel from his heart until he builds a miniature arc reactor and the prototype armor (*Iron Man*)
