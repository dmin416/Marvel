# MCU Timeline: Movies in In-Universe Order

Dates follow Marvel's 2023 official timeline book (The Marvel Cinematic Universe: An Official Timeline). Approximate where the films leave the year loose.

---

## The Infinity Saga

### 1. Captain America: The First Avenger
**In-universe:** June 1943 to March 1945 (framing scene in 2011)
- Steve Rogers receives the Super Soldier Serum; Dr. Erskine is assassinated
- Steve rescues the 107th and forms the Howling Commandos
- Bucky Barnes falls from a Hydra train and is presumed dead
- Red Skull touches the Tesseract (Space Stone) and is transported to Vormir
- Steve crashes the Valkyrie into the Arctic; Howard Stark recovers the Tesseract
- Steve wakes in 2011 after nearly 70 years frozen

### 2. Captain Marvel
**In-universe:** Summer 1995 (flashbacks to 1989)
- Carol Danvers gains powers in 1989 from an explosion of Mar-Vell's Tesseract-powered light-speed engine
- The Kree erase her memory and train her as "Vers" in Starforce
- Skrulls are revealed as refugees rather than invaders
- Nick Fury loses his eye to Goose the Flerken
- Fury drafts the "Avengers Initiative" and receives the emergency pager

### 3. Iron Man
**In-universe:** Early to spring 2008
- Tony Stark is captured by the Ten Rings in Afghanistan and builds the Mark I
- Yinsen dies helping him escape
- Obadiah Stane is revealed as the traitor and dies as Iron Monger
- Tony publicly declares "I am Iron Man"
- Fury introduces the Avengers Initiative

### 4. Iron Man 2
**In-universe:** Spring 2010
- The arc reactor's palladium core is poisoning Tony
- Ivan Vanko attacks as Whiplash with backing from Justin Hammer
- Rhodey takes a suit and becomes War Machine
- Natasha Romanoff is introduced undercover at Stark Industries
- Tony synthesizes a new element from Howard's Expo model
- Post-credits: Mjolnir is found in New Mexico

### 5. The Incredible Hulk
**In-universe:** Spring 2010
- Bruce Banner hides in Brazil while General Ross hunts him
- Emil Blonsky is enhanced and transforms into the Abomination
- Hulk defeats Abomination in Harlem
- Tony Stark approaches Ross about a team

### 6. Thor
**In-universe:** Spring 2010
- Odin banishes Thor to Earth and strips him of Mjolnir
- Thor meets Jane Foster, Erik Selvig and Darcy Lewis
- Loki learns he is a Frost Giant and seizes the throne
- Thor regains his worthiness and Mjolnir by defeating the Destroyer
- The Bifrost is destroyed and Loki falls into the void
- Post-credits: Selvig is shown the Tesseract under Loki's influence

Note: Iron Man 2, The Incredible Hulk and Thor take place within roughly the same week.

### 7. The Avengers
**In-universe:** Spring 2012
- Loki steals the Tesseract using a scepter holding the Mind Stone
- Coulson is killed by Loki
- The Chitauri invade New York
- The Avengers form; Tony flies a nuclear missile through the portal
- Loki and the Tesseract go to Asgard
- Post-credits: Thanos is revealed as Loki's backer

### 8. Thor: The Dark World
**In-universe:** Fall 2013
- The Convergence aligns the Nine Realms
- Malekith and the Dark Elves pursue the Aether (Reality Stone)
- Frigga is killed
- Loki fakes his death and secretly replaces Odin on the throne
- The Aether is given to the Collector

### 9. Iron Man 3
**In-universe:** December 2013 to early 2014
- Tony suffers PTSD from the Battle of New York
- Aldrich Killian weaponizes Extremis
- "The Mandarin" is revealed as Trevor Slattery, an actor
- Pepper is dosed with Extremis and kills Killian
- Tony destroys his suits and has the shrapnel removed from his chest

### 10. Captain America: The Winter Soldier
**In-universe:** Spring 2014
- Hydra is revealed to have grown inside S.H.I.E.L.D. since WWII
- The Winter Soldier is revealed as Bucky Barnes
- Sam Wilson joins as the Falcon
- Project Insight is stopped and S.H.I.E.L.D. collapses
- Fury fakes his death
- Post-credits: Strucker holds Loki's scepter and the Maximoff twins

### 11. Guardians of the Galaxy
**In-universe:** Summer 2014 (prologue 1988)
- Peter Quill is abducted from Earth by Yondu's Ravagers in 1988
- Quill steals the Orb containing the Power Stone
- Ronan the Accuser attacks Xandar
- The Guardians form; Groot sacrifices himself and returns as a sapling
- The Power Stone is entrusted to the Nova Corps

### 12. Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2
**In-universe:** Fall 2014
- Quill meets his father Ego the Living Planet
- Ego is revealed to have killed Quill's mother; the Guardians destroy him
- Yondu dies saving Quill
- Mantis joins; Nebula begins reconciling with Gamora

### 13. Avengers: Age of Ultron
**In-universe:** Spring 2015
- The Avengers recover Loki's scepter from Hydra in Sokovia
- Tony and Bruce use the Mind Stone to create Ultron
- Wanda and Pietro Maximoff join the fight
- Vision is created with the Mind Stone in his forehead
- Ultron lifts Sokovia into the sky; Pietro dies; the city is destroyed
- Hulk leaves Earth; a new Avengers roster forms
- Post-credits: Thanos takes the Infinity Gauntlet

### 14. Ant-Man
**In-universe:** Summer 2015
- Hank Pym recruits Scott Lang as the new Ant-Man
- Darren Cross becomes Yellowjacket
- Scott enters the Quantum Realm and returns
- Hope van Dyne is set up to become the Wasp

### 15. Captain America: Civil War
**In-universe:** Spring 2016
- The Lagos incident leads to the Sokovia Accords
- A bombing in Vienna kills King T'Chaka; Bucky is framed
- The airport battle; Spider-Man and Black Panther debut
- Rhodey is paralyzed
- Zemo reveals Bucky killed Tony's parents
- The Avengers split; Steve goes rogue and drops the shield

### 16. Black Widow
**In-universe:** Spring to summer 2016 (immediately after Civil War)
- Natasha is on the run and reunites with Yelena, Red Guardian and Melina
- The Red Room is revealed to still be operating under Dreykov
- Taskmaster is revealed as Dreykov's daughter Antonia
- The Red Room is destroyed and the Widows are freed

### 17. Black Panther
**In-universe:** Summer 2016 (about a week after Civil War)
- T'Challa is crowned king of Wakanda
- Killmonger wins ritual combat and seizes the throne
- T'Challa returns and defeats Killmonger
- Wakanda reveals its true nature to the world at the UN

### 18. Spider-Man: Homecoming
**In-universe:** Fall 2016
- Peter Parker is mentored by Tony Stark
- Adrian Toomes runs a salvage operation selling Chitauri tech as the Vulture
- Peter stops the Vulture without Stark's suit
- Peter declines Avengers membership
- Aunt May discovers Peter is Spider-Man

### 19. Doctor Strange
**In-universe:** Fall 2016 to 2017
- Stephen Strange's car crash ruins his hands
- He trains at Kamar-Taj under the Ancient One
- Kaecilius serves Dormammu; the Ancient One dies
- Strange uses the Eye of Agamotto (Time Stone) to trap Dormammu in a loop
- Mordo breaks from the Masters of the Mystic Arts

### 20. Thor: Ragnarok
**In-universe:** Fall 2017
- Odin dies; Hela is freed and destroys Mjolnir
- Thor is stranded on Sakaar and fights Hulk as a gladiator
- Valkyrie joins; Loki allies with Thor
- Surtur destroys Asgard; the Asgardians flee
- Loki secretly takes the Tesseract
- Post-credits: Thanos's ship intercepts the Asgardian refugees

### 21. Ant-Man and the Wasp
**In-universe:** Spring 2018
- Scott is under house arrest after Civil War
- Hope becomes the Wasp
- Ghost (Ava Starr) hunts for quantum energy
- Janet van Dyne is rescued from the Quantum Realm
- Post-credits: Scott is stranded in the Quantum Realm when the Snap happens

### 22. Avengers: Infinity War
**In-universe:** Spring 2018
- Thanos collects all six Infinity Stones:
  - Power Stone taken from Xandar
  - Space Stone taken from Loki, who is killed
  - Reality Stone taken from Knowhere
  - Soul Stone gained by sacrificing Gamora on Vormir
  - Time Stone surrendered by Strange
  - Mind Stone ripped from Vision, who is killed
- Heimdall dies
- Battle of Wakanda
- Thanos snaps and erases half of all life in the universe

### 23. Avengers: Endgame
**In-universe:** 2018 to fall 2023
- Thanos is found to have destroyed the stones; Thor kills him
- Five-year jump; Scott escapes the Quantum Realm in 2023
- Time Heist to 2012, 2013 and 2014 to retrieve the stones
- Natasha sacrifices herself on Vormir for the Soul Stone
- 2012 Loki escapes with the Tesseract (leads to the Loki series)
- Hulk snaps everyone back (the Blip)
- 2014 Thanos attacks; all heroes return for the final battle
- Tony snaps and dies
- Steve returns the stones, lives a life with Peggy and passes the shield to Sam
