# Marvel Comics Villains by Relative Strength

Sorted weakest to strongest, from street-level up to multiversal. **Base** is the villain's usual location or seat of power. **Debut** is the first comic appearance; Marvel comics run on a sliding timescale, so publication order is the closest thing to a comics timeline.
