# Attainable Powers in the Marvel Universe

**Key:** Untagged entries come from the comics. **(MCU)** marks entries shown in Marvel Studios films or Disney+ series. Netflix, AoS (Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.), Sony and other show names mark other screen versions. Born powers (mutants, Inhumans, gods and Eternals) are left out.

---

## Super-Soldier Serums and Chemical Formulas
- **Super-Soldier Serum with Vita-Rays:** peak human strength, speed, stamina, reflexes and healing plus slowed aging. Steve Rogers (MCU).
- **Prototype serum:** enhanced strength at the cost of a skull face. Red Skull (MCU).
- **Hydra serum:** super-soldier physiology for Bucky Barnes and the five Siberian Winter Soldiers (MCU).
- **Soviet and Red Room serums:** slowed aging and peak conditioning for Black Widow. MCU: Red Guardian (Alexei Shostakov).
- **Rebirth recreations:** Isaiah Bradley (MCU); William Burnside and Jack Monroe as the 1950s Captain America and Bucky, driven paranoid without Vita-Rays; Clinton McIntyre as Protocide.
- **Dr. Nagel's serum:** made for the Power Broker. Karli Morgenthau, the Flag Smashers and John Walker (MCU).
- **Power Broker augmentation:** superhuman strength from Dr. Karl Malus's process with a risk of deformity. John Walker (U.S. Agent) and Lemar Hoskins (Battlestar).
- **Super-soldier pills:** strength and aggression on demand. Frank Simpson (Nuke); Netflix: Will Simpson's IGH red pills.
- **Infinity Formula:** halts aging. Nick Fury; mixed with super-soldier serum to save Mockingbird.
- **Super-soldier blood transfusion:** Eli Bradley (Patriot) from his grandfather Isaiah.
- **Nazi prison formula:** super strength for Brian Falsworth (Destroyer, later Union Jack).
- **Taskmaster's serum:** photographic reflexes that erase personal memories (Tony Masters).
- **Sentry serum:** strength, flight, light powers and a dark side called the Void. Bob Reynolds; MCU: O.X.E. Group's Sentry Project.
- **Hyde formula:** monstrous strength and durability. Calvin Zabo (AoS).
- **Burstein's cell regeneration process:** unbreakable skin and super strength. Luke Cage (Netflix).
- **Nightshade:** Jamaican herbal compound for strength and fast healing. Bushmaster (Netflix).
- **Mutant Growth Hormone (MGH):** temporary random powers for anyone who takes it.
- **Goblin Formula:** strength, intellect, healing and insanity. Norman Osborn (No Way Home), Harry Osborn, Roderick Kingsley (Hobgoblin).
- **Kraven's jungle potions:** heightened strength, speed, senses and lifespan (Sergei Kravinoff).
- **Grandmaster's gifts:** Speed Demon's super speed, Nighthawk's night-strength formula and Doctor Spectrum's Power Prism (Squadron Sinister).
- **Owl's serum:** gliding flight (Leland Owlsley).
- **Cobra treatment:** a radioactive cobra bite plus an experimental antidote gave snake-like agility and flexibility (Klaus Voorhees).
- **Spilled experimental chemical:** stone body and petrifying touch for the Grey Gargoyle (Paul Duval).
- **Purple Man exposure:** mind-control pheromones. Comics: nerve gas spill; Netflix: virus from his parents' experiments (Kilgrave).
- **Mongoose blood transfusion:** super speed for the Whizzer (Robert Frank).
- **Android blood transfusion:** super speed for Spitfire from the original Human Torch.

## Gamma Radiation (Hulk Family)
- **Gamma Bomb blast:** Bruce Banner becomes the Hulk. MCU: a gamma experiment meant to recreate the Super-Soldier Serum.
- **18 months in a gamma lab:** Banner fuses with the Hulk into Smart Hulk (MCU).
- **Blood transfusion from Banner:** Jennifer Walters becomes She-Hulk. MCU: Bruce's blood enters her wound after a car crash.
- **Gamma ray machine:** Emil Blonsky becomes the Abomination. MCU: super-soldier serum plus Banner's blood.
- **Gamma-contaminated wound:** super-intellect and an enlarged skull for Samuel Sterns, the Leader (MCU).
- **Gamma siphoned from Banner:** strength and green hair for Doc Samson (Leonard Samson).
- **Intelligencia gamma process:** Thaddeus Ross as Red Hulk (heat rising with anger plus radiation absorption), Betty Ross as Red She-Hulk and Rick Jones as A-Bomb. MCU: Sterns' gamma pills create Ross's Red Hulk.
- **MODOK's gamma treatment:** Betty Ross as the winged Harpy.
- **Nanite gamma extraction:** Amadeus Cho absorbs Banner's radiation as the Totally Awesome Hulk.
- **Self-exposure to gamma rays:** Walter Langkowski becomes Sasquatch.
- **Gamma Corps:** military team of deliberately gamma-mutated soldiers.
- **World War Hulks gamma bomb:** the Leader's bomb creates Hulked-out heroes.
- **The Green Door:** gamma mutates return from death through it (Immortal Hulk).

## Powered Armor and Suits
- **Iron Man armors:** flight, repulsors, unibeam, strength, AI support and nanotech from the Mark 50 onward. Tony Stark (MCU).
- **Stark-built variants:** War Machine and Iron Patriot (James Rhodes), Rescue (Pepper Potts), Iron Spider (Peter Parker) and the Hulkbuster (MCU); comics add Guardsman armor and Norman Osborn's Iron Patriot.
- **Ironheart armor:** self-built suit of Riri Williams (MCU).
- **Iron Monger:** Obadiah Stane's oversized armor (MCU).
- **Whiplash harness:** arc-reactor energy whips. Ivan Vanko (MCU).
- **Doom's armor:** force fields, energy weapons and life support boosted by sorcery (Doctor Doom).
- **Soviet armors:** Crimson Dynamo plus the giant Titanium Man.
- **Beetle and MACH armors:** Abner Jenkins, Janice Lincoln.
- **Mandroid armor:** S.H.I.E.L.D. issue power suits.
- **Vulture harness:** flight plus boosted strength. Adrian Toomes; MCU version built from Chitauri tech.
- **Spider-suits:** Stark suit with web wings and AI, Iron Spider nanotech legs, Integrated suit (MCU).
- **Black Panther Habit:** vibranium weave that absorbs and releases kinetic energy; nanotech in the MCU.
- **Falcon gear:** EXO-7 wings and the Redwing drone for Sam Wilson and Joaquin Torres plus Sam's vibranium Captain America suit (MCU); comics wings built by Black Panther.
- **Ant-Man, Wasp and Yellowjacket suits:** size changing, flight, stingers and blasters (MCU).
- **Ghost suit:** stabilizes quantum phasing for Ava Starr (MCU); the comics Ghost wears intangibility tech.
- **Taskmaster suit:** combat mimicry (MCU).
- **Kang's armor:** energy blasts, force fields and time travel (MCU).
- **Quantum suits:** time travel through the Quantum Realm (MCU).
- **Blizzard suit:** ice generation. Gregor Shapanka, Donnie Gill.
- **Stilt-Man armor:** telescoping legs (Wilbur Day).

## Weapons, Gadgets and Materials
- **Captain America's shield:** vibranium (MCU); comics version is a vibranium-adamantium alloy.
- **Vibranium tech:** kinetic-absorbing metal behind Wakandan sonic weapons, Kimoyo beads and gauntlets (MCU).
- **Adamantium:** near-indestructible metal.
- **Uru:** Asgardian metal that holds enchantments.
- **Trick arrows:** Clint Barton, Kate Bishop (MCU).
- **Widow's Bite:** electroshock gauntlets (MCU).
- **Shocker gauntlets:** vibro-shock blasts. Herman Schultz (MCU).
- **Mysterio tech:** holograms, hallucinogenic gas and illusion drones. Quentin Beck (MCU with Stark's B.A.R.F.).
- **E.D.I.T.H.:** AR glasses commanding Stark's orbital drone arsenal (MCU).
- **Goblin glider and pumpkin bombs:** Norman Osborn, Hobgoblin.
- **Chitauri salvage:** Vulture's wings, Shocker's gauntlet and alien energy weapons (MCU).
- **Tesseract weapons:** Hydra disintegration guns (MCU).
- **Yaka Arrow:** whistle-guided arrow; the MCU version needs a head-fin implant (Yondu, Kraglin).
- **Star-Lord gear:** element guns, mask and jet boots (MCU).
- **TVA tech:** TemPad portals, Time Sticks, reset charges and the Time Ripper (MCU).
- **Sonic converter:** turned Klaw into living sound; MCU Klaue gets a vibranium sonic arm cannon.
- **Fistigons and X-Ray goggles:** fire gauntlets and see-through vision for Chase Stein (Runaways).
- **Street-level gadgets:** Prowler gauntlets, Boomerang's boomerangs, Trapster's paste gun, the Wizard's anti-gravity discs, the Melter's melting ray, Mister Fear's fear gas, the Unicorn's power horn and Constrictor's coils.

## Cybernetics, Implants and Mind Transfer
- **Winter Soldier arm:** cybernetic super-strength arm; the MCU upgrade is Wakandan vibranium.
- **Deathlok conversion:** cyborg soldier body. Luther Manning; AoS: Mike Peterson.
- **Bionic arm:** Misty Knight's Stark-made arm (comics) or Rand-funded arm (Netflix).
- **Tentacle harness:** four brain-linked mechanical arms. Doctor Octopus (No Way Home).
- **Thanos' cybernetic upgrades:** Nebula and Gamora (MCU).
- **High Evolutionary augmentation:** Rocket's intellect and cybernetics (MCU).
- **Adamantium bone lacing:** Bullseye's reinforced skeleton.
- **Steel skull surgery:** Hammerhead's flat battering-ram head.
- **Cyborg conversion:** Lady Deathstrike (Spiral's Body Shop), the Reavers.
- **Weapon X cybernetics:** Garrison Kane's arms.
- **Sonic throat implants:** Songbird (Screaming Mimi), from Roxxon.
- **Arc reactor:** chest power source for Tony Stark (MCU); also powers Electro in No Way Home.
- **Neural combat chip:** Antonia Dreykov copies fighting styles as Taskmaster (MCU).
- **Life Model Decoys:** android bodies that can house copied minds (AoS).
- **Mind upload:** Arnim Zola's mind in a robot body (comics) or a computer (MCU).
- **Octobot mind swap:** Doctor Octopus takes Peter's body as the Superior Spider-Man.
- **Nanotech housing:** Iron Man's Bleeding Edge armor stored in his chest (MCU).

## Pym Particles and Quantum Tech
- **Pym Particles:** shrinking, growing and density control. Hank Pym, Janet van Dyne, Hope van Dyne, Scott Lang, Cassie Lang, Bill Foster and Darren Cross (MCU); Clint Barton and Erik Josten as Goliath.
- **Pym biomodification:** Janet's wings and bio-stingers.
- **Absorbed particles:** Cassie Lang changes size without a suit as Stature.
- **Cybernetic ant helmet:** commands insects (MCU).
- **Quantum Realm exposure:** Janet's quantum healing and energy plus Ava Starr's phasing as Ghost (MCU).
- **Quantum Realm rebuild:** Kang turns Darren Cross into MODOK (MCU).

## Cosmic Rays, Radiation and Energy Accidents
- **Cosmic rays:** elasticity for Reed Richards, invisibility and force fields for Sue Storm, fire and flight for Johnny Storm, rock body and strength for Ben Grimm (MCU). The same exposure created the U-Foes plus the Red Ghost and his Super-Apes.
- **Radioactive spider bite:** wall-crawling, strength, agility and spider-sense. Peter Parker (MCU), Cindy Moon (Silk).
- **Genetically engineered spider bite:** Miles Morales.
- **Radioactive chemical spill:** heightened senses and radar sense. Matt Murdock (Netflix).
- **Chemical exposure in a car crash:** strength, durability and flight for Jessica Jones. Netflix: IGH experiments instead.
- **Lightning strike on power lines:** electrical control for Electro (Max Dillon).
- **Nuclear test site radiation:** sand body for Sandman (Flint Marko).
- **Experimental generator accident:** water body for Hydro-Man (Morris Bench).
- **Metallic alloy splash:** golden heat-generating skin for Molten Man (Mark Raxton).
- **Ionic ray treatment:** strength, durability and longevity. Wonder Man (Simon Williams), Atlas (Erik Josten), Count Nefaria.
- **Graviton particle accident:** gravity control. Franklin Hall; AoS: Gravitonium also empowers Glenn Talbot.
- **Anti-matter reactor explosion:** Blue Marvel (Adam Brashear).
- **Particle accelerator accident:** control over matter and energy for Molecule Man (Owen Reece).
- **Deliberate self-irradiation:** Radioactive Man (Chen Lu).
- **Laser implants:** Living Laser (Arthur Parks), who later becomes living light.
- **Energy device fall:** electrical body for Living Lightning (Miguel Santos).
- **Extra-dimensional energy accident:** kinetic bubble powers for Speedball (Robbie Baldwin).
- **Dimensional portal experiment:** body portals for the Spot (Jonathan Ohnn).
- **Psyche-Magnitron explosion:** Kree-hybrid strength, flight and energy blasts for Carol Danvers, later Binary through Brood experiments. MCU: Tesseract-powered engine blast plus a Kree blood transfusion.
- **Energy disruptor blast:** energy-form powers for Monica Rambeau. MCU: passing through the Westview Hex.
- **Weather machine accident:** ice powers for Donnie Gill (AoS).
- **Experimental drug:** Darkforce teleportation for Cloak and light daggers for Dagger; their TV series uses a Roxxon rig explosion.
- **Zero Matter:** absorption and intangibility. Whitney Frost, Jason Wilkes (Agent Carter).
- **Lab chemical explosion:** power copying for the Mimic (Calvin Rankin).
- **Toxic waste:** adult body and strength for Rage (Elvin Haliday).

## Biotech, Mutagens and Transformations
- **Lizard formula:** reptile DNA giving regeneration and a reptile form. Curt Connors (No Way Home).
- **Bat-based treatment:** pseudo-vampire strength, flight and healing. Michael Morbius (Sony).
- **Stillwell's mutagenic procedure:** Scorpion's strength plus a tail suit (Mac Gargan).
- **Chemical and radiation treatments:** Rhino's strength and bonded armor hide (Aleksei Sytsevich).
- **Spider serum:** Jessica Drew (Spider-Woman); Julia Carpenter's formula of spider venom and plant extracts.
- **Genetic engineering:** High Evolutionary's evolved New Men and animals.
- **A.I.M. mutation:** super-intellect and psionics for MODOK (George Tarleton).
- **Extremis:** regeneration, extreme body heat, fire breath, strength and tech interface. Tony Stark; MCU: Aldrich Killian, Eric Savin, Ellen Brandt and Pepper Potts.
- **Centipede serum:** Extremis, super-soldier serum, gamma and Chitauri tech combined. Mike Peterson (AoS).
- **GH-325:** Kree-derived healing and resurrection. Phil Coulson, Skye (AoS).
- **Weapon X healing factor:** Wade Wilson as Deadpool (Deadpool films).
- **Cat People transformation:** feline strength, agility and claws for Tigra (Greer Grant Nelson).
- **Probability-field treatment:** bad-luck powers for Black Cat from Kingpin's scientists.
- **Skrull cow meat:** shapeshifting and Skrull detection for the Skrull Kill Krew.

## Symbiotes
- **Venom symbiote:** strength, shapeshifting, webbing, camouflage and spider-sense immunity. Peter Parker's black suit, Eddie Brock (Sony), Mac Gargan, Ann Weying (She-Venom); Flash Thompson as Agent Venom through Project Rebirth 2.0.
- **Carnage:** Cletus Kasady (Sony).
- **Other symbiotes:** Toxin, Scream, Riot, Phage, Lasher, Agony and Mania.
- **Anti-Venom:** Eddie Brock after Mister Negative's touch purges the symbiote.
- **All-Black the Necrosword:** shadow constructs and god-slaying from the first symbiote. Gorr (MCU).

## Herbs, Rituals and Chi
- **Heart-Shaped Herb:** strength, speed, senses and access to the Ancestral Plane. T'Challa, Killmonger and Shuri's synthetic version (MCU).
- **Vibranium-infused herb:** water breathing, strength and long life for the Talokanil (MCU).
- **White Gorilla ritual:** M'Baku (Man-Ape) eats its flesh and bathes in its blood for its strength.
- **Iron Fist:** defeating the dragon Shou-Lao and plunging into its molten heart grants a chi-charged fist plus healing. Danny Rand, Orson Randall, Lin Lie; Netflix: Danny, Davos and Colleen Wing.
- **Immortal Weapons:** chi champions of the Seven Capital Cities of Heaven.
- **The Chaste:** chi and sensory training under Stick (Netflix Daredevil).
- **Ta Lo:** dragon-scale armor and flowing chi martial arts (MCU).
- **Shao-Lom monks:** telepathy and telekinesis through training for Moondragon (Heather Douglas).

## Mystic Arts and Witchcraft
- **Kamar-Taj training:** sling ring portals, astral projection, the Mirror Dimension, shields, whips and the Crimson Bands of Cyttorak. Doctor Strange, Wong, Mordo (MCU).
- **Dark Dimension power:** Kaecilius and the Zealots; the Ancient One's long life (MCU).
- **Witchcraft:** Agatha Harkness's power absorption (MCU), Jennifer Kale's Book of Zhered-Na, Nico Minoru's Staff of One (Runaways).
- **Sorcery plus science:** Doctor Doom blends learned magic with his armor tech.
- **Voodoo:** Doctor Voodoo (Jericho Drumm) channels his brother's spirit.
- **Shamanism:** Shaman's mystic medicine pouch (Michael Twoyoungmen).
- **Darkhold spells:** dreamwalking (MCU), vampire creation and the werewolf curse.
- **Book of Vishanti:** white magic that counters the Darkhold (MCU).
- **The Hand's rituals:** resurrection for Elektra; Netflix adds a dragon-bone substance for long life.

## Magical Artifacts and Enchanted Items
- **Mjolnir:** strength, lightning and flight for the worthy. Jane Foster, Steve Rogers, Eric Masterson, Beta Ray Bill and Red Norvell; MCU: Jane, Steve and Vision.
- **Stormbreaker:** Uru axe-hammer that channels lightning and summons the Bifrost. Beta Ray Bill; MCU: Thor.
- **Thunderstrike mace:** Asgardian mace with Thor-level power for Eric Masterson.
- **Zeus' Thunderbolt:** Olympian lightning weapon wielded by Valkyrie (MCU).
- **Ten Rings:** comics rings fire ice, mind control, electricity, fire, light, darkness, disintegration, vortex winds, matter rearrangement and impact blasts (Mandarin). MCU rings grant centuries of life, energy blasts and chain strikes (Wenwu, Shang-Chi).
- **Crimson Gem of Cyttorak:** unstoppable momentum. Cain Marko, Colossus.
- **The Serpent's hammers:** godlike power for the Worthy (Juggernaut, Hulk, Thing, Absorbing Man, Titania, Grey Gargoyle, Attuma and Sin).
- **Ebony Blade:** cursed unbreakable sword of Dane Whitman (MCU).
- **Hood's cloak and boots:** invisibility and levitation. Parker Robbins (MCU).
- **Norn Stones:** Loki's stones; the Hood used them to power up his gang.
- **Enchanted crowbar:** Karnilla's spell gave the Wrecker Asgardian strength; a lightning strike shared it with the Wrecking Crew.
- **Loki's potion:** Absorbing Man (Carl Creel) copies anything he touches.
- **Kree lightstones:** flight, intangibility and energy blasts for Moonstone.
- **Moon gem:** werewolf transformation for Man-Wolf (John Jameson).
- **Eye of Agamotto:** truth-revealing light; the MCU version holds the Time Stone.
- **Cloak of Levitation:** sentient cloak granting flight (MCU).
- **Vaulting Boots of Valtorr and the Staff of the Living Tribunal:** Mordo's relics (MCU).
- **Wand of Watoomb:** amplifies a sorcerer's power.
- **Coronet of Enchantment:** mystic tiara granting shamanic magic to Talisman (Elizabeth Twoyoungmen).
- **Serpent Crown:** mind control fueled by the demon Set.
- **Bloodstone:** immortality and strength (Ulysses Bloodstone); MCU Werewolf by Night relic.
- **Spider Society tattoo:** spider powers and an exoskeleton for Anya Corazon (Araña).
- **Destroyer armor:** animated by a user's life force; Loki in the MCU.
- **Zodiac Key:** energy blasts and teleportation for Scorpio.

## Demonic Pacts, Spirits and Curses
- **Spirit of Vengeance:** hellfire, the Penance Stare and a hellfire chain. Johnny Blaze (Mephisto's deal), Danny Ketch; Robbie Reyes (AoS, briefly passed to Coulson).
- **Demon deal:** Isaac Christians becomes the stone-bodied Gargoyle.
- **Vampire bite:** strength, shapeshifting and longevity for Hannibal King.
- **Werewolf curse:** wolf form passed down Jack Russell's family from the Darkhold (MCU Werewolf by Night).
- **Wendigo curse:** monstrous strength and endless hunger from cannibalism in the Canadian north.
- **Man-Thing:** Ted Sallis' recreated super-soldier formula plus swamp magic (MCU Werewolf by Night).
- **Voodoo zombification:** undead strength under amulet control for Simon Garth.
- **Zombie plague:** hunger-driven undead durability (Marvel Zombies, alternate universe).

## Divine Mantles and Avatars
- **Avatar of Khonshu:** resurrection, moon-linked strength and healing armor. Marc Spector, Steven Grant (MCU).
- **Avatar of Taweret:** golden armor, wings and blades for Layla El-Faouly as Scarlet Scarab (MCU).
- **Avatar of Ammit:** soul judgment for Arthur Harrow (MCU).
- **Valkyrie mantle:** Asgardian strength, flight and death-sense. Barbara Norriss, Jane Foster.
- **Captain Britain Corps:** Amulet of Right powers from Merlyn. Brian Braddock, Kelsey Leigh, Betsy Braddock.
- **Kronos' rebirth:** Arthur Douglas resurrected in a body built to kill Thanos as Drax the Destroyer.

## Cosmic Power Sources
- **Power Cosmic:** heralds Silver Surfer, Firelord, Terrax and Frankie Raye (Nova); Doctor Doom stole it. MCU: Shalla-Bal in First Steps.
- **Nova Force:** flight, strength and energy blasts. Richard Rider, Sam Alexander.
- **Quantum Bands:** energy constructs, flight and blasts. Wendell Vaughn, Avril Kincaid (Quasar).
- **Nega-Bands:** Kree wristbands granting strength, flight and blasts. Mar-Vell, Rick Jones.
- **Uni-Power:** bonds to someone in crisis as Captain Universe. Ray Coffin, Spider-Man, Tamara Devoux.
- **Phoenix Force:** cosmic fire and rebirth; hosts include the non-mutant Echo (Maya Lopez) plus the Phoenix Five.
- **Starbrand:** planetary-defense power. Ken Connell, Kevin Connor.
- **Destiny Force:** latent human potential unlocked in Rick Jones by the Supreme Intelligence.
- **Cosmic Cube:** reality warping. Red Skull, Thanos, Kobik.
- **Heart of the Universe:** omnipotence seized by Thanos (Marvel: The End).
- **Ultimate Nullifier:** Galactus' weapon that erases anything.
- **Cosmic Control Rod:** longevity and energy blasts for Annihilus.
- **Beyonder power:** Doctor Doom (Secret Wars 1984), God Emperor Doom (Secret Wars 2015).
- **Watcher's eye:** hidden-secret knowledge for the Orb; Nick Fury becomes the Unseen.

## Infinity Stones
- **Power Stone:** Ronan's hammer; the Guardians share its force (MCU).
- **Space Stone:** Tesseract teleportation and portals; fueled Carol Danvers' origin (MCU).
- **Reality Stone:** Aether infecting Jane Foster; Malekith's weapon (MCU).
- **Soul Stone:** obtained by sacrificing a loved one on Vormir (MCU).
- **Time Stone:** Eye of Agamotto time loops (MCU).
- **Mind Stone:** Loki's scepter mind control; Hydra used it to empower Wanda and Pietro Maximoff (MCU).
- **Infinity Gauntlet:** Thanos, Nebula, Adam Warlock and Gamora; MCU: Thanos, Hulk (Nano Gauntlet) and Tony Stark.

## Alien Tech and Gifts
- **Kree experimentation:** explode-and-reform body for Nitro (Robert Hunter).
- **Universal Weapon:** Kree energy hammer. Ronan (MCU).
- **Super-Skrull process:** combined Fantastic Four powers for Kl'rt. MCU: Gravik and G'iah through the Harvest's hero DNA.
- **Kymellian gift:** gravity, acceleration, density and energy powers for Power Pack.
- **Battleworld tech:** Doctor Doom gave Titania strength and Volcana a lava body.
- **Darkhawk amulet:** swaps the wearer with an alien android armor (Chris Powell).
