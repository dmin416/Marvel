# Spider-Man Movie Timelines: Raimi, Amazing and MCU

Reference only. These are the source films for inspiration. Nothing here is a fact of the D story unless a story file marks it as approved.

Years are release years for the Raimi and Webb films. MCU years follow the MCU timeline book.

Accuracy note: written from memory of the films and not fact checked. The films rarely state ages or dates, so ages are inferred. Verify before relying on any detail.

---

## Raimi Trilogy (Tobey Maguire)

### Spider-Man (2002)

- **School:** Midtown High School, a public high school in Queens. Peter is a nerdy senior and Harry is the rich kid who transferred in. Graduation comes partway through the film.
- **Events:**
  - A class field trip to a Columbia University genetics lab, where a genetically engineered super-spider bites Peter
  - Peter wakes with powers and fights Flash at school
  - He enters an underground wrestling match to win money for a car, beats Bone Saw and is cheated out of his prize. He lets the robber who stole the promoter's money escape
  - The robber carjacks and shoots Uncle Ben. Peter catches the carjacker, who falls to his death
  - Norman Osborn tests the performance enhancer on himself under military pressure and becomes the Green Goblin
  - The Goblin kills the Oscorp board at the World Unity Festival
  - The Goblin makes Spider-Man choose between Mary Jane and a falling tram on the Queensboro Bridge. New Yorkers help Spider-Man and he saves both
  - Norman dies impaled on his own glider. Spider-Man brings his body home and Harry blames Spider-Man
  - Peter turns down Mary Jane at Norman's funeral to protect her
- **Enemies:** Green Goblin (Norman Osborn), Bone Saw, the carjacker
- **Opportunities:** Norman praises Peter's science knowledge and offers him help. The Bugle pays for Spider-Man photos
- **Work:** cage wrestling for one night; freelance photographer for J. Jonah Jameson at the Daily Bugle
- **Family:** Uncle Ben (killed) and Aunt May in Forest Hills, Queens. Peter's parents are dead and never explained
- **Friends and others:** Mary Jane Watson (next-door neighbor, waitress and aspiring actress), Harry Osborn (best friend), Flash Thompson (bully and MJ's boyfriend at first), Norman Osborn (Harry's father). Peter and Harry share an apartment after graduation

### Spider-Man 2 (2004)

- **School:** Empire State University. Peter studies physics. Dr. Curt Connors is his professor and warns him his grades are failing
- **Events:**
  - Peter is broke, late with rent and missing classes
  - Peter meets Dr. Otto Octavius through Harry. Oscorp funds Otto's fusion experiment
  - The fusion demonstration fails. Otto's wife Rosalie dies and his inhibitor chip is destroyed, so the arms control him
  - Doc Ock robs a bank while Peter and May are there asking about a loan. May is taken hostage
  - Peter's powers fail under stress. He quits being Spider-Man and his life improves for a while
  - Peter tells May the truth about Ben's death
  - Mary Jane gets engaged to astronaut John Jameson
  - Peter's powers return. He stops a runaway elevated train. The passengers see his face and keep his secret
  - Ock kidnaps Mary Jane for Harry. Harry unmasks Peter
  - Otto drowns the rebuilt reactor in the East River and dies with it
  - Mary Jane leaves John at the altar and runs to Peter
  - Harry finds Norman's Goblin lair
- **Enemies:** Doctor Octopus (Otto Octavius)
- **Opportunities:** a paper on Octavius; access to Otto's lab through Harry and Oscorp
- **Work:** Joe's Pizza delivery (fired for being late); Daily Bugle photographer
- **Family:** Aunt May is struggling with money and loses her house
- **Friends and others:** Mary Jane (stage actress in "The Importance of Being Earnest"), Harry (now running Oscorp's science division and obsessed with Spider-Man), John Jameson, landlord Mr. Ditkovich and his daughter Ursula, who likes Peter. At the Bugle: Betty Brant, Robbie Robertson and Hoffman

### Spider-Man 3 (2007)

- **School:** Still at Empire State University. Gwen Stacy is Peter's lab partner in Dr. Connors' class
- **Events:**
  - A meteorite brings the black symbiote to Earth
  - Harry attacks Peter as the New Goblin, gets amnesia and forgets the feud
  - Flint Marko escapes prison and falls into a particle experiment, becoming the Sandman
  - Police reveal Marko as the man who actually shot Ben. The carjacker had been his accomplice
  - Spider-Man saves Gwen from a falling crane. At a key to the city ceremony Spider-Man kisses Gwen as a stunt, which hurts Mary Jane
  - Peter's planned proposal fails. Harry recovers his memory and forces Mary Jane to break up with Peter
  - The symbiote bonds to Peter and makes him aggressive and cocky. He scars Harry with a pumpkin bomb, humiliates Mary Jane at a jazz club and gets Eddie Brock fired for a faked photo
  - Peter rejects the symbiote in a church bell tower and it bonds to Eddie, creating Venom
  - Venom and Sandman kidnap Mary Jane. Harry joins Peter and dies saving him
  - Peter forgives Marko. Venom dies with the symbiote
- **Enemies:** Sandman (Flint Marko), New Goblin (Harry Osborn), Venom (Eddie Brock), the symbiote
- **Opportunities:** Peter and Eddie compete for a staff photographer job at the Bugle
- **Work:** Daily Bugle photographer
- **Family:** Aunt May gives Peter her engagement ring for Mary Jane
- **Friends and others:** Mary Jane (fired from a Broadway show), Harry, Gwen Stacy, Captain George Stacy, Eddie Brock (rival photographer dating Gwen), Marko's sick daughter Penny

## The Amazing Spider-Man Duology (Andrew Garfield)

### The Amazing Spider-Man (2012)

- **School:** Midtown Science High School in Queens. Peter, Gwen Stacy and Flash Thompson are students. Gwen is head intern at Oscorp
- **Events:**
  - As a child Peter is left with Ben and May after his parents' home is broken into. Richard and Mary Parker later die in a plane crash
  - Peter finds his father's briefcase with a cross-species "decay rate algorithm"
  - Peter sneaks into an Oscorp intern tour and is bitten by a genetically engineered spider in a lab
  - Peter gives Dr. Curt Connors the algorithm. Connors tests the lizard serum on himself and becomes the Lizard
  - After an argument at home, Ben goes looking for Peter and is shot by a thief Peter let go. Peter hunts the thief as a vigilante
  - Peter reveals his identity to Gwen
  - The Lizard attacks the Williamsburg Bridge, then Midtown Science High
  - The Lizard tries to spread the serum over Manhattan from Oscorp Tower. Peter cures him with Gwen's antidote
  - Captain Stacy dies helping and makes Peter promise to keep Gwen out of it. Peter breaks the promise later
- **Enemies:** the Lizard (Curt Connors), Ben's killer, the Oscorp security man Rajit Ratha
- **Opportunities:** Gwen's Oscorp internship; Richard Parker's research
- **Work:** none steady in this film
- **Family:** Uncle Ben (killed) and Aunt May. Richard and Mary Parker (dead)
- **Friends and others:** Gwen Stacy (girlfriend), Captain George Stacy, Flash Thompson (bully who softens after Ben's death), Curt Connors (Richard's former partner, missing his right arm)

### The Amazing Spider-Man 2 (2014)

- **School:** Peter and Gwen graduate from Midtown Science High. Gwen is valedictorian and is up for a scholarship at Oxford
- **Events:**
  - Peter keeps seeing Captain Stacy's ghost and breaks up with Gwen
  - Harry Osborn returns to New York. Norman dies of retroviral hyperplasia and Harry learns he has it too
  - Oscorp electrician Max Dillon falls into a tank of electric eels and becomes Electro. He attacks Times Square
  - Harry decides Spider-Man's blood can cure him. Peter refuses
  - Peter finds his father's hidden lab in an abandoned subway station and learns why Richard left
  - Oscorp pushes Harry out. Harry breaks Electro out of Ravencroft and injects spider venom, becoming the Green Goblin
  - Electro blacks out the city from a power plant. Peter and Gwen beat him
  - The Goblin learns Peter's identity and drops Gwen in a clock tower. Peter's web catches her too late and she dies
  - Peter quits for five months. Rhino attacks the city and Peter returns
- **Enemies:** Electro (Max Dillon), Green Goblin (Harry Osborn), Rhino (Aleksei Sytsevich), Donald Menken at Oscorp; Gustav Fiers recruits villains for Harry
- **Opportunities:** Gwen's Oxford scholarship and Peter's plan to follow her to England; Richard Parker's research
- **Work:** freelance photographer selling Spider-Man photos to the Daily Bugle
- **Family:** Aunt May trains to be a nurse and tells Peter what she knows about his father
- **Friends and others:** Gwen Stacy, Harry Osborn (childhood friend), Felicia Hardy (Harry's assistant)

## MCU (Tom Holland)

### Captain America: Civil War (2016)

- **School:** Midtown School of Science and Technology in Queens. Peter is 15
- **Events:** Tony Stark recruits Peter in his apartment. Peter fights Team Cap at the Leipzig/Halle Airport and gets a new Stark suit
- **Enemies:** Team Cap in the airport fight
- **Opportunities:** a "Stark internship" as cover
- **Family:** Aunt May (younger in this version). Uncle Ben is dead but not shown
- **Friends and others:** Tony Stark (mentor)

### Spider-Man: Homecoming (2016)

- **School:** Midtown School of Science and Technology. Peter is on the academic decathlon team and the school has a homecoming dance
- **Events:**
  - Peter waits for Stark to call him for an Avengers mission and does street-level work in Queens
  - Adrian Toomes loses his Battle of New York salvage contract to Damage Control and builds alien-tech weapons as the Vulture
  - Ned discovers Peter's identity
  - The decathlon trip to Washington ends with Peter saving his classmates in the Washington Monument
  - The Vulture's deal on the Staten Island Ferry splits the ferry. Stark rescues people and takes the suit away
  - Peter learns Toomes is the father of Liz, his homecoming date
  - Peter stops Toomes' theft of the Avengers' cargo plane and saves Toomes' life
  - Peter turns down Avengers membership. May sees him in the suit
- **Enemies:** Vulture (Adrian Toomes), Shocker (Jackson Brice, then Herman Schultz), Tinkerer (Phineas Mason), Mac Gargan
- **Opportunities:** the Stark internship; Avengers membership offer (declined); academic decathlon
- **Work:** none paid. Superhero work as a Stark intern
- **Family:** Aunt May
- **Friends and others:** Ned Leeds (best friend and "guy in the chair"), Michelle "MJ" Jones, Liz Allan (crush and decathlon captain), Flash Thompson (rich rival), Happy Hogan, Karen (the suit AI), Mr. Delmar at the corner deli

### Avengers: Infinity War and Endgame (2018 to 2023)

- **Events:** Peter is on a school bus when Ebony Maw attacks Greenwich Village. He stows away on the Q-ship, goes to Titan and fights Thanos with Stark, Strange and the Guardians. He turns to dust in the Snap. Five years later he returns, fights in the final battle and watches Tony die
- **Enemies:** Thanos and the Black Order
- **Friends and others:** Tony Stark (dies), Doctor Strange

### Spider-Man: Far From Home (2024)

- **School:** Midtown's summer science class trip to Europe. The class is half blipped and half older students
- **Events:**
  - Peter grieves Tony and plans to tell MJ he likes her on the trip
  - Nick Fury (really Talos) recruits Peter to fight "Elementals" with Quentin Beck
  - Peter gives Beck Tony's E.D.I.T.H. glasses. Beck is a former Stark employee using drone illusions
  - Fights in Venice, Prague, Berlin and London
  - MJ guesses Peter's identity and they start dating
  - Beck's footage on TheDailyBugle.net frames Spider-Man for murder and reveals his identity
- **Enemies:** Mysterio (Quentin Beck) and his team of former Stark employees
- **Opportunities:** E.D.I.T.H. and Stark's tech; Happy's support
- **Family:** Aunt May, dating Happy Hogan and running charity work for blip survivors
- **Friends and others:** MJ, Ned (dating Betty Brant on the trip), Flash, Happy, Nick Fury, J. Jonah Jameson (online host)

### Spider-Man: No Way Home (2024)

- **School:** Senior year at Midtown. Peter, MJ and Ned are rejected by MIT because of the controversy
- **Events:**
  - Peter's identity is public. Damage Control charges are dropped
  - Doctor Strange's spell to make the world forget goes wrong and pulls villains from other universes
  - Peter tries to cure the villains instead of sending them home to die
  - The Green Goblin kills Aunt May. Her last words are "with great power comes great responsibility"
  - Peter Parkers from the Raimi and Webb universes arrive. The three cure the villains at the Statue of Liberty
  - Strange makes everyone forget Peter Parker. Peter lives alone, studies for a GED and makes a homemade suit
- **Enemies:** Green Goblin (Norman), Doctor Octopus (Otto), Electro (Max Dillon), Sandman (Flint Marko) and the Lizard (Curt Connors), all from other universes
- **Opportunities:** MIT (lost, then made moot by the spell); a GED afterward
- **Work:** none
- **Family:** Aunt May (dies)
- **Friends and others:** MJ and Ned (who no longer remember him), Happy, Doctor Strange, the other two Peters
