# MCU Technology Timeline: Spring 2008 to Fall 2023

Runs from Tony Stark's capture in Afghanistan to *Avengers: Endgame*. Dates follow Marvel's 2023 official timeline book.

---

## Spring 2008: *Iron Man*

- **Chest arc reactor:** a palladium-core miniature reactor powering the electromagnet that keeps shrapnel out of Tony's heart
- **Mark I:** cave-built armor with flamethrowers and a forearm rocket. It flies just long enough to escape, then crashes.
- **Mark II:** silver flight prototype that ices up at high altitude
- **Mark III:** red-and-gold armor using the gold-titanium alloy from the Seraphim satellite design. It has palm repulsors, flares, shoulder guns and a forearm anti-tank missile.
- **Workshop:** J.A.R.V.I.S., holographic design tools and the robot arms Dum-E and U
- **Iron Monger:** Stane's giant suit, reverse-engineered from the Mark I wreckage. His scientists can't shrink the reactor, so he paralyzes Tony with a handheld device and takes his.
- **Stark Industries reactor:** the full-size arc reactor Pepper overloads to kill Stane

## Spring 2010: *The Incredible Hulk*, *Iron Man 2*, *Thor*

- **Super-soldier serum revived:** Ross has Blonsky injected with a serum based on the WWII program. Banner's blood then turns him into the Abomination. (*The Incredible Hulk*)
- **Hulk-fighting tech:** truck-mounted sonic cannons at Culver University, Banner's heart-rate watch that alerts him before he hits 200 bpm and Samuel Sterns' experimental antidote (*The Incredible Hulk*)
- **Whiplash:** Vanko's homemade arc reactor powers electrified whips at the Monaco Grand Prix. His later full armor has a self-destruct. (*Iron Man 2*)
- **Mark V:** the briefcase suit Happy delivers in Monaco (*Iron Man 2*)
- **War Machine:** Rhodes takes the Mark II, and the Air Force has Hammer load it with weapons (*Iron Man 2*)
- **Hammer Drones:** Vanko-built robot soldiers for the Army, Navy, Air Force and Marines, hijacked at the Stark Expo (*Iron Man 2*)
- **Foreign copies:** Senate hearing footage shows other nations' failed Iron Man attempts (*Iron Man 2*)
- **Lithium dioxide:** S.H.I.E.L.D.'s stopgap injection for Tony's palladium poisoning (*Iron Man 2*)
- **New element:** Tony builds a particle accelerator at home to make Howard's element. It powers the Mark VI's triangular reactor, and the Mark VI carries a one-shot laser. (*Iron Man 2*)
- **Asgardian tech on Earth:** the Bifrost, Mjolnir and the Destroyer automaton (*Thor*)
- **S.H.I.E.L.D.:** seizes Jane Foster's wormhole research, builds a compound around Mjolnir and starts studying the Tesseract with Erik Selvig (*Thor*)

## 2012: *The Avengers* and Aftermath

- **Phase 2:** S.H.I.E.L.D. builds Tesseract weapons at Project PEGASUS
- **Helicarrier:** S.H.I.E.L.D.'s flying aircraft carrier, lifted by four rotors and hidden by light-bending panels. It carries a drop-away cell built for the Hulk.
- **Quinjets:** S.H.I.E.L.D.'s VTOL jets
- **Loki's scepter:** mind control through the Mind Stone
- **Stark Tower:** runs on its own arc reactor as a self-sustaining clean-energy building. Selvig's Tesseract-powered generator on its roof opens the portal.
- **Mark VII:** launches from Stark Tower and locks onto homing bracelets to suit Tony up in midair
- **Chitauri:** flying chariots, Leviathan troop carriers and energy rifles
- **Other gear:** Hawkeye's explosive and system-hacking arrows, plus Coulson's prototype gun built from Destroyer tech
- **Aftermath:** Damage Control takes over the cleanup while Adrian Toomes' crew keeps salvaged Chitauri tech (*Spider-Man: Homecoming*). A stray Chitauri gun becomes S.H.I.E.L.D.'s Item 47 (*Item 47* One-Shot).

## Fall 2013: *Thor: The Dark World*

- **Dark Elves:** the Aether (Reality Stone), cloaked warships, black hole grenades and the Kursed stone that turns Algrim into Kurse
- **Asgard:** the Soul Forge medical scanner, a defensive energy shield and flying skiffs
- **Selvig's gravimetric spikes:** devices that move objects through Convergence portals. They send Malekith back to Svartalfheim.

## December 2013: *Iron Man 3* (book date)

- **Extremis:** regeneration, superhuman strength and extreme heat. Failed doses make subjects explode, which caused the "Mandarin" bombings.
- **A.I.M.:** Killian's think tank behind Extremis
- **Mark 42:** a prehensile suit whose pieces fly to Tony on command
- **House Party Protocol:** dozens of Iron Legion suits flown remotely by J.A.R.V.I.S.
- **Iron Patriot:** Rhodes' rebranded War Machine
- **Clean Slate Protocol:** Tony destroys his suits, has the shrapnel removed and throws away his chest reactor

## 2014: *The Winter Soldier*, *Guardians of the Galaxy*, *Vol. 2*

- **Project Insight:** three Helicarriers linked to spy satellites, firing at targets picked by Zola's data-mining algorithm (*The Winter Soldier*)
- **EXO-7 Falcon:** Sam Wilson's jet-powered wing pack (*The Winter Soldier*)
- **Spy gear:** Fury's armored computer-run SUV, Natasha's Widow's Bite, face-disguise masks and magnetic cuffs (*The Winter Soldier*)
- **Zola's 1970s computer:** destroyed at Camp Lehigh by a S.H.I.E.L.D. missile (*The Winter Soldier*)
- **Hydra:** Baron Strucker experiments on volunteers with Loki's scepter (*The Winter Soldier* mid-credits)
- **Off-world:**
  - Quill's ship the Milano, Yondu's whistle-guided Yaka arrow and Rocket's heavy guns (*Guardians of the Galaxy*)
  - Ronan's warship the Dark Aster, plus Nova Corps Star Blasters linking into an energy net (*Guardians of the Galaxy*)
  - The Sovereign's remote-piloted drone fleet, Nebula's cybernetic body and Yondu's prototype head fin (*Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2*)
  - Ego's seedlings that xenoform planets, including one on Earth (*Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2*)

## 2015: *Age of Ultron*, *Ant-Man*

- **Maximoff twins:** Strucker's scepter experiments give them their powers (*Age of Ultron*)
- **Iron Legion:** Tony's security drones (*Age of Ultron*)
- **Ultron:** the AI Tony and Bruce build from the scepter's gem. It spreads through the internet into robot bodies and ends up in a vibranium frame. (*Age of Ultron*)
- **Hulkbuster:** heavy armor whose parts drop from orbit via "Veronica" (*Age of Ultron*)
- **Regeneration Cradle:** Helen Cho's synthetic-tissue printer that heals wounds and builds Vision's vibranium-laced body (*Age of Ultron*)
- **Vision and F.R.I.D.A.Y.:** Vision is the Cradle body plus the Mind Stone plus J.A.R.V.I.S.'s code. F.R.I.D.A.Y. replaces J.A.R.V.I.S. (*Age of Ultron*)
- **Sokovia:** Ultron's vibranium-core engines lift the city. Fury arrives with a decommissioned Helicarrier to evacuate it. (*Age of Ultron*)
- **Pym tech:** Scott's Ant-Man suit, shrinking and growing discs and an earpiece that commands bullet, crazy, carpenter and fire ants (*Ant-Man*)
- **Yellowjacket:** Darren Cross's militarized shrinking suit with plasma stingers. His Cross Particle tests turn living subjects to goo. (*Ant-Man*)
- **Avengers Compound:** the team's new upstate New York headquarters

## 2016: *Civil War*, *Black Widow*, *Black Panther*, *Spider-Man: Homecoming*

- **B.A.R.F.:** Tony's holographic system for reliving memories (*Civil War*)
- **Black Panther habit:** T'Challa's vibranium suit (*Civil War*). Shuri's nanotech upgrade absorbs kinetic energy and fires it back (*Black Panther*).
- **Spider-Man suit:** Tony's suit for Peter (*Civil War*). It has the Karen AI, 576 web combinations, Instant Kill mode and a spider-drone, all locked behind the Training Wheels Protocol (*Spider-Man: Homecoming*).
- **Other *Civil War* tech:** Scott's Giant-Man growth, Falcon's Redwing drone, the Raft underwater prison and Tony's leg braces that let Rhodey walk again
- **Wakandan cryo:** Bucky goes back into cryosleep until a cure is found (*Civil War*)
- **Red Room:** Dreykov's airborne base, chemical mind control with a pheromone lock and Taskmaster, who copies fighting styles from footage (*Black Widow*)
- **Wakanda** (*Black Panther*):
  - Kimoyo beads, which can remote-drive a car by hologram
  - sound-dampening sneakers and Royal Talon Fighters
  - a holographic barrier hiding the capital
  - medicine that heals Everett Ross's spinal wound
- **Klaue's arm cannon:** a vibranium mining tool rebuilt as a sonic weapon (*Black Panther*)
- **Vulture's crew:** a winged flight suit, Shocker's vibro-blast gauntlet and guns built from Chitauri salvage. One of those guns splits the Staten Island Ferry. (*Spider-Man: Homecoming*)
- **Mark 47:** an Iron Man suit Tony pilots remotely. The Iron Spider suit is unveiled at the end. (*Spider-Man: Homecoming*)

## Fall 2017: *Thor: Ragnarok*

- **Sakaar:** obedience disks that shock captives, the Grandmaster's Melt Stick, his pleasure ship the Commodore and the huge Statesman that carries Asgard's refugees

## Spring 2018: *Ant-Man and the Wasp*, *Infinity War*

- **Pym lab:** a quantum tunnel to the Quantum Realm, housed in a building that shrinks to suitcase size (*Ant-Man and the Wasp*)
- **Wasp suit:** Hope's suit with wings and wrist blasters (*Ant-Man and the Wasp*)
- **Ghost's suit:** a S.H.I.E.L.D.-built containment suit that steadies Ava's phasing (*Ant-Man and the Wasp*)
- **Nidavellir:** Eitri forges Thanos's Infinity Gauntlet, then Stormbreaker for Thor (*Infinity War*)
- **Nanotech:** Tony's Mark 50, stored in his arc reactor housing, plus Peter's Iron Spider suit with mechanical legs (*Infinity War*)
- **Wakanda's defenses:** its energy dome, Bucky's vibranium arm and Shuri's attempt to remove the Mind Stone from Vision (*Infinity War*)
- **Other gear:** Bruce in the Hulkbuster and Rocket's spare cybernetic eye for Thor (*Infinity War*)
- **Ships:** Thanos's Sanctuary II, the Black Order's Q-ships and the Guardians' Benatar (*Infinity War*)
- **Fury's pager:** the 1995 device sends its signal to Carol in the final scene (*Infinity War*)

## 2018 to Fall 2023: *Avengers: Endgame*

- **Time Heist:** Tony cracks time travel through the Quantum Realm with an inverted Möbius strip model. The team uses a quantum tunnel, nanotech time-travel suits, Time-Space GPS wrist units and a scarce supply of Pym Particles.
- **Professor Hulk:** Bruce merges with the Hulk after 18 months of work in the gamma lab
- **Nano Gauntlet:** a Stark-built gauntlet for all six Stones. Hulk's snap returns the vanished, and Tony's snap erases Thanos's forces.
- **Armor:** Tony's Mark 85 and Pepper's Rescue suit
- **Nebula's cybernetics:** her implants sync with her 2014 self, which leaks the plan to Thanos
- **2014 Thanos:** Sanctuary II bombards the Avengers Compound

## TV-Only Highlights

- **Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. (2013 to 2014):**
  - GH-325, the Kree-derived drug that revived Coulson
  - the Extremis-based Centipede serum and Cybertek's Deathlok cybernetics
  - Coulson's hover-capable Corvette Lola and the Bus command plane
  - a Hydra 0-8-4 device powered by Tesseract energy
- **Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. (2016 to 2017):** Holden Radcliffe's Life Model Decoys, his sentient android AIDA, the Framework virtual world and Zephyr One's cloaking
- **Luke Cage (2016):** Judas bullets made of alien metal from a stolen Hammer Industries prototype, the only rounds that pierce Luke's skin
